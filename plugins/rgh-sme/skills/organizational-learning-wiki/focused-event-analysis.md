Focused event analysis (FEA) is a standing ritual for learning from
[complex failures](failure-taxonomy.md). After an event, everyone whose work
touched it meets to share observations from multiple perspectives — like the
blind men and the elephant, but aimed at identifying contributing factors to
improve the system rather than assign blame.

People leave FEAs not feeling blamed but empowered, with deeper appreciation
of interdependence, and remain willing to keep reporting. Combined with
blameless reporting policies, FEAs exemplify
[responding productively to voice](responding-productively-to-voice.md).

Children's Hospital's FEA practice, instituted under Julie Morath, became a
model for turning [dangerous silence](dangerous-silence.md) into system
improvement.
