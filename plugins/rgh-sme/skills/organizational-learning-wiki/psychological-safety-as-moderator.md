title: Psychological Safety as Moderator
description: >
  Psychological safety strengthens or weakens how other team attributes —
  diversity, dispersion, conflict — translate into outcomes.
sources:
  - title: The Fearless Organization
    resource: "The Fearless Organization (Edmondson), ch. 2"
---

Psychological safety often acts statistically as a **moderator** — an extra
ingredient that determines whether another variable helps or hurts.

**Geographic dispersion:** In innovation teams across 18 nations, psychological
safety helped virtual teams manage electronic communication, cultural
diversity, time zones, and shifting membership.

**Conflict:** In student project teams, conflict improved performance under
high psychological safety and harmed it otherwise — because people could
engage critical discussion without excessive personal friction. Senior
management teams can build safe climates for candid strategic disagreement.

**Diversity:** Expertise-diverse teams performed well under high psychological
safety and badly otherwise. A positive climate for diversity plus psychological
safety drove more discretionary effort, with stronger effects for minority
employees — suggesting safety may be especially important for inclusion and
belonging.

This pattern explains why hiring for diversity alone is insufficient without
[psychological safety](psychological-safety.md) as the climate that lets
diverse perspectives be heard.
