type: concept
title: Psychological Safety and Performance Standards
description: >
  Two independent leadership dimensions — safety and standards — whose
  combination defines apathy, comfort, anxiety, and learning zones.
sources:
  - title: The Fearless Organization
    resource: "The Fearless Organization (Edmondson), ch. 1"
---

[Psychological safety](psychological-safety.md) and performance standards are
separate, independently important dimensions. Leaders who conflate high
standards with fear-based management land in the anxiety zone.

**Apathy zone** (low safety, low standards): people show up but disengage,
self-protect, and withhold effort.

**Comfort zone** (high safety, low standards): collegial and open, but
under-challenged — little learning, innovation, or fulfillment.

**Anxiety zone** (high standards, low safety): the quadrant Edmondson worries
about most. Employees fear speaking up; quality and safety suffer. Especially
dangerous when work is uncertain, interdependent, or volatile.

**Learning zone** (high standards, high safety): also the high-performance
zone for complex, innovative work. People collaborate, learn from each other,
and meet demanding goals.

Psychological safety [takes off the brakes](psychological-safety-not-enough.md);
standards and purpose supply the fuel.
