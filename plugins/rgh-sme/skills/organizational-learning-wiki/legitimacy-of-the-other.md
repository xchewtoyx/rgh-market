title: Legitimacy of the Other
description: >
  Intelligent collective action requires recognizing each network member as a
  legitimate participant — Maturana's "love" as the emotion that expands intelligence.
sources:
  - title: "The Fifth Discipline: The Art and Practice of the Learning Organization"

Chilean biologist Humberto Maturana, studying cognition in living systems,
found that intelligent action arises in social systems where all network members
accept each other as **legitimate participants**.

At HP seminars Maturana discussed love as "the recognition of the other as a
legitimate other" and "the emotion that expands intelligence." This is not
sentimentality but a precondition for [networks of collaboration](networks-of-collaboration.md)
and [reflective openness](reflective-openness.md): people must feel "seen" and
legitimate before they share what they know.

Connects to [psychological safety](psychological-safety.md) and [inviting participation](inviting-participation.md)
— lowering barriers so diverse participants count. Without legitimacy, formal
open-communication procedures produce [participative openness without reflective
learning](reflective-openness.md).

Pairs with [peacekeeping circles](peacekeeping-circles.md): "we all have problems
and we learn by helping one another" — equality as legitimacy, not hierarchy.
