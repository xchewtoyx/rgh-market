title: Psychological Safety Is Not Niceness
description: >
  Psychological safety enables candor and productive disagreement — not
  polite agreement or unconditional praise.

  - title: The Fearless Organization

[Psychological safety](psychological-safety.md) is not about being nice. It
does not mean people always agree or offer unconditional praise; it enables
candor and productive disagreement.

Edmondson describes companies with a "Company Nice" problem: polite agreement
in meetings, real disagreement voiced privately afterward, and meeting decisions
quietly not implemented. Psychological safety does not imply ease or comfort —
it is willingness to engage in productive conflict.

It is also [not lowering performance standards](psychological-safety-and-performance-standards.md)
and not an "anything-goes" environment. Leaders can hold people accountable
while still making it safe to surface problems early.
