---
type: concept
title: Reinforcing Feedback
description: >
  A feedback loop where a small change builds on itself in the same direction —
  the engine of growth, decline, or escalation in organizational systems.
sources:
  - title: "The Fifth Discipline: The Art and Practice of the Learning Organization"
    resource: "The Fifth Discipline (Senge), ch. 5"
---

**Reinforcing (amplifying) feedback** is present wherever something is growing
or accelerating in decline — "whenever you are in a situation where things are
growing, you can be sure that reinforcing feedback is at work." A small change
builds on itself, amplifying in the same direction, resembling compounding interest.

Examples relevant to organizational learning:

- **Self-fulfilling prophecy / Pygmalion effect**: a manager's belief in an
  employee's potential drives differential attention, producing performance that
  confirms the original belief.
- **Vicious cycles**: rumor-driven scarcity, blame spirals, [war-on-terrorism
  escalation](systems-thinking-as-learning-language.md) — "escalation structures
  where small moves in an undesirable direction set off reinforcing spirals."
- **Virtuous cycles**: word-of-mouth product growth; exercise and wellbeing;
  the reinforcing loop spreading [shared vision](why-shared-visions-die.md)
  clarity and enthusiasm.

Folk names: "snowball effect," "bandwagon effect," "vicious circle," "momentum
is everything."

**Delayed noticing**: the French lily-pad jingle — doubling daily, filling the
pond in 30 days, but unnoticed until day 29 when half full — explains why
reinforcing dangers (including [boiled frog](boiled-frog.md) gradual threats)
are worrisome: "by the time the problem is noticed, it may be too late."

Pure accelerating growth rarely continues unchecked; reinforcing processes
eventually encounter [balancing feedback](balancing-feedback.md) limits. Building
blocks for [systems archetypes](systems-archetypes.md) used as [systems thinking as learning language](systems-thinking-as-learning-language.md).
