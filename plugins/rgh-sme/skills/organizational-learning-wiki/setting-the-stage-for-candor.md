title: Setting the Stage for Candor
description: >
  Leadership practice of framing work and purpose so people understand why
  voice, failure reporting, and learning matter.
sources:
  - title: The Fearless Organization

---

**Setting the stage** is the first category in the
[leader's toolkit](leaders-toolkit-for-psychological-safety.md). Leaders
frame the work and emphasize purpose so people understand why candor matters.

**Framing the work** alters assumptions people layer onto reality without
seeing them. Julie Morath at Children's Hospital replaced "investigation" with
"study" and "error" with "accident" or "failure" — reframing events away from
"someone screwed up" toward system learning.

Leaders also [reframe failure](failure-taxonomy.md) by type of work: celebrate
"good catches" in high-volume repetitive work; court dramatic failures in
innovation; balance vigilance and learning in complex operations like
hospitals.

**Clarifying the need for voice** means calling attention to uncertainty
(stay curious for early signals), interdependence (frequent coordination
conversations), and stakes (human life versus bruised ego) — putting
[interpersonal risk](interpersonal-risk-taking-at-work.md) in perspective.

**Emphasizing purpose** reconnects tired, distracted people to why the work
matters — even when the purpose seems obvious.

**Reframing the boss** shifts from "has all answers, gives orders" to "sets
direction, invites input, creates conditions for learning" — especially
critical when goals require innovation.
