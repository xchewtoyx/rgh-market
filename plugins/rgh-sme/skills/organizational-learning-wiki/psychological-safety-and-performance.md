type: concept
title: Psychological Safety and Performance
description: >
  In uncertain, interdependent work, psychological safety enables the voice,
  coordination, and innovation that performance depends on.
sources:
  - title: The Fearless Organization
    resource: "The Fearless Organization (Edmondson), ch. 2"
---

As routine modular work declines, more tasks require judgment, coping with
uncertainty, new ideas, and coordination — making voice mission-critical to
performance.

Studies link [psychological safety](psychological-safety.md) to higher team
performance ratings, process-innovation payoff (German firms' ROA only rose
when innovation was paired with psychological safety), and R&D team output
where withholding ideas is especially costly.

Google's **Project Aristotle** tested team composition variables across 180
teams and found no pattern. Psychological safety emerged as the critical
factor — "the underpinning" of clear goals, dependable colleagues,
meaningful work, and belief the work matters.

Safety is not the fuel for performance — see
[psychological safety is not enough](psychological-safety-not-enough.md) —
but without it, talent and standards cannot fully convert into results.
