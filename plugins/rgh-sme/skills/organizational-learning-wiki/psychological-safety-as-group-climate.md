type: concept
title: Psychological Safety as Group Climate
description: >
  Psychological safety is a shared property of a team or unit, shaped by local
  leadership — not an individual trait or dyadic relationship.
sources:
  - title: The Fearless Organization
    resource: "The Fearless Organization (Edmondson), ch. 1"
---

Psychological safety describes [shared perceptions among people working
together](psychological-safety.md) — a feature of the workplace leaders can
help create, not a personality difference. Research shows it is not correlated
with introversion or extroversion; climate affects people of different
personality types similarly.

Even within one company or hospital, different floors, branches, or teams can
have very different levels. Edmondson's dissertation found psychological safety
varies at the group level and predicts learning behavior and objective
performance — including, in some studies, lower mortality.

The key mechanism claim: local leaders shape the climate. Some create
conditions for openness; others do not — across hospital floors, factory
teams, bank branches, and restaurant chains alike.
