---
type: concept
title: Suspending Assumptions
description: >
  A dialogue practice of holding assumptions visible and open to questioning
  rather than defending or suppressing them.
sources:
  - title: "The Fifth Discipline: The Art and Practice of the Learning Organization"
    resource: "The Fifth Discipline (Senge), ch. 11"
---

In [dialogue](dialogue-versus-discussion.md), all participants suspend their
assumptions — hold them "as if suspended before us," accessible to questioning,
not defended, suppressed, or eliminated.

Senge gives an extended example: a tech company's management team surfaces
mutual, previously unspoken assumptions between marketing and R&D ("keeper of
the flame" / "unapproachable"). Once shared *as* assumptions rather than facts,
candid strategic dialogue opens.

Suspending assumptions is akin to seeing leaps of abstraction and inquiring into
reasoning behind the abstraction — but done collectively rather than
individually. It connects to surfacing and testing [mental models](mental-models-as-organizational-routine.md)
as organizational routine.

One of three conditions David Bohm sets for dialogue.
