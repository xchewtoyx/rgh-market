  A performance-oriented culture where messengers are trained, failure triggers
  inquiry, and information flows transparently — prerequisites for learning at scale.

  - title: "Accelerate: The Science of Lean Software and DevOps"

Ron Westrum's typology distinguishes three organizational cultures by how they
handle **information flow** — which determines safety, resilience, and performance:

| Dimension | Pathological | Bureaucratic | Generative |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Focus | Power, threat, fear | Rules, turf, process | Organizational mission |
| Messengers | Shot (punished) | Neglected | **Trained** (welcomed) |
| Failure | Scapegoating | Procedural justice | **Inquiry** (blameless learning) |
| Cross-silo work | Discouraged | Tolerated under control | **Encouraged** |
| Novelty | Crushed | Treated as risk | Implemented and evaluated |

**Generative culture** prerequisites: mutual trust, transparent information flow,
and capacity to reverse decisions when new evidence emerges. Google's Project
Aristotle found team dynamics and [psychological safety](psychological-safety.md)
matter far more than individual talent — mirroring generative attributes.

Edgar Schein's three levels parallel this: basic assumptions (deepest), values,
and artifacts (visible processes). This bundle owns generative culture as a
**general knowledge-work learning construct** — the social conditions for candid
reporting and inquiry — not high-risk sociotechnical safety-science theory
(resilience-engineering's domain).

Human error in complex systems is the **start** of inquiry, not its end —
investigating system design and information flow that permitted error to escalate.
Connects to [failure taxonomy](failure-taxonomy.md) and [responding productively to voice](responding-productively-to-voice.md).

John Shook (NUMMI): "The way to change culture is not to first change how people
think, but instead to start by changing how people behave." Behavioral practices
can transform culture toward generative states over time — complementing [deep learning cycle](deep-learning-cycle.md)
interventions in beliefs and practices.
