A culture of silence does not only inhibit speaking up. It also fails to
listen thoughtfully to those who do — especially when the news is unpleasant.

Edmondson names this a **Cassandra culture** after the figure cursed to speak
true prophecy but never be believed. Before the Challenger launch, an engineer
raised cold-temperature O-ring concerns; the group could have resolved the
ambiguity with simple analysis had they listened intensely.

At Fukushima, multiple warnings about tsunami risk were dismissed over years —
including in-house studies acknowledging possible 50-foot waves and historical
evidence of larger tsunamis than design assumptions allowed.

For [voice](asymmetry-of-voice-and-silence.md) to be effective requires a
culture of listening. [Psychological safety](psychological-safety.md) spans
both speaking and being heard.
