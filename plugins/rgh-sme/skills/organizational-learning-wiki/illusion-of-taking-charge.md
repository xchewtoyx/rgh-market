---
type: concept
title: Illusion of Taking Charge
description: >
  A learning disability where aggressive "proactiveness" is reactiveness against
  an external enemy — not true proactiveness from seeing one's own contribution.
sources:
  - title: "The Fifth Discipline: The Art and Practice of the Learning Organization"
    resource: "The Fifth Discipline (Senge), ch. 2"
---

"Vogue" proactive management can be an antidote to passive waiting — but often
it is reactiveness in disguise. Becoming more aggressive against an
[enemy out there](the-enemy-is-out-there.md) is still reacting, regardless of
label.

An insurance company VP planned to fight litigation by taking more cases to
trial. Systemic modeling showed the plan would raise total costs — he tore up
his speech.

True proactiveness comes from seeing how we contribute to our own problems —
a product of thinking, not emotional state. One of seven
[learning disabilities](seven-learning-disabilities.md).
