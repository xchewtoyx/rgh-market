title: Team Collective Open-Mindedness
description: >
  How a group thinks collectively is an emergent property of communication
  patterns — not simply the sum of members' individual open-mindedness.
sources:
  - title: "Superforecasting: The Art and Science of Prediction"

---

Tetlock's Good Judgment Project found that **team-level active open-mindedness**
correlated with team forecast accuracy — but team AOM was **not** simply the sum
of members' individual AOM scores. "How the group thinks collectively is an
emergent property of the group itself, a property of communication patterns
among group members, not just the thought processes inside each member."

A group of open-minded people who don't engage each other underperforms the sum
of their parts; a group of opinionated people who genuinely engage each other
outperforms it. Superteams avoided both groupthink and internet-flame-war
extremes, fostering a [psychologically safe](psychological-safety.md) culture
Tetlock explicitly parallels to Edmondson's surgical teams — where a nurse does
not hesitate to correct a surgeon.

Linguistic marker of shared purpose: superteams said "our" more than "my."
Elaine Rich initially stayed quiet, intimidated by credentialed teammates; over
time she felt she had to "shoulder my part of the burden, rather than being a
freeloader." Marty Rosenthal described early "dancing around" — disagreeing
indirectly out of fear of offense — until members explicitly invited pushback
and thanked each other for constructive criticism.

The Good Judgment Project's team primer warned against both failure modes:
[groupthink](groupthink.md) (cooperative but deferential — consensus mistaken
for correctness) and dysfunction (rancor — the opposite extreme). Teams should
be cooperative but not deferential; "pointed questions are as essential to a
team as vitamins are to a human body." [Precision questioning](precision-questioning.md)
was taught as the technique for constructive confrontation.

Connects to [team learning](team-learning.md), [inviting participation](inviting-participation.md),
and [dialogue versus discussion](dialogue-versus-discussion.md) — collective
intelligence depends on how members engage, not only who they are.
