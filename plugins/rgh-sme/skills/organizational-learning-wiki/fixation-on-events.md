---
type: concept
title: Fixation on Events
description: >
  A learning disability where short-term events dominate attention, obscuring
  longer patterns needed for generative learning.
sources:
  - title: "The Fifth Discipline: The Art and Practice of the Learning Organization"
    resource: "The Fifth Discipline (Senge), ch. 2"
---

People are conditioned to see life as a series of events, each with one obvious
cause. Organizational conversation is dominated by last month's sales, budget
cuts, quarterly earnings — reinforced by media where old news is no longer news.

Event-based explanations may be true but obscure longer-term patterns. Today's
primary threats often come from slow, gradual processes: environmental decay,
erosion of education systems, decline in product quality relative to competitors.

"Generative learning cannot be sustained in an organization if people's
thinking is dominated by short-term events." Event-focus caps learning at
optimal reaction — "we cannot learn to create." Part of the seven
[learning disabilities](seven-learning-disabilities.md); pairs with the
[boiled frog](boiled-frog.md) pattern.
