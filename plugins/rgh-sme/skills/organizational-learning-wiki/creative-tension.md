**Creative tension** is holding a vision while looking deeply and honestly at
current reality. Senge: "I have never seen an effective leader who did not
recognize this principle."

Martin Luther King Jr., from Birmingham jail: it is necessary to create tension
in the mind so individuals rise from bondage of myths and half-truths — to
"dramatize the present situation." The juxtaposition of dream and present reality
is the force for change.

[Leaders as stewards](leader-as-steward.md) use creative tension rather than
false certainty. [Leaders as teachers](leader-as-teacher.md) help people see
reality in ways that reinforce confidence in shaping the future rather than
accepting limitation.

Connects to [psychological safety and performance standards](psychological-safety-and-performance-standards.md)
— high standards (vision) paired with safety to surface current reality
honestly.
