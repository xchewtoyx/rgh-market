---
type: concept
title: Ecology of Leadership
description: >
  Three interdependent leadership types — local line, network, and executive —
  that together sustain organizational learning.
sources:
  - title: "The Fifth Discipline: The Art and Practice of the Learning Organization"
    resource: "The Fifth Discipline (Senge), ch. 15"
---

Senge critiques narrowing "leader" to positional authority at the top. Real
change depends on networks of diverse roles at many levels — an **ecology of
leadership** with three interdependent types:

**Local line leaders** integrate innovative practices into daily work — testing
systems thinking, deepening conversations, building shared visions connected
to people's reality. Without them, ideas from the top don't translate into
action.

**Network leaders** are helpers, seed carriers, connectors — spreading ideas
between working groups, connecting innovative line leaders, building networks
that diffuse knowledge.

**Executive leaders** shape the environment for innovation; develop guiding ideas
about purpose, values, and vision; address structural impediments like poorly
designed measurement systems; and serve as role models whose actions speak
loudest.

Each type needs the others: line leaders need executives to remove systemic
barriers and networks to prevent isolation; executives need line leaders to
convert strategy into capability.

Across positions, leaders in learning organizations center on three roles:
[designer](leader-as-designer.md), [teacher](leader-as-teacher.md), and
[steward](leader-as-steward.md).

Edmondson frames leadership as the vital force enabling people to overcome
inherent barriers to voice and engagement — exercised at **all levels**, not
only the top. Leadership is "about harnessing others' efforts to achieve
something no one can achieve alone" and helping people go as far as they can
with their talents. Substituting candor for silence and engagement for fear are
essential leadership responsibilities today — including for people without
formal authority ([psychological safety without formal authority](psychological-safety-without-formal-authority.md)).
