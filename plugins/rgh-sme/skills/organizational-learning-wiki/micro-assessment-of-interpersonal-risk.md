title: Micro-Assessment of Interpersonal Risk
description: >
  A largely unconscious moment-by-moment calculation weighing interpersonal
  risk against the value of speaking up.
sources:
  - title: The Fearless Organization
    resource: "The Fearless Organization (Edmondson), ch. 1"
---

Before speaking up, people often run a quick, largely unconscious risk
calculation: will the immediate interpersonal cost outweigh the delayed
benefit of raising an idea, question, or concern?

Edmondson's opening case — a nurse practitioner who notices a missing
prophylactic order but stays silent after hearing a colleague publicly
berated — illustrates how a single prior incident can tip the scale toward
silence even when patient welfare is at stake.

These [micro-assessments](interpersonal-risk-taking-at-work.md) rarely
announce themselves. The cost of not speaking is invisible except to the person
who held back, which is why managers routinely underestimate how much
interpersonal fear suppresses the exchange of ideas.
