title: Psychological Safety
description: >
  A group-level belief that the work environment is safe for interpersonal
  risk-taking — speaking up with ideas, questions, or concerns.

  - title: The Fearless Organization

Psychological safety is the shared belief that the work environment is safe
for [interpersonal risk-taking](interpersonal-risk-taking-at-work.md). People
experience it as feeling able — even obligated — to be candid: raising
concerns in meetings, giving feedback, admitting a project is behind
schedule, or offering a dissenting viewpoint.

It is a property of a [group climate](psychological-safety-as-group-climate.md),
not a personality trait. Different units within the same company or hospital
can have sharply different levels. Local leaders shape it; it is not random
group chemistry.

Psychological safety [removes brakes on learning](psychological-safety-not-enough.md)
but does not by itself set direction or standards. Leaders must pair it with
[high performance standards](psychological-safety-and-performance-standards.md)
to reach the learning zone where uncertain, interdependent work gets done.
