type: concept
title: Shifting the Burden Archetype
description: >
  Symptomatic fixes relieve pressure quickly while fundamental solutions
  atrophy — addiction to palliatives masks worsening underlying problems.
sources:
  - title: "The Fifth Discipline: The Art and Practice of the Learning Organization"
    resource: "The Fifth Discipline (Senge), ch. 6, ch. 7"
---

In **shifting the burden**, an underlying problem generates symptoms. The
underlying problem is hard to confront, so people apply well-intentioned
symptomatic fixes that ameliorate symptoms but leave the core problem
worsening while capacity for fundamental solutions atrophies.

**Management principle:** beware the symptomatic solution — short-term benefit
at best; the problem resurfaces, pressure for more symptomatic response
increases, and fundamental capability erodes.

Two balancing loops correct the same symptom: a fast upper loop (symptomatic)
and a slower lower loop (fundamental). Side effects of the symptomatic fix can
create a reinforcing loop that further impedes the fundamental response —
the generic dynamic of addiction at individual, organizational, and societal
scale.

**Eroding goals** is a recurring special case: when goals and current reality
gap, pressure exists to improve the situation or lower the goal. Unemployment
targets, inflation tolerance, and delivery-time standards can drift silently
downward — "if all else fails, lower your goals."

**Leverage** requires simultaneously strengthening the fundamental response
and weakening the symptomatic one — often needing long-term orientation and
shared vision so short-term palliatives do not dominate. Symptomatic fixes
sometimes are necessary but must be acknowledged as such, paired with
rebuilding fundamental capacity.

Diagnostic clues: gradual long-term worsening despite periodic relief;
declining overall system health; growing helplessness and dependency.

Central to [growth and underinvestment](growth-and-underinvestment-archetype.md)
when disgruntled customers become the symptomatic "fix" for delivery delays.
