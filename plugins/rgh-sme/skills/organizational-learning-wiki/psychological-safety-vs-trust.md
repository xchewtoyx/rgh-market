---
type: concept
title: Psychological Safety Versus Trust
description: >
  Psychological safety is group-level and immediate; trust is dyadic and
  forward-looking — related but distinct constructs.
sources:
  - title: The Fearless Organization
    resource: "The Fearless Organization (Edmondson), ch. 1"
---

[Psychological safety](psychological-safety.md) and trust overlap in everyday
speech but measure different things.

Trust is dyadic — between two people or between a person and an organization
— and can vary by target. It concerns expectations about future behavior:
will this person or institution act reliably over time?

Psychological safety is [group-level climate](psychological-safety-as-group-climate.md)
about an immediate interpersonal experience: if I ask for help or admit a
mistake right now, will others give me the benefit of the doubt?

A useful formulation: trust is about giving others the benefit of the doubt;
psychological safety is about whether others will give *you* the benefit of
the doubt when you take an interpersonal risk.
