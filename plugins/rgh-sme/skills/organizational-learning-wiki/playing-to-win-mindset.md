---
type: concept
title: Playing to Win Mindset
description: >
  A orientation toward upside, opportunity, and necessary risk-taking — contrasted
  with playing not to lose, which reliably forecloses growth and innovation.
sources:
  - title: The Fearless Organization
    resource: "The Fearless Organization (Edmondson), ch. 8"
---

Edmondson distinguishes **playing to win** from **playing not to lose** (Wilson
& Wilson's *Play to Win!*): the latter plays it safe, focused unconsciously on
protecting against downside; the former focuses on upside, opportunity, and
necessary risk-taking. Playing not to lose reliably succeeds at not losing but
forecloses growth, innovation, and deeper fulfillment; playing to win risks
public failure but enables meaningful contribution.

The same dichotomy appears across fields: Kahneman and Tversky's loss aversion,
Higgins's promotion versus prevention focus, Dweck's growth versus fixed mindset.
Sustainable individual, team, and organizational performance comes from seeking
to gain, not fear of loss.

Suggested practice: "the best way to experience [psychological safety](psychological-safety.md)
is to act as if you have it already" — doing so tends to create a safer, more
energizing environment for others too. Pairs with [uncoupling fear and failure](uncoupling-fear-and-failure.md)
and [safe-to-fail innovation culture](safe-to-fail-innovation-culture.md).
