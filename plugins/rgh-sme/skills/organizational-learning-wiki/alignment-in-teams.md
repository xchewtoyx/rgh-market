title: Alignment in Teams
description: >
  When a group's energies harmonize toward shared results — the precondition
  before empowering individuals empowers the whole team.
sources:
  - title: "The Fifth Discipline: The Art and Practice of the Learning Organization"
    resource: "The Fifth Discipline (Senge), ch. 11"
---

**Alignment** is when a group functions as a whole. In an unaligned team,
individuals' personal power points in different directions and energy is wasted
even when people work extraordinarily hard. In an aligned team, energies
harmonize — like coherent laser light versus scattered bulb light.

Individuals do not sacrifice personal interest; the shared vision becomes an
extension of personal visions. Jazz musicians "in the groove" illustrate the
same phenomenon.

Crucially, **alignment is the necessary precondition before empowering the
individual empowers the whole team** — empowering individuals under low
alignment worsens chaos.

[Team learning](team-learning.md) develops alignment alongside capacity to
create desired results.
