---
type: concept
title: I Am My Position
description: >
  A learning disability where people limit their sense of responsibility to
  job boundaries and confuse role with identity.
sources:
  - title: "The Fifth Discipline: The Art and Practice of the Learning Organization"
    resource: "The Fifth Discipline (Senge), ch. 2"
---

People are trained into loyalty to their jobs to the point of confusing job
with identity. Displaced steelworkers described acute identity crises: "How
could I do anything else? I am a lathe operator."

Most people, asked what they do, describe daily tasks rather than the purpose
of the larger enterprise — and see themselves within a system they have little
influence over.

Detroit auto engineers used three different bolts for three engine components
where a Japanese competitor used one — because three separate groups each
considered only its own part successful. Focusing only on one's position
removes responsibility for results produced by the interaction of all positions.

When results disappoint, "all you can do is assume that 'someone screwed up.'"
This disability is one of seven [learning disabilities](seven-learning-disabilities.md).
