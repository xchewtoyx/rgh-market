Growth today is driven by ideas and ingenuity rather than standardization.
Organizations must keep finding new ways to create value — starting with putting
existing talent to its best use. Hiring smart people is insufficient when they
cannot contribute what they know at the critical moment, often from reluctance
to look bad or offend the boss.

**The fearless organization** is one in which interpersonal fear is minimized
so team and organizational performance can be maximized in a knowledge-intensive
world. It is not devoid of anxiety about the future — people may still fail,
receive negative feedback, or face industry-driven job loss.

What changes: people aren't hindered by interpersonal fear. They fear holding
back full participation *more* than sharing a potentially sensitive or wrong
idea. [Psychological safety](psychological-safety.md) is the enabling climate.

Work is increasingly team-based: employees spend 50% more time collaborating
than twenty years ago. **Teaming** — communicating and coordinating across
boundaries of expertise, status, and distance in shifting configurations — happens
best in psychologically safe workplaces.

When safety is reasonably high: mistakes get reported quickly, cross-group
coordination improves, and game-changing ideas surface.
