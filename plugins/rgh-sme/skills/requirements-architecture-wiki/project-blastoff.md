title: Project Blastoff
description: >
  Project Blastoff is the inception phase that fixes a project's purpose,
  boundary, stakeholders, and constraints before significant resources
  are committed to it.
sources:
  - title: Mastering the Requirements Process
    resource: "Mastering the Requirements Process: Getting Requirements Right (Suzanne Robertson, James Robertson), ch. 3"
---

Project Blastoff determines whether a project is viable, aligned with a
real business goal, and clear enough in scope before significant resources
are committed. Its core deliverables are: a measurable **project goal**
statement (e.g. "reduce road de-icing operating costs by 20% while
maintaining zero ice-related accidents") that every later requirement can
be checked against; a [context diagram](context-diagram.md) fixing the
boundary of the business area under study and the adjacent systems it
exchanges data with; a stakeholder analysis identifying client, customer,
hands-on users, subject-matter experts, and any negative stakeholders (see
[stakeholder roles in requirements](stakeholder-roles-in-requirements.md));
[mandated constraints](mandated-constraint.md) fixed ahead of analysis
(budget, target platform, hard deadlines, legacy integration); a shared
data dictionary establishing terminology so business and technical
audiences mean the same thing by the same word; and a first-pass estimate
of risk and cost, used to halt or rescope the project immediately if
projected cost already exceeds projected benefit.

Everything discovered later in the [Volere
process](volere-requirements-process.md) is checked against the project
goal set here — this is what keeps
[essence-of-the-work](essence-of-the-business-work.md) discovery from
drifting into unrelated feature requests.
