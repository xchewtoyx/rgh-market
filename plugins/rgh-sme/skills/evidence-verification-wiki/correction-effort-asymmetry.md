# Correction Effort Asymmetry

**Brandolini's principle** (the "bullshit asymmetry principle"): the amount of energy needed to refute a false or misleading claim is an order of magnitude larger than the energy needed to produce it in the first place. A single fabricated data point or misleading framing can be stated in a sentence; showing it's wrong typically requires tracing sources, reproducing analysis, and building a counter-argument that can withstand scrutiny.

## Correction Rarely Fully Overtakes the Original
Even a well-sourced, widely-publicized correction does not reliably reach everyone who was exposed to the original false claim. A documented real-world case: a false rumor spread to tens of thousands of shares despite being debunked and despite thousands of corrective posts responding to it directly — the correction ran in parallel with continued spread rather than stopping it. Broader research tracing debunked claims found false rumors travel further than true ones even after a correction is published, and that posts are more likely to eventually be removed than corrected in a way that reaches the original audience, but rarely fast enough to prevent most of the spread.

## Implications for Verification Priorities
- This asymmetry is an argument for catching a false or misleading claim **before** it's published or widely shared, not just for correcting it well afterward — prevention has much better odds of reach than correction.
- It's also an argument for triaging verification effort toward the claims most likely to spread widely if wrong (see [claim verification triage](claim-verification-triage.md)) — high-risk, high-reach claims deserve disproportionate upfront scrutiny precisely because a correction issued later will not undo most of the damage.
- When a refutation is published, treat its own quality as more, not less, important given this asymmetry — see [responsible refutation practice](responsible-refutation-practice.md) for the added rigor a public correction demands.

A [firehose of falsehood](firehose-of-falsehood.md) is the extreme case of this asymmetry deployed deliberately: high-volume, self-contradicting messaging designed to make correction-by-refutation infeasible at scale.
