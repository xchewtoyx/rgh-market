# Verification Models

Verification workflows generally fall into one of three structural models, which define who holds the responsibility for verifying claims and at what stage of the lifecycle verification occurs.

## 1. The Magazine Model (Independent Review)
In the Magazine Model, verification is handled by an **independent reviewer (fact-checker)** who is not the author of the document. 
- **Workflow**: The reviewer receives a near-final draft of the document along with the author's primary sources and notes. The reviewer performs a line-by-line extraction of all [checkable claims](defining-checkable-claims.md) and independently verifies each one against the sources or by contacting primary experts.
- **Key Benefit**: Provides the highest level of assurance because it eliminates the author's blind spots and [knowledge bias](knowledge-bias-in-verification.md).
- **Use Case**: Long-form, complex, or legally and technically sensitive documents where the cost of error is high.

## 2. The Newspaper Model (Author-Led Verification)
In the Newspaper Model, the primary responsibility for verification rests on the **author** of the document.
- **Workflow**: The author must perform their own verification (e.g., using [author self-verification](author-self-verification.md) techniques). Editors and peer reviewers act as a backup, probing high-stakes or doubtful assertions ("How do you know this?") rather than checking every line.
- **Key Benefit**: Highly efficient and fast; requires fewer resources.
- **Use Case**: Breaking news, short updates, or standard technical documentation with tight release cycles.

## 3. The Hybrid Model
The Hybrid Model dynamically switches between the independent and author-led approaches depending on the risk profile of each document.
- **Workflow**: A triaging authority (e.g., managing editor or lead architect) reviews incoming drafts. High-risk, high-impact, or contentious pieces are routed to the Magazine Model for independent verification, while standard or low-risk documents are routed to the Newspaper Model.
- **Key Benefit**: Optimizes resource allocation while maintaining safety gates for critical items.
