# Firehose of Falsehood

The **firehose of falsehood** is a propaganda strategy whose goal is not to persuade an audience of any single specific claim, but to exhaust critical thinking and drown out genuine signal through sheer volume of messaging — including messages that deliberately contradict each other across channels. A documented case: a state actor's social media campaign reached roughly half of a target country's adult population with over 100,000 messages engineered to amplify *both* extremes of several divisive issues simultaneously, deliberately deepening polarization rather than pushing a single coherent narrative. The strategic aim, in the words of one commentator describing this approach, is "to exhaust your critical thinking, to annihilate truth" rather than to sell one lie.

## Why This Changes the Appropriate Response
Ordinary fact-checking assumes a claim worth checking: identify it, trace it, verify or refute it. A firehose defeats that model by volume — [correction effort asymmetry](correction-effort-asymmetry.md) already establishes that refuting one false claim costs far more than producing it; a firehose multiplies the number of claims requiring that costly refutation faster than any verification process can keep pace, and its internal self-contradiction means no single refutation can even fully characterize "the claim" being made. Treating a firehose as a set of individually fact-checkable claims to work through one at a time is a losing strategy; the more useful response is to recognize the *pattern* (high volume, rapid succession, internal contradiction, amplification of extremes rather than a coherent position) and flag the source or campaign itself as compromised, rather than exhausting review effort chasing each individual message.

## Verification Action
- When a topic is flooded with a high volume of claims arriving faster than any could plausibly be independently verified, treat the volume and pattern itself as diagnostic — a firehose is evidence about the source, not a queue of legitimate claims each deserving equal individual scrutiny.
- Watch specifically for messaging that contradicts itself across audiences or channels; internal contradiction is a stronger signal of firehose tactics than any single message's plausibility.
- Distinguish this from [selection bias](selection-bias.md) in what reaches a reviewer's attention — a firehose is a deliberate flooding tactic, not an incidental sampling artifact.
