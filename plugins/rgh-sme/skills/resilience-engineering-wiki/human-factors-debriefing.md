description: >
  Klein's four-stage protocol for eliciting what participants actually
  experienced — unassisted story first, then read-back, joint juncture
  identification, and probing — because memory is malleable and leading cues
  corrupt it.
sources:
  - title: The Field Guide to Understanding 'Human Error'
    resource: "The Field Guide to Understanding 'Human Error' (Dekker), ch. 3"

Human memory is an interconnected, malleable network, not a videotape:
post-event cues (replaying recordings, leading questions, other accounts)
easily distort recall. Debriefing therefore follows a strict order (Gary
Klein's framework):

1. **Unassisted participant story** — the operator tells the narrative
   uninterrupted, before any external replays or cues can overwrite memory.
2. **Investigator read-back** — the investigator retells the story to verify
   they have grasped the operator's perspective, not their own version.
3. **Joint critical-juncture identification** — investigator and participant
   collaboratively locate the key decision points.
4. **Progressive probing at each juncture** — rebuild the internal world
   state along specific dimensions: *cues* (what was seen, expected, where
   attention was), *interpretation* (how the situation was described at that
   moment), *knowledge/experience* (what prior events it recalled, what rule
   seemed applicable), *goals and constraints* ([competing goals, time
   pressure](goal-conflicts-and-production-pressure.md)), and
   *actions/outcomes* (what was tried and whether results matched
   expectations).

This is the evidence-gathering half of the [local rationality
principle](local-rationality-principle.md): the probes reconstruct the
tunnel from inside, juncture by juncture, instead of asking the participant
to explain themselves against the outcome.

Recordings and parameter logs complement debriefing but give only a partial
"letterbox" view of the event. Keep **factual data** and **analytic
inferences** distinct: derived conclusions are legitimate evidence only when
the analytic structure and logic behind them are explicitly documented —
part of [leaving an analytic
trace](operationalizing-psychological-labels.md). In event-driven domains,
organise everything on a timeline whose axis is *time itself* — the process
pace dictated what the humans could do — at high enough resolution to
preserve pauses, overlaps, and simultaneity rather than a flattened list of
utterances.

Everything above presupposes participants can speak candidly — the
precondition a [just culture](just-culture.md) exists to protect.
