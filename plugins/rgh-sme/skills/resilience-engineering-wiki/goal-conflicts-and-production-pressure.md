Systems exist to provide goods and services and to generate profit — not to be
safe. Safety is one goal among several, and the goals conflict: faster versus
thorough, cheaper versus redundant, on-time versus double-checked. NASA's
1990s "Faster, Better, Cheaper" programme pursued contradictory goals
simultaneously, eroded launch verification, and lost the Mars Climate Orbiter
and Mars Polar Lander.

The crucial organisational move is that these conflicts are rarely resolved at
the blunt end. Management issues irreconcilable directives ("be safe" and
"hit the schedule") and the contradiction flows downhill, so operators at the
[sharp end](sharp-end-and-blunt-end.md) must negotiate punctuality versus
thoroughness locally, case by case, under time pressure. What later gets
labelled a shortcut or violation is usually a reasonable local settlement of a
conflict the organisation refused to settle — the heart of [local
rationality](local-rationality-principle.md). Hollnagel (2009) names these
settlements the [efficiency–thoroughness trade-off
(ETTO)](efficiency-thoroughness-trade-off.md); demanding efficiency daily and
then condemning the trade-off after an accident is the [authority–responsibility
mismatch](authority-responsibility-mismatch.md) in action. The rarer,
deliberate move in the opposite direction — relaxing production on purpose to
protect safety — is a [sacrifice judgement](sacrifice-judgements.md), and how
an organisation treats one that turns out (with hindsight) to have been
unnecessary is a sharper test of its culture than how it treats one that was
vindicated.

These daily settlements are also what moves an operation inside [Rasmussen's
three-boundary space](rasmussen-boundary-model.md): pressure has nowhere else
to go except toward the workload and safety boundaries, since retreating
toward the economic boundary is the one direction competitive pressure
forecloses.

Consequences:

- Repeated local settlements in favour of production are the engine of
  [drift into failure](drift-into-failure.md) and [normalisation of
  deviance](normalization-of-deviance.md): each settlement pays off
  immediately, and margins erode invisibly.
- A [systems-theoretic](systems-theoretic-accident-model.md) safety strategy
  therefore centres on making goal trade-offs *visible* and enforcing safety
  constraints explicitly, rather than exhorting individuals to "put safety
  first".
- After an incident, ask who created the double bind the operator was in, not
  why the operator failed to be both fast and careful at once.
