  Any psychological label in a finding must be decomposed into observable
  components with a documented path from data to conclusion — otherwise it
  is a judgment pasted over the data.

Large, underspecified labels — "complacency", "non-compliance", "loss of
situation awareness", "loss of CRM" — get pasted over data as if they were
findings. The discipline that prevents this is **operationalization**: break
the label into observable components and leave an analytic trace showing how
the specific data support each component, so a reader can audit the
inference chain rather than trust the label. A label that cannot be
operationalised should be deleted (see [complacency and loss of SA as
pseudo-explanations](complacency-and-loss-of-situation-awareness.md)).

Worked example — crew resource management. "Loss of CRM" is vague; two
frameworks make it observable:

- **Orasanu (NASA)**: did the crew maintain (1) shared understanding of the
  situation and its cause, (2) shared understanding of goals and desired
  outcomes, (3) shared understanding of the solution strategy — who does
  what, when, why?
- **Nevile (conversation analysis)**: fine-grained transcripts with prosodic
  notation — pauses in seconds `(1.2)`, micro-pauses `(.)`, accelerated
  `>text<` and slowed `<text>` talk, quiet talk `°text°`, overlapping talk
  `[text]` — expose interaction dynamics that low-resolution transcripts
  flatten: unequal turn-taking, expected prompts left unanswered,
  overlapping/stepped-on speech, unsolicited repair of another's speech —
  each an observable marker of role conflict, stress, or hierarchical
  friction.

This is the discipline behind **process tracing** — Dekker's answer to Jim
Reason's challenge that reconstructing a dead crew's cognition means
"crawling into the skull of a dead man". Process tracing builds two
parallel, interlinked accounts of a problem-solving episode: a
**domain-specific account** (operational telemetry, voice recordings,
technical system states) and a **concept-dependent account** (established
patterns of human performance — e.g. mapping erratic display checks to
automation surprise or [cognitive fixation](cognitive-fixation.md)). The
concepts earn their place only by their documented mapping onto the domain
data — the same analytic-trace standard, and the method that makes
[speaking for the dead](speaking-for-the-dead.md) possible at all.

The evidential inputs come from [debriefings and
recordings](human-factors-debriefing.md); the standard protects findings
against the [decontextualization
traps](decontextualization-traps.md) (an operationalised claim must survive
being checked against the timeline it came from) and keeps analysis on the
explanatory side of [judgmental language](judgmental-language.md).
