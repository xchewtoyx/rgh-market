---
type: concept
title: Speaking for the Dead
description: >
  The ethical stance of fatal-accident investigation — reconstructing why
  actions made local sense, rather than reducing the deceased to a list of
  failures — protects both the dead and the operators who face the same
  traps tomorrow.
sources:
  - title: The Field Guide to Understanding 'Human Error'
    resource: "The Field Guide to Understanding 'Human Error' (Dekker), epilogue"
---

Two ways to write about practitioners who died in the accident being
investigated:

- **Speaking *about* the dead** (Old View): driven by hindsight and outcome
  knowledge, lining up "mistakes" toward the known bad ending, reducing a
  life to a bulleted list of deficiencies — "failed to monitor speed",
  "violated regulations", "failed to initiate go-around" — the combined
  output of [counterfactuals](counterfactual-reasoning.md) and [judgmental
  language](judgmental-language.md).
- **Speaking *for* the dead** (New View): reconstructing the unfolding
  situation from inside the tunnel to uncover why decisions made local sense
  ([local rationality](local-rationality-principle.md)) — display ambiguity,
  [goal trade-offs](goal-conflicts-and-production-pressure.md), workload
  peaks — and translating those details into performance concepts that
  prevent recurrence (via [process
  tracing](operationalizing-psychological-labels.md)).

The stance carries two ethical imperatives:

1. **Protect the deceased and their families.** Operators went to work
   expecting to come home. Speaking for them prevents investigation boards
   from defaming dead practitioners — who cannot give
   [their account](just-culture.md) — to shield commercial or political
   interests, and gives families closure with dignity.
2. **Safeguard future operators.** The dead met hazards that living
   operators will meet tomorrow. Dismissing their actions as inexplicable
   "human error" leaves the traps in the system ([error as
   symptom](human-error-as-symptom.md)); conceptualising the performance
   regularities involved converts past tragedy into future protection.
