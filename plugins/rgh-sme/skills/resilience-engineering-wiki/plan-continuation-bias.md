type: concept
title: Plan-Continuation Bias
description: >
  Sticking with the original plan as conditions gradually deteriorate,
  because strong early cues supported it and later contrary cues arrive weak,
  ambiguous, and incremental.
sources:
  - title: The Field Guide to Understanding 'Human Error'
    resource: "The Field Guide to Understanding 'Human Error' (Dekker), ch. 4"
---

Plan continuation (Orasanu / NASA) is sticking to an original operational
plan even though dynamic conditions call for revision. It is not
stubbornness; it follows from how decision-making actually works in motion.
Operators do not compare discrete options at decision points — cognition is
front-loaded into continuous situation assessment: *"is this still doable?"*
As long as the answer reads yes, the plan continues by default.

The engine is **cue asymmetry**. The cues that supported the plan arrived
early, strong, salient, and unambiguous. The cues that should undo it arrive
late, weak, ambiguous, and incremental — deterioration is gradual, so no
single cue is decisive enough to dislodge established momentum.

The aviation exemplar is the unstabilised approach. Guidelines prescribe
breaking off at fixed altitude gates (1,000 ft / 500 ft), yet crews continue:
they treat the gates as negotiable parameters weighed against traffic and
weather, not rigid stop rules — a live example of [procedures as resources
for action](procedures-as-resources-for-action.md). Reminding crews of the
rule fails because it does not touch the real mechanism, continued
do-ability assessment, nor the [production
pressures](goal-conflicts-and-production-pressure.md) (schedule, fuel)
leaning on it.

What works is changing the incentive structure rather than exhorting
vigilance: non-punitive, positively rewarded abandonment — "no questions
asked" go-around policies, a culture in which "every landing is a failed
go-around". Generalised: give people a pre-legitimised exit from the plan,
so abandoning it costs nothing socially or professionally.

After an accident, "they pressed on despite the warnings" is
[hindsight](hindsight-bias.md) talking — inside the tunnel, the warnings were
the weak late cues, and continuing was passing every local do-ability check.

One concrete countermeasure is deliberate [choke-point
detection](choke-point-detection.md): explicitly summing several
individually-tolerable risk factors into a cumulative judgment, rather than
checking each against its own separate limit — the addition is not automatic
even among trained people with identical information.
