Where incident data no longer maps real vulnerability ([Wald's bomber
paradox](walds-bomber-paradox.md)) and more rules add nothing ([the
ultra-safe plateau](amalberti-safety-continuum.md)), protection comes from
organisational practices aimed at the places no reports come from:

1. **Meta-monitoring** — continuously monitor and audit *how the
   organisation monitors safety*, not just the safety numbers.
2. **Distrust past success** — never read accident-free operation as
   evidence of future safety (the core of [chronic
   unease](chronic-unease.md)).
3. **Resist distancing through differencing** — stop dismissing other
   organisations' disasters as "couldn't happen here because we're
   different"; the differences cited are usually cosmetic, the
   [drift mechanisms](drift-into-failure.md) generic.
4. **Resist fragmented problem-solving** — organisational silos hide
   cross-departmental drift; each silo sees only a local, tolerable
   deviation.
5. **Measure work-as-imagined against work-as-done** — actively track the
   distance between formal procedure and floor practice
   ([the gap](work-as-imagined-vs-work-as-done.md) where [procedural
   drift](procedural-drift.md) accumulates).
6. **Keep risk conversations alive** — discuss operational threats
   continuously, *especially* when conditions appear calm; calm is what the
   incubation period feels like. The organisational failure mode this
   guards against has a name — **hysteresis**: retaining an obsolete risk
   model (most commonly, one that still attributes threats primarily to
   individual human error) well past the point new evidence should have
   updated it, simply because updating a standing model takes active
   effort a calm period gives no trigger for.
7. **Empower structural dissent** — create formal roles with authority to
   challenge consensus safety assumptions, so minority doubt does not
   depend on individual courage (which [blame
   destroys](blame-suppresses-reporting.md)).
8. **Institutionalise fresh perspectives** — bring in external,
   multi-disciplinary observers to surface assumptions insiders have
   [normalised](normalization-of-deviance.md) into invisibility.
