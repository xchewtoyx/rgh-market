---
type: concept
title: "Investigate Up and Out, Not Down and In"
description: >
  A componential investigation digs deeper into the failing subsystem's own
  parts; a systemic one steps outward into the organisational, historical,
  and political context that put those parts under pressure in the first place.
sources:
  - title: "Drift into Failure: From Hunting Broken Components to Understanding Complex Systems"
    resource: "Drift into Failure (Dekker), ch. 3"
---

Two investigations can both claim to look "deeper" and produce opposite kinds
of depth. **Down and in** means staying inside the failing subsystem and
resolving it into ever-smaller components — a bag, a label, a connector, a
scanning step — each of which yields another componential fix
([root-cause fallacy](root-cause-fallacy.md)). **Up and out** means stepping
outward from the event into the organisational, historical, budgetary, and
political conditions that put the people at the sharp end under the pressure
they were under: staffing policy, contractor structures, decades of
resource-constrained decisions, an absent institutional vision.

The contrast is visible in how differently two investigations of a similar
technical failure can turn out depending only on which direction they dig.
One investigation can go down and in even while nominally reaching all the
way to a regulator, and still terminate in a list of broken components; a
later investigation of a structurally similar failure can go up and out and
surface the organisational drift the first one missed entirely — with a
worked contrast in [protective structures fuelling
drift](protective-structures-can-fuel-drift.md). Going up and out is also
what separates [explanatory factors from change
factors](explanatory-vs-change-factors.md): the true change factor is
disproportionately more likely to live in the blunt-end direction (up and
out) than among the sharp-end components an initial inquiry naturally
gravitates toward ([sharp end and blunt
end](sharp-end-and-blunt-end.md)).

Choosing which direction to dig is not neutral fact-finding — it is a choice
about [which accident model](accident-model-shapes-what-you-find.md) is in
play, made (often tacitly) before the investigation starts.
