---
type: concept
title: Unruly Technology
description: >
  Brian Wynne's term for the gap between a technology's tidy engineering
  image and its messy, not-fully-governable behaviour in real operational
  practice, and the specific conditions under which people reinterpret
  anomalous data rather than revise their beliefs about it.
sources:
  - title: "Drift into Failure: From Hunting Broken Components to Understanding Complex Systems"
    resource: "Drift into Failure (Dekker), ch. 2"
---

Wynne's "unruly" is deliberately not "uncertain." Uncertain implies a
calculable unknown that better engineering will eventually close. Unruly
means disorderly — not amenable to discipline or control even after
certification, regulation, and maintenance have all been applied. It names
the gap between the engineering department's tidy model of how a technology
behaves and its actual, messy interior once released into a field of
practice, subject to wear, workarounds, and locally-adapted procedures.

The operational consequence is a specific reconciliation move: when field
data conflicts with what people believe about an unruly technology, they far
more often reinterpret the data than revise the belief. This move becomes
especially likely when three conditions hold together:

1. The anomaly is one of many similarly common, already-tolerated problems
   (it doesn't stand out).
2. A routine operational compensation already exists (added redundancy, an
   inspection workaround) that lets work continue without addressing the
   anomaly directly.
3. A real fix would be economically or operationally disruptive.

When all three hold, an anomaly gets absorbed into the category of "known,
manageable issue" rather than treated as a warning — the same absorption
mechanism described in [ambiguous threats](ambiguous-threats.md), but
grounded here specifically in properties of the technology and its
maintenance economics rather than in organisational mindset alone. Unruly
technology is what [normalisation of deviance](normalization-of-deviance.md)
operates on: the technology's real tolerance is never fully known, so each
successful stretch of an interval or a margin looks like new, sound
knowledge about the equipment rather than borrowed risk.
