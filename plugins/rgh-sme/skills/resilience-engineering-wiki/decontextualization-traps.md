---
type: concept
title: Decontextualization Traps
description: >
  Three ways investigators strip behaviour of its unfolding context —
  micro-matching against procedures, cherry-picking quotes to fit a label,
  and shopping-bagging scattered cues into one clear warning.
sources:
  - title: The Field Guide to Understanding 'Human Error'
    resource: "The Field Guide to Understanding 'Human Error' (Dekker), ch. 3"
---

Behavioural data only means what it meant *in context*. Three traps detach
it:

- **Micro-matching**: comparing fragments of performance line-by-line
  against written procedures or against cues hindsight marked as critical
  (as in the Cali B757 analysis). This measures behaviour against the
  investigator's reconstructed reality instead of the operator's unfolding
  one — [hindsight bias](hindsight-bias.md) with a checklist — and against
  [work-as-imagined](work-as-imagined-vs-work-as-done.md) rather than the
  situation the procedure never anticipated.
- **Cherry-picking**: extracting isolated quotes from across a timeline to
  support a label chosen in advance ("hurried", ["loss of
  SA"](complacency-and-loss-of-situation-awareness.md)), ignoring how the
  situation evolved between utterances and treating distinct moments as
  interchangeable evidence.
- **Shopping-bagging**: bundling all the scattered, weak, contradictory cues
  from the whole event into one bag and presenting them as if they arrived
  simultaneously as a clear warning. In reality the cues trickled in
  ambiguously — the very asymmetry that drives [plan
  continuation](plan-continuation-bias.md) and makes
  [fixation](cognitive-fixation.md) reasonable.

All three traps generate the appearance of inexplicable operator failure
where a contextualised reading shows [locally rational
work](local-rationality-principle.md). Guarding against them requires
reconstructing the situation as it unfolded — via [structured
debriefing](human-factors-debriefing.md) and attention to [what was
observable, not merely available](data-availability-vs-observability.md) —
and [operationalising any label](operationalizing-psychological-labels.md)
into observable components before applying it.
