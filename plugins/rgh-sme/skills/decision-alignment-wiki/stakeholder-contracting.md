**Stakeholder contracting** is the foundational exchange of
[decision-engagement-phases](decision-engagement-phases.md):
what each party wants, will offer, and is responsible for.
How the work arrived —
[modes-of-entry-into-decision-work](modes-of-entry-into-decision-work.md) —
shapes what needs establishing first. It occurs at entry and
whenever the project changes — concretely, whenever scope,
sponsorship, data access, roles, timing, or expected outcomes
shift — not as a procedural formality. Run the actual
conversation using the
[contracting-meeting-structure](contracting-meeting-structure.md).

Build balanced partnership: parties state wants directly,
negotiate a workable exchange, and retain real choice to
proceed or not. Latent wishes for unilateral control distort
the contract into coercion.

Essential outcomes to lock before analysis:
- presenting problem and desired results
  ([desired-result-as-decision-criterion](desired-result-as-decision-criterion.md)),
- roles, methods, resources, timing, confidentiality,
  reporting,
- access and support required,
- facilitator limits and requirements,
- [multi-party-decision-contracts](multi-party-decision-contracts.md)
  (all influencers and veto holders).

Also
[surface-ambivalence-early](surface-ambivalence-early.md).
Without an explicit contract, later recommendations lack a
shared mandate and
[unfinished-alignment-business](unfinished-alignment-business.md)
appears as resistance.
[Line-ownership-of-implementation](line-ownership-of-implementation.md)
starts here: results cannot be guaranteed because
stakeholders control whether to act.
