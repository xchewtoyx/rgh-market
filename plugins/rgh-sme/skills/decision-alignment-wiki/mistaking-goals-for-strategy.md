---
type: concept
title: Mistaking Goals for Strategy
description: >
  Substituting performance targets or motivational slogans
  for a plan that overcomes named obstacles.
sources:
  - title: "Good Strategy/Bad Strategy: The Difference and Why It Matters"
    resource: "Good Strategy/Bad Strategy: The Difference and Why It Matters (Richard P. Rumelt), ch. 3"
---

**Mistaking goals for strategy** replaces a path through
obstacles with performance targets, financial desires, or
motivational slogans. A "20/20 plan" (20% growth, 20%
margin) states ambitions; when pressed on *how* against
stronger competitors, "motivation" and positive thinking are
not a [guiding-policy](guiding-policy.md).

This pattern is the content defect behind the
[execution-fallacy](execution-fallacy.md): after the targets
miss, people blame execution. A decision memo must still
carry [strategic-diagnosis](strategic-diagnosis.md), policy,
and [coherent-actions](coherent-actions.md) — not only
numbers that restate the desired end-state
([desired-result-as-decision-criterion](desired-result-as-decision-criterion.md)
is a yardstick for options, not a substitute for strategy).
