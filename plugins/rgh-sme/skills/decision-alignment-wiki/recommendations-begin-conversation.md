Recommendations begin a conversation; they do not end it.
A clear, fresh picture of why a problem or possibility
exists is roughly most of the contribution — enough that
stakeholders can often generate sound actions themselves.

Resist exhaustive data dumps and the wish to craft perfect,
untouchable recommendations. Use
[funneling-to-decision-themes](funneling-to-decision-themes.md)
and a few themes with clear
[observation-interpretation-implication](observation-interpretation-implication.md)
logic. Include necessary
[implementation-context-in-recommendations](implementation-context-in-recommendations.md)
and the
[three-layer-system-picture](three-layer-system-picture.md)
(system contribution, politics, relationships) without
treating it as personal criticism.

Anticipate
[managing-stakeholder-resistance](managing-stakeholder-resistance.md)
without trying to eliminate it: state the picture simply,
acknowledge uncertainty, ask for reactions, and stay with
emotional responses before pushing toward action in
[managing-alignment-meetings](managing-alignment-meetings.md).

[Walk-stakeholders-through-the-reasoning](walk-stakeholders-through-the-reasoning.md)
extends this with a specific presentation mechanism: move
step by step through the reasoning chain and pause for input
at each link, rather than presenting the finished picture as
one block and only then opening the floor.
