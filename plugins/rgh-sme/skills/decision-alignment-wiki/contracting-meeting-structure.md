type: concept
title: Contracting Meeting Structure
description: >
  A five-step sequence for conducting the contracting
  conversation itself, ending in an explicit agreement or an
  explicit decision not to proceed.
sources:
  - title: "Flawless Consulting"
    resource: "Flawless Consulting (Peter Block), ch. 6"
---

**Stakeholder contracting** (see
[stakeholder-contracting](stakeholder-contracting.md) for what
must be locked) is transacted in a specific conversation. Run
it in five steps:

1. **Personal acknowledgment** — a real connection before
   business, not a formality to rush past.
2. **Clarify the presenting concern** — ask the client to
   describe the concern and its consequences. Do not jump to
   solutions; pursue why the issue matters and what a
   successful outcome would look like
   ([discovery-before-solutioning](discovery-before-solutioning.md)).
3. **State wants and offers** — the consultant states wants as
   clearly as the client states theirs: access to people and
   data, sponsorship, openness to difficult feedback,
   resources, decisions, and active participation. Ask what
   the client will *do*, not only what they expect to receive.
4. **Address exposure, control, and motivation** — explore
   reservations directly rather than letting them stay
   unspoken (see
   [surface-ambivalence-early](surface-ambivalence-early.md)).
5. **Reach explicit agreement, or decide not to proceed** — a
   clear no is preferable to a project built on hidden
   reluctance. Agreement should cover purpose, outcomes,
   roles, methodology, boundaries, schedule, resources,
   confidentiality, feedback, and termination or
   recontracting conditions. If this step stalls, see
   [naming-the-contracting-impasse](naming-the-contracting-impasse.md).

Early choices in the meeting — setting, timing, who initiates,
how interaction unfolds — are informative about how the
project may unfold; notice them without treating them as proof
or final judgment. Running this sequence well is what fills in
the essential outcomes named in
[stakeholder-contracting](stakeholder-contracting.md) and
opens the first
[decision-engagement-phases](decision-engagement-phases.md).
