The **Create-Destroy Technique** is a rigor-testing method for options analysis. Rather than simply arguing in favor of a preferred recommendation, the team must generate a robust alternative and then actively attempt to *destroy* both options by exposing their fault lines, internal contradictions, and empirical weaknesses.

### Virtual Panel of Experts
Because attacking one's own ideas is psychologically difficult, decision-makers can simulate a "virtual panel of experts." This involves mentally evaluating the proposal through the lens of specific, well-understood industry leaders or internal stakeholders (e.g., asking how a highly customer-centric leader or a strict finance director would critique the plan).

### Focus on Corner Solutions
In executing this technique, teams should strive for "corner solutions"—proposals that concentrate resources heavily on one dimension—rather than diluted, average compromises. Focused proposals are easier to critique, test, and execute, avoiding [consensus-driven-paralysis](consensus-driven-paralysis.md).

Applying this technique makes decisions far more defensible and prevents the organization from committing to weak, unvetted plans.
