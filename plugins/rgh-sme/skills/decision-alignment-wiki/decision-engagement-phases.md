title: Decision Engagement Phases
description: >
  Sequence alignment work through contract, discovery,
  feedback, decision, and implementation—completing each
  phase before advancing.
sources:
  - title: "Flawless Consulting"
    resource: "Flawless Consulting (Peter Block), ch. 4"
---

Keep decision and consulting work simple by completing the
business of the current phase before jumping ahead.
Core requirements by phase:

1. **Contract** — mutual wants, offers, and motivation
   ([stakeholder-contracting](stakeholder-contracting.md)).
2. **Discover** — a fresh picture and the system's
   contribution.
3. **Feed back** — focused, actionable analysis
   ([recommendations-begin-conversation](recommendations-begin-conversation.md)).
4. **Decide and engage** — secure a decision in
   [managing-alignment-meetings](managing-alignment-meetings.md).
5. **Implement** — with
   [line-ownership-of-implementation](line-ownership-of-implementation.md)
   and
   [implementation-as-engagement](implementation-as-engagement.md).
6. **Extend, recycle, or terminate** deliberately.

Skipping phase completion leaves
[unfinished-alignment-business](unfinished-alignment-business.md)
that reappears as resistance or stalled action. Authenticity
— putting the actual experience of working together into
words — surfaces disagreement early instead of managing the
relationship with cleverness or control.
