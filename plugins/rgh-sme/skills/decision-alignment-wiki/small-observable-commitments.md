---
type: concept
title: Small Observable Commitments
description: >
  Build follow-through from small, visible commitments with
  clear support, feedback, learning, and recontracting when
  conditions change.
sources:
  - title: "Flawless Consulting"
    resource: "Flawless Consulting (Peter Block), ch. 19"
---

After a decision, prefer small, observable commitments over
one grand launch. Each commitment should specify:

- what will be done and by whom
  ([line-ownership-of-implementation](line-ownership-of-implementation.md)),
- what support is required,
- how feedback will show consequences,
- when to recontract if conditions or understanding change.

This turns
[managing-alignment-meetings](managing-alignment-meetings.md)
"what / who / by when / support" into ongoing coordinated
action under
[implementation-as-engagement](implementation-as-engagement.md).
It also operationalizes
[coherent-actions](coherent-actions.md) without pretending
that naming actions once is enough follow-through.

Engagement must continue after the decision: invite affected
people into shaping the work and surface doubts early so
resistance is handled before commitments silently die.

At scale, back each commitment with
[action-item-closure-tracking](action-item-closure-tracking.md)
so follow-through can be audited rather than assumed.
