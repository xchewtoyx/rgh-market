---
type: concept
title: Decision Pod Accountability
description: >
  A small, chartered group that will hear the reasoning behind a
  decision afterward changes how carefully that decision gets
  made in the moment, even with no money or authority at stake.
sources:
  - title: "Thinking in Bets: Making Smarter Decisions When You Don't Have All the Facts"
    resource: "Thinking in Bets (Annie Duke), ch. 4"
---

A small, standing group chartered specifically for honest
after-the-fact review — as few as three people ("two to disagree
and one to referee") — catches errors an individual reliably misses
in their own reasoning, because
[blind-spot bias](self-serving-outcome-attribution.md) is much
easier for someone else to see than for the person holding it. The
group does not need formal authority over the decision to matter:
anticipating having to explain a real-time choice to people whose
judgment you respect changes how carefully you make it, before any
review ever happens. A gambler with a preset spending limit held
that limit not through in-the-moment willpower (unreliable once
cash and temptation are both available) but because he knew he
would have to justify blowing through it to people he answered to
— the anticipated conversation did the disciplining work, not the
limit itself.

This works only if the group is explicitly chartered for it, not
merely present: people default to confirming each other's views
unless the group has deliberately set accuracy, not agreement, as
its goal (see
[conditions-for-exploratory-thought](conditions-for-exploratory-thought.md)
for what actually turns a group toward genuine scrutiny rather than
reflexive agreement). Different relationships can serve different
functions — a decision pod does not need to be, and usually should
not try to be, every relationship a person has.

This complements
[standing-decision-council](standing-decision-council.md): a
council holds *authority* over a decision and gives a hard call
institutional backing; a decision pod holds no authority at all and
exists purely to improve the *reasoning* behind decisions its
members make elsewhere, including ones the pod itself never votes
on.
