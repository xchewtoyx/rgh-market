---
type: concept
title: Multi-Party Decision Contracts
description: >
  Map every party who can influence or veto the work—
  triangles and rectangles included—so agreements are not
  later undermined by invisible sponsors.
sources:
  - title: "Flawless Consulting"
    resource: "Flawless Consulting (Peter Block), ch. 5"
---

Identify all parties who influence the decision work — profile
each with
[stakeholder-analysis-attributes](stakeholder-analysis-attributes.md)
so the map is not limited to whoever is already in the room.
A client plus their boss forms a **triangular contract**;
adding the facilitator’s boss forms a **rectangle**.
Invisible sponsors or decision makers must be acknowledged
so a bilateral agreement is not later undone.

In decision documents and
[stakeholder-contracting](stakeholder-contracting.md),
distinguish who must **agree**, who must be **informed**,
and who can **veto**. Sequence review and sign-off
accordingly in
[managing-alignment-meetings](managing-alignment-meetings.md).
Missing a vertex is a common source of
[unfinished-alignment-business](unfinished-alignment-business.md)
and post-decision reversal.

When the other party to the contract is a group rather than a
single client, add group-specific clarity before proceeding:
who actually speaks for the group, who holds real decision
authority within it, how dissent inside the group will be
handled, and what support the group needs from its own
leaders. Being direct about these realities up front is itself
the first model of the working style the rest of the
engagement will follow.
