---
type: concept
title: Alignment Before Empowerment
description: >
  Coordinated action requires aligned direction first — empowering
  individuals when energies still point different ways wastes
  effort and worsens chaos.
sources:
  - title: "The Fifth Discipline: The Art and Practice of the Learning Organization"
    resource: "The Fifth Discipline (Peter Senge), ch. 11"
---

**Alignment** is when a group functions as a whole: personal power
— the ability to accomplish intended results — harmonizes toward
shared aims rather than canceling out. In an unaligned team,
individuals may work extraordinarily hard yet waste energy because
their efforts point in different directions — like incoherent light
from a bulb versus coherent light from a laser.

Alignment is the necessary precondition before empowering individuals
empowers the **whole** team. Empowering people under low alignment
"worsens the chaos." Shared vision and talented individuals are not
enough; many teams share a vision for a while yet fail to learn or
execute together.

Team learning — aligning and developing a team's capacity to create
results its members truly desire — is a **collective** discipline.
It is meaningless to say one person has "mastered team learning" any
more than one musician has "mastered being a great jazz ensemble."
Three dimensions matter for decision work:

1. Thinking insightfully about complex issues — many minds can be
   more intelligent than one, though teams often default to *less*
   intelligent than their best member unless process counters
   [groupthink](groupthink.md) and
   [defensive routines](defensive-routines.md).
2. Innovative, coordinated action — operational trust where each
   member stays conscious of others and acts in complementary ways.
3. Propagation to other teams — senior-team choices are usually
   executed through sub-teams, so practices must spread.

Alignment work belongs before delegating execution authority in
[communicating-intent-for-coordinated-action](communicating-intent-for-coordinated-action.md)
and before assuming
[stakeholder-buy-in-sequencing](stakeholder-buy-in-sequencing.md)
will hold without a shared picture of what is being bought into.
