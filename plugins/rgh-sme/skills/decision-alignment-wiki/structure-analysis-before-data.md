---
type: concept
title: Structure Analysis Before Data
description: >
  Design the analytical structure and plausible causes first
  so fact-gathering tests decisions instead of producing
  unwieldy reports.
sources:
  - title: "The Minto Pyramid Principle: Logic in Writing, Thinking and Problem Solving"
    resource: "The Minto Pyramid Principle (Barbara Minto), ch. 9"
---

Do not collect all available data and hunt for meaning
later. Broad company or industry sweeps generate irrelevant
facts, incomplete evidence, and reports that cannot drive a
decision.

Structure analysis first: generate possible explanations of
why R1 exists
([problem-definition-framework](problem-definition-framework.md)),
devise discriminating yes/no tests, gather only the data
that confirms or rules them out, then formulate actions.
The opening scene of the problem definition shows where to
seek an appropriate diagnostic frame for
[strategic-diagnosis](strategic-diagnosis.md).

This discipline feeds
[yes-no-issue-analysis](yes-no-issue-analysis.md) and keeps
options work aimed at a recommendation rather than a data
museum. It complements
[observation-interpretation-implication](observation-interpretation-implication.md)
on the presentation side.
