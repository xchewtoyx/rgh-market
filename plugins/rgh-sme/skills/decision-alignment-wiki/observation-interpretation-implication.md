type: concept
title: Observation–Interpretation–Implication
description: >
  Structure recommendation themes as linked observations,
  interpretations, and implications instead of raw data
  dumps.
sources:
  - title: "Flawless Consulting"
    resource: "Flawless Consulting (Peter Block), ch. 17"
---

When preparing a decision or feedback document, focus on a
few themes — not every finding. For each theme, make the
logic chain explicit:

1. **Observation** — what was seen or heard.
2. **Interpretation** — what that pattern means.
3. **Implication** — what follows for choice or action.

This chain lets stakeholders follow the argument to a
recommendation without drowning in evidence. It supports
[recommendations-begin-conversation](recommendations-begin-conversation.md):
the picture and its logic invite dialogue; perfect
prescriptions that clients cannot touch shut it down.

Use the same discipline in
[managing-alignment-meetings](managing-alignment-meetings.md)
so data and dialogue stay sequenced toward a decision.
