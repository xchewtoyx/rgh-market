The **Strategic Kernel** is the core logical structure of any good strategy or decision-making framework. It ensures that a decision is not merely a list of uncoordinated goals, but a coherent argument for action. 

Regardless of the scope or domain, the kernel consists of three closely linked components:
1. **[Strategic Diagnosis](strategic-diagnosis.md)**: Naming and simplifying the challenge.
2. **[Guiding Policy](guiding-policy.md)**: Selecting an overall approach to address the challenge.
3. **[Coherent Actions](coherent-actions.md)**: Designing a set of coordinated steps to carry out the guiding policy.

By structuring a decision document around these three components, authors can present a clear, defensible argument that moves from analysis to execution, reinforcing the [necessity-of-strategic-choice](necessity-of-strategic-choice.md).
