---
type: concept
title: Anchor Stakeholder Suggestions to Outcomes
description: >
  When a stakeholder proposes work that misses the current target
  audience or outcome, respectfully restate those constraints
  instead of accepting or dismissing the idea outright.
sources:
  - title: "User Story Mapping"
    resource: "User Story Mapping (Jeff Patton), ch. 18"
---

Stakeholder reviews surface ideas from people who were not part of
deferral and prioritization decisions. Some suggestions are useful
new input; others would pull the team off the agreed target
audience or outcome.

When a suggestion is off-target, **anchor** the conversation to
the stated target customers and success criteria — remind everyone
what this slice of work is trying to achieve and for whom — rather
than dismissing the person or blindly adding the request to the
backlog. The stakeholder may not have had the framing context the
delivery team used when saying no to other opportunities.

This keeps [stakeholder-discovery-review-timing](stakeholder-discovery-review-timing.md)
and [team-then-stakeholder-review-sequence](team-then-stakeholder-review-sequence.md)
productive: feedback is welcome, but alignment requires tying new
input back to the decision already in flight. It complements
[fair-treatment-of-objections](fair-treatment-of-objections.md)
(take objections seriously) and
[desired-result-as-decision-criterion](desired-result-as-decision-criterion.md)
(have a concrete R2 so "off-target" is checkable, not arbitrary).

If anchoring reveals that stakeholders actually want a different
outcome, that is a **new decision**, not a tweak — escalate or
re-run scoping rather than smuggling a scope change through as
"just one more story."
