The **Neutralizing Resistance Protocol** is a communication technique used to address stakeholder resistance during alignment and decision discussions. Rather than trying to argue, interpret, or reassure prematurely, this protocol brings attention to the current dynamic to enable authentic commitment.

### The Intervention Sequence
1. **State the observable behavior neutrally**: Name what is happening without judgment (e.g., *"You've asked for additional detail on the minor points three times now..."* or *"We've been silent on this proposal for a few minutes..."*).
2. **Name your own experience/concern**: Express your perspective without accusation or blame (e.g., *"I'm concerned we are focusing on minor details to avoid deciding on the core path..."*).
3. **Ask the stakeholder to share their perspective**: Prompt them to express their underlying feelings or reservations using open-ended questions. Avoid "why" questions that invite defensiveness (e.g., *"What reservations do you have about proceeding?"* or *"What do you think this recommendation will require from your team?"*).
4. **Be silent and listen**: Allow the stakeholder to respond fully without interruption.

Once the resistance has been expressed and heard, the leader can return to the business at hand: clarifying information, choice, and next action, which can be structured using [managing-alignment-meetings](managing-alignment-meetings.md).
