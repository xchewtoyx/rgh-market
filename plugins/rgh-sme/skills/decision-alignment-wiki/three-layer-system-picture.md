title: Three-Layer System Picture
description: >
  Frame discovery as presenting issue, others' contribution,
  and the client's own sustaining behaviors so action follows
  from responsibility.
sources:
  - title: "Flawless Consulting"
    resource: "Flawless Consulting (Peter Block), ch. 16"
---

After discovery, the decision picture works at three layers:

1. **Presenting issue** — what was asked about.
2. **Others / structures** — how people and systems around
   the issue contribute.
3. **Client contribution** — what the requesting party does
   that helps create or sustain the situation.

The third layer is essential: action follows from
responsibility, not blame. It is the core of
[implementation-context-in-recommendations](implementation-context-in-recommendations.md)
and keeps
[recommendations-begin-conversation](recommendations-begin-conversation.md)
honest about ownership
([line-ownership-of-implementation](line-ownership-of-implementation.md)).

Choose consciously between problem focus and
asset/possibility focus, and between third-party inquiry and
whole-system self-assessment — but do not skip layer three
to stay comfortable. That omission becomes
[unfinished-alignment-business](unfinished-alignment-business.md).
