Unfinished business accumulates across
[decision-engagement-phases](decision-engagement-phases.md).
If contracting leaves coercion, unclear motivation, or
missing agreements unspoken, those gaps later show up
indirectly:

- endless clarifying questions,
- resistance to data
  ([managing-stakeholder-resistance](managing-stakeholder-resistance.md)),
- stalled
  [implementation-as-engagement](implementation-as-engagement.md).

Surface disagreement and commitment gaps in the phase where
they belong — especially
[stakeholder-contracting](stakeholder-contracting.md) —
rather than hoping analysis will paper them over. Completing
phase business is how alignment stays defensible through
action, not only through the recommendation text.
