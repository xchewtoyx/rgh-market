title: Dialogue Session Ground Rules
description: >
  Explicit norms for a multi-day alignment session — suspend
  assumptions, leave hierarchy at the door, and inquire into
  reasoning — so entrenched splits can be reframed before
  options are chosen.
sources:
  - title: "The Fifth Discipline: The Art and Practice of the Learning Organization"
    resource: "The Fifth Discipline (Peter Senge), ch. 11"
---

When a leadership team carries a long-standing split that blocks
coordinated decisions — for example, R&D and marketing each
running separate product strategies while everyone denies an
adversarial arm-wrestle — a **dialogue session** with explicit
ground rules can reframe the conflict before the group returns to
advocacy and choice.

A workable rule set, communicated in advance (David Bohm, via Senge):

1. **Suspension of assumptions** — hold assumptions "as if suspended
   before us," accessible to questioning, not defended, suppressed,
   or eliminated. Surfacing mutual assumptions as *assumptions*
   (for example, R&D as "keeper of the flame" versus marketing as
   "unapproachable") opens strategic dialogue that positional debate
   blocks.
2. **Regard one another as colleagues** — even when views differ;
   hierarchy is antithetical to dialogue unless seniors surrender
   the privilege of automatic prevail and juniors surrender the
   security of nondisclosure.
3. **Spirit of inquiry** — ask what leads someone to say or
   believe something, rather than debating conclusions first.
4. **A facilitator who holds the context** — maintains group ownership
   of process, catches drift into positional discussion, and models
   dialogue (for example, "but the opposite may also be true"). As
   skill grows, the facilitator role can dissolve into the group.

See [dialogue-versus-discussion](dialogue-versus-discussion.md) for
why these rules apply to divergence, not to decision convergence.

Over a sustained session (often one to two days), these rules let
participants name the unspoken frames driving a split — such as an
implicit "research-driven product company" identity that casts R&D
and everyone else as adversaries, or an "empowerment versus
control" dynamic underneath budget fights — and rebuild from one
coordinated plan instead of separate, competing ones.

This is not a substitute for
[managing-alignment-meetings](managing-alignment-meetings.md)'s
decision-forcing closure (what, who, by when). Dialogue sessions
heal the mental models and relationships that make later decision
meetings productive; they belong earlier in
[decision-engagement-phases](decision-engagement-phases.md), before
a recommendation is ready for sign-off. They work best when paired
with [conditions-for-exploratory-thought](conditions-for-exploratory-thought.md)
— an audience genuinely interested in accuracy, not in winning —
and when participants use
[dissent-without-confrontation](dissent-without-confrontation.md)
rather than accusatory framing.
