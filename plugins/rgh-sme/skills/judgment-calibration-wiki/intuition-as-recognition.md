  and the answer feels immediate, not mysterious.

Herbert Simon: "The situation has provided a cue; this cue has given the expert
access to information stored in memory, and the information provides the
answer. Intuition is nothing more and nothing less than recognition."

Gary Klein's fire commander ordered team out of burning kitchen based on inarticulate
"sixth sense" (fire unusually quiet, ears unusually hot) — floor collapsed moments
later; fire source was basement. Compare chess master seeing "White mates in three,"
physician diagnosing at a glance, toddler naming a dog.

Original heuristics-and-biases work focused on errors as evidence for heuristics; the
book aims for a richer picture where **skill and heuristics are alternative sources**
of intuitive judgments — accurate expert intuition from prolonged practice, not the
same shortcuts that misfire in [wicked and low-validity environments](wicked-and-low-validity-environments.md).

Contrasting misfire: CIO invested in Ford because impressed at auto show — [affect
heuristic](affect-heuristic.md) substituted "do I like Ford cars?" for "is Ford
stock underpriced?" When no heuristic answer comes, people shift to slow thinking.

Gary Klein's **recognition-primed decision (RPD) model** describes
fireground commanders and chess experts: an automatic System 1 process generates
a tentative plan via associative memory; a deliberate System 2 process then
mentally simulates it to check, modify, or discard it for the next option.

The mystery of "knowing without knowing how" is not special to intuition — it
is the normal condition of memory (instantly recognizing a friend's face).
Gladwell's kouros story illustrates recognition, but a properly conducted
inquiry would likely still find the guiding cue; substituting "does he look
like a strong leader" for "will he be a good president" (President Harding)
shows recognition gone wrong.

Genuine expertise in complex domains is a large collection of miniskills taking
roughly **10,000 hours** of dedicated practice — chess masters become familiar
with thousands of recurring configurations, reading positions at a glance like
skilled readers perceive whole clauses.

Trust such intuitions only when [two conditions for trustworthy expert intuition](two-conditions-for-trustworthy-expert-intuition.md)
are met.
