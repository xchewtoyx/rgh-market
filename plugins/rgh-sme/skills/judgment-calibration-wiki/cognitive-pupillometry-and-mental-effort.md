Pupil size indexes **mental effort** distinct from emotional arousal (Hess): pupils
dilate with harder problems. Kahneman and Beatty's **Add-1** task — read a random
4-digit string, wait two beats, report each digit incremented by 1 (5294 → 6305)
at metronome pace — makes [System 2](system-1-and-system-2.md) "work at full tilt."
Few manage more than four digits; **Add-3** is harder still.

Pupil size traces an **inverted-V** during transformation tasks: effort builds as
digits are heard, peaks during the transformed-string response, relaxes as short-term
memory unloads. Longer strings → larger dilation; Add-1 with four digits dilates more
than passively holding seven digits for recall. Add-3 is near the human limit — in the
first five seconds pupil area increases ~50% and heart rate ~7 bpm; pupils stop
dilating or shrink when subjects are given more digits than they can handle.

Pupils are an **electricity-meter** index of current mental-energy use: we choose
what to do but have limited control over effort required. Even if your life depended
on holding four digits for ten seconds, you could not exert as much effort as Add-3
on the same digits forces.

## Pace of mental life

During routine conversation, pupil size stays small — "no more than retaining two or
three digits" of demand. Mental life is normally a **comfortable walk**, sometimes
**jogging**, rarely a **frantic sprint**. Add-1/Add-3 are sprints; casual chat is
a stroll. Connect [ego depletion and mental effort budget](ego-depletion-and-mental-effort-budget.md).

## Relative effort (pupil-dilation ranking)

From Kahneman and Beatty's studies, roughly:

baseline arousal < holding one–two digits or learning word-digit association (~5%
of Add-3 increase) < pitch discrimination < inhibiting tendency to read words (Stroop-like)
< holding six–seven digits (comparable to retrieving a phone number while organizing
speech) < two-digit multiplication and Add-3 (near most people's limit).

As **skill** increases, energy demand diminishes (fewer brain regions active). **Talent**
has a similar effect — more intelligent individuals need less effort for the same
problems, by both pupil and brain imaging. See [law of least effort](law-of-least-effort.md).
