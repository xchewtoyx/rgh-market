---
type: concept
title: Anchoring as Priming
description: >
  Anchors selectively evoke compatible associative evidence in System 1, biasing
  retrieval before System 2 estimates.
sources:
  - title: Thinking, Fast and Slow
    resource: "Thinking, Fast and Slow (Kahneman), ch. 11"
---

Kahneman's account: [anchoring](anchoring-effect.md) as **suggestion/priming** —
bringing a number to mind causes compatible impressions, like asking "Do you feel
numbness in your left leg?" prompting reports of exactly that.

Asked whether Gandhi died before or after 144, nobody believes the figure, but
associative machinery generates an impression of a very old man. System 1 tries
to make sentences true — associative coherence.

Mussweiler and Strack: German temperature anchor 68°F sped recognition of
summer words; 40°F sped winter words — different memory sets biased estimates.
High car-price anchors primed luxury brands; low anchors primed Volkswagen.

Suggestion and priming share one automatic System 1 operation. Random anchors
carry zero information yet work — the mechanism is associative, not evidential.
Links to [WYSIATI](wysiati.md): judgment depends on retrieved story material,
largely independent of anchor truth.
