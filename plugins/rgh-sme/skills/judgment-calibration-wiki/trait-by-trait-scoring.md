Structured **trait-by-trait scoring** breaks global impressions into independent
dimensions evaluated separately before synthesis — reducing [halo effect](halo-effect.md)
contamination from associative coherence.

The Israeli officer-selection observers assigned holistic scores with high
confidence after one hour of behavior — a substitute for years of future
performance. Decomposing judgments (and collecting independent ratings before
discussion) decorrelates errors and improves calibration.

Kahneman's 1955 Israeli army redesign: six traits (responsibility, sociability,
masculine pride, etc.) via factual pre-enlistment questions, each rated 1–5 in
fixed order before the next — explicit anti-halo design. "Your function is to
provide reliable measurements… Leave the predictive validity to me." Generalized
**Do It Yourself** hiring: pick ~six prerequisite traits, factual separate scoring
per trait, not one global impression.
