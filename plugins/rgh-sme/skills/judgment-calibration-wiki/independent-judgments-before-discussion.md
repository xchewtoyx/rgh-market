**Decorrelate error**: pooled judgments beat individuals when errors are independent
and average toward zero — wisdom of crowds (jar of pennies). Shared bias survives
aggregation; mutual influence shrinks effective sample size.

Police bar witnesses from discussing before testimony — prevents collusion and
unbiased correlation of errors. Meetings should require each member to write a
brief position summary **before** open discussion — preserving diverse knowledge;
standard discussion over-weights early assertive speakers.

Kahneman's grading fix: score one question across all students (not all questions
per student) to break [halo effect](halo-effect.md) where first essay biased later
ones for the same student — revealed real inconsistency at cost of spurious
homogeneity.

and [trait-by-trait scoring](trait-by-trait-scoring.md).
