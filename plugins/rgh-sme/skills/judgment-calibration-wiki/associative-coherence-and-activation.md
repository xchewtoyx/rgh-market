title: Associative Coherence and Activation
description: >
  System 1 spreads activation across ideas, emotions, and body responses into
  self-reinforcing coherent patterns that feel like understanding.

Reading "Bananas Vomit" triggers unpleasant images, disgust expressions, increased
heart rate, and sweat — an attenuated reaction to the real event, automatic and
beyond conscious control. The mind assumes temporal sequence and causal connection;
memory reconfigures to recognize associated words (sick, stink, yellow, fruit).

**Associative activation** is a spreading cascade: ideas evoke other ideas quickly,
automatically, effortlessly — "you did not will it and you could not stop it."

**Associative coherence** is the essential feature: each element connects to and
reinforces others — memories evoke emotions, emotions evoke facial expressions and
avoidance, which intensify feelings and reinforce compatible ideas. In roughly a
second, [System 1](system-1-and-system-2.md) links words into a causal story,
evaluates threat, and prepares for likely futures. System 1 treats mere word
conjunction as representation of reality; bodily reaction is part of the interpretation
("you think with your body, not only with your brain").

David Hume's association principles — **resemblance, contiguity, causality** — remain
a starting point. Modern **associative memory** models ideas as nodes linked by
causes, properties, categories; most activation is silent — only a few ideas reach
consciousness. "You know far less about yourself than you feel you do."

**Priming** (1980s breakthrough): exposure to a word changes ease of evoking related
words — EAT makes SO_P → SOUP more likely than SOAP; effects radiate (fork, hungry,
diet). Actions and emotions can be primed by events one is not aware of.

Notable demonstrations: **Florida effect** (elderly words → slower walking);
**ideomotor effect** (ideas influence actions, reversible — slow walking speeds
old-age word recognition); pencil-in-mouth smiling increases cartoon funniness;
nodding while listening increases acceptance of editorials.

Real-world priming: school polling stations increase school-funding support; money
primes increase self-reliance and selfishness; **Lady Macbeth effect** (shame →
cleansing products); honesty-box **eyes vs. flowers** poster tripled contributions.

Priming threatens System 2's self-image as sole author of choices. Effects are robust
but not always large — a few percent can tip an election. Disbelief is not an option;
subjective experience is "largely the story System 2 tells itself" while priming arises
in inaccessible System 1.
