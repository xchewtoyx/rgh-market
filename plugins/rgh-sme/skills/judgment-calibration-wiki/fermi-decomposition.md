Commandment **(2) Fermi-ize** (after Enrico Fermi's ballpark-estimate habit):
decompose intractable problems into knowable and unknowable parts; flush
ignorance into the open; expose and examine assumptions; dare to be wrong via
best guesses rather than hiding behind [vague verbiage](sherman-kent-probability-language.md).

Peter Backus's estimate of potential female partners in London: start from
population (~6M), successively narrow by proportion female, single, age range,
education, mutual attraction, compatibility — yielding ~26, a daunting but not
impossible search. Tournament data show good probability estimates often emerge
from crude assumption chains.

Superforecasters Fermi-ize routinely — Arafat autopsy questions, bird flu,
oil prices, Boko Haram, Aleppo, bond spreads. Pair with [inside view vs outside view](inside-view-vs-outside-view.md)
balance and [reference class forecasting](reference-class-forecasting.md).
