title: Categorical Norms and Plausible Ranges
description: >
  Shared category norms specify both typical values and the range of plausible
  variation — letting language communicate efficiently without literal violations.
sources:
  - title: Thinking, Fast and Slow
    resource: "Thinking, Fast and Slow (Kahneman), ch. 6"
---

Shared norms for objects (e.g., "table") and categories (e.g., "mouse," "elephant")
let language communicate efficiently. Norms specify both a **typical/average value**
and the **range of plausible variability** within a category — not just a point
estimate.

"The large mouse climbed over the trunk of the very small elephant" reliably produces
a shared mental image (mouse smaller than a shoe, elephant larger than a sofa) rather
than a literal size-ordering violation — because [System 1](system-1-and-system-2.md),
which understands language, has direct access to categorical norms including their
plausible range. "Large" and "small" are interpreted relative to category norms, not
absolute scales.

(typical exemplars) and category-norm discussion in [single versus joint evaluation](single-vs-joint-evaluation.md).
