Tetlock's composite portrait is a sketch of the **modal** superforecaster — many
paths to the role, and not every attribute matters equally. Something central is
still missing from the individual portrait: other people (group forecasting is
separate).

**Philosophic outlook**: cautious (nothing is certain), humble (reality is
infinitely complex), nondeterministic (what happens is not meant to be).

**Abilities and thinking styles**: actively open-minded (beliefs are hypotheses
to test, not treasures to protect); intelligent and knowledgeable with high
"need for cognition"; reflective and self-critical; numerate.

**Methods**: pragmatic (not wedded to one agenda); analytical (step back from the
tip-of-your-nose view); [dragonfly-eyed](dragonfly-eye-perspective-aggregation.md)
(synthesize diverse views); probabilistic (many grades of maybe, not yes/no);
thoughtful updaters ([granular Bayesian updating](granular-bayesian-updating.md));
good intuitive psychologists (audit own thinking for [System 1](system-1-and-system-2.md)
biases).

**Work ethic**: growth mindset; [grit](perpetual-beta.md) and
[perpetual beta](perpetual-beta.md) commitment.

Illustrative cases: Elizabeth Sloane persisted through illness and used
forecasting to "re-grow synapses"; Anne Kilkenny — no geopolitical background —
queried primary sources directly, stayed skeptical under partisan praise and
condemnation, and kept engaging feedback even when high-confidence forecasts
went badly.

The single strongest predictor of rising into the ranks remains perpetual-beta
dedication to updating — not IQ alone.
