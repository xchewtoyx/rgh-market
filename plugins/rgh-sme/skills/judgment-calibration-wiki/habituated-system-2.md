title: Habituated System 2
description: >
  Deliberate debiasing techniques absorbed into automatic habit through
  practice, lightening cognitive load without eliminating fragility.
sources:
  - title: Superforecasting
    resource: "Superforecasting (Tetlock, Gardner), ch. 11"
---

Continuous System 2 self-scrutiny is exhausting; the feeling of knowing is
seductive. Cognitive illusions resemble the Müller-Lyer lines: knowing they're
equal doesn't switch off seeing them unequal — "we can only monitor answers
that bubble up and, when we have time, use a ruler to check."

Tetlock's hypothesis: some superforecasters practice [System 2](system-1-and-system-2.md)
corrections (outside view, scope checks) until techniques become habitual —
effectively absorbed into System 1. Analogy: a golfer's stance correction is
effortful at first, then automatic with deliberate practice — true of reading,
surgery, flying.

External aids help too (custom news-source software countering like-minded-source
bias). Even so, superforecasting remains hard work; practitioners expect to
stumble. Superforecasters are "always just a System 2 slipup away from a blown
forecast."

What makes superforecasters good is less what they are than what they do —
research, self-criticism, synthesizing perspectives, [granular updating](granular-bayesian-updating.md).
