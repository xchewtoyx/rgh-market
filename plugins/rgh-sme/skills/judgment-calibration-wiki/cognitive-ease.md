[System 1 and System 2](system-1-and-system-2.md) runs continuous automatic
computations: "Is anything new? Is there a threat? Are things going well?" One dial
measures **cognitive ease**, from Easy to Strained. Easy signals no threats, no
need to redirect attention; Strained signals a problem requiring more System 2.

The dial connects an interchangeable network of causes and consequences:

**Causes of ease**: clear font, repeated statements, primed ideas, good mood (pencil
smile trick).

**Causes of strain**: poor font, complicated wording, bad mood, induced frown.

**Consequences of ease**: good mood, liking, believing, trusting intuitions,
familiarity feeling, superficial thinking.

**Consequences of strain**: vigilance, suspicion, more effort, fewer errors, less
intuition and creativity.

## Affect as cause of coherence, not just correlate

Word-triad experiments (coherent vs. incoherent): the intuition of coherence and the
good feeling that accompanies it are causally linked, not merely correlated. When
participants were told (cover story) that background music explained their emotional
reaction, they lost the intuition of coherence entirely — the brief affective response
to a triad (pleasant if coherent, unpleasant otherwise) is the actual basis for judging
coherence. Once the emotional change has an alternative explanation, it is no longer
treated as informative. "There is nothing here that [System 1](system-1-and-system-2.md)
cannot do."

Same mechanism as giving an external explanation for retrieval fluency in
[availability heuristic](availability-heuristic.md) experiments — unexpected fluency
is the cue; explained-away fluency is not.

## Illusions of remembering and truth

Familiarity is inferred from ease, not direct past experience (Jacoby's "Becoming
Famous Overnight": once-seen nonsense names misidentified as celebrities). System 2
uses ease for true/false calls — Kahneman passed a driving-rules test via "if it
feels familiar, probably true." Repetition makes falsehoods believable; partial
phrase repetition suffices ("body temperature of a chicken" → accepting "144°").

Persuasion corollary: legibility, high contrast, rhyme, pronounceable source names
marginally increase belief — but nonsense or known-false claims override these cues;
[lazy System 2 and rationality](lazy-system-2-and-rationality.md) usually adopts
System 1's suggestions.

## Strain mobilizes System 2

CRT in strain-inducing font: 90% error vs. 35% with bad font — strain mobilizes
System 2 to reject intuitive wrong answers.

## Mere exposure and mood

Zajonc's **mere exposure effect**: repeated arbitrary stimuli become mildly liked,
stronger when subliminal — safety signal after harmless repetition. Pronounceable
stock names/tickers get short-term market bonus.

RAT creativity test: good mood more than doubles "intuition index" for sensing
linked word triads before solving; unhappy subjects at chance. Good mood, intuition,
creativity, and gullibility cluster; sadness, vigilance, and analytic effort cluster
— biologically sensible (safe → guard down; threat → vigilance).
