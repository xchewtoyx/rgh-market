---
type: concept
title: Probability Neglect
description: >
  Small risks are either ignored entirely or overweighted with little sensitivity
  to actual probability — the numerator story dominates the denominator.
sources:
  - title: Thinking, Fast and Slow
    resource: "Thinking, Fast and Slow (Kahneman), ch. 13"
---

Paul Slovic: concern is not adequately sensitive to probability of harm — people
imagine the **numerator** (the tragic story) and neglect the **denominator**.
Sunstein coined **probability neglect** for this pattern.

A parent waiting for a late teenage daughter cannot keep disaster images from mind
despite knowing there is little to worry about — nothing proportional between
negligible and serious risk.

Combined with [availability cascades](availability-cascade.md), probability neglect
produces gross exaggeration of minor threats (Alar, Love Canal). Terrorism: weekly
casualties in Israel rarely approached traffic deaths, but media images keep everyone
on edge — difficult to reason into calm even knowing statistics.

Related: [possibility and certainty effects](possibility-and-certainty-effects.md),
[denominator neglect](denominator-neglect.md), [overestimation of unlikely events](overestimation-of-unlikely-events.md),
[prototype vs sum representation](prototype-vs-sum-representation.md).

Forecasting discipline: express risks numerically, compare to base rates, and test
whether probability changes actually move judgments.
