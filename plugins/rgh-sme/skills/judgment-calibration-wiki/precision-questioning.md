Commandment **(9)**: bring out the best in others. Master team-management arts:

- **Perspective taking** — understand the other side's arguments well enough to
  reproduce them to their satisfaction.
- **Precision questioning** — help others clarify arguments so they aren't
  misunderstood.
- **Constructive confrontation** — disagree without being disagreeable.

Given "soccer popularity is declining," bad responses: personal attack ("you're
silly"), flat dismissal, vague "what do you mean?" Good responses zero in: "What
do you mean by 'pastime'?" "What evidence over what time frame?" — expose reasoning
for testing. Applied to Bay of Pigs: "How far to Escambray Mountains?" "Eighty
miles." "Terrain?" "Swamp and jungle." — would likely have surfaced fatal flaw.
See [groupthink in forecasting teams](groupthink-in-forecasting-teams.md).

Tommy Lasorda: "Managing is like holding a dove in your hand. If you hold it
too tightly you kill it, but if you hold it too loosely, you lose it."

Decorrelating errors by collecting independent judgments before discussion
improves aggregation. Connects to [dragonfly eye perspective aggregation](dragonfly-eye-perspective-aggregation.md)
and [granular Bayesian updating](granular-bayesian-updating.md) in team settings.

Contrast [wrong-side-of-maybe fallacy](wrong-side-of-maybe-fallacy.md) public
incentives that push forecasters toward vague language.
