[System 2](system-1-and-system-2.md) has a natural low-effort speed (stroll pace)
where routine monitoring costs little; at extremes, walking and thinking compete —
computing 23×78 while walking makes people stop. **Self-control and deliberate
thought draw on the same limited budget** — see [law of least effort](law-of-least-effort.md).

Not all effort is aversive: Csikszentmihalyi's **flow** — effortless deep concentration
where task focus is intense but attention control requires no self-control exertion.

## Cognitive busyness

Holding digits in memory while choosing cake vs. fruit salad: loaded subjects pick
cake more — System 1's "sweet tooth" gains influence when System 2 is busy. Busy
people also make selfish choices, use sexist language, and superficial social judgments.
Other weakeners: alcohol, sleeplessness, performance anxiety.

## Ego depletion (Baumeister)

Sequential experiments: voluntary effort of any kind (cognitive, emotional, physical)
draws on a **shared pool**. Stifling film emotions → reduced grip strength; resisting
cookies → sooner quit on hard cognitive tasks. Depletion tasks include white-bear
suppression, conflict-laden choices, impressing others, kind responses to partner's
bad behavior.

Differs from simultaneous cognitive load: partly **loss of motivation**, not hard ceiling
— strong incentives can resist depletion, unlike holding six digits in memory.

**Glucose link**: effortful activity draws blood glucose; depletion reversed by glucose
(not Splenda) in attention-control studies.

## Parole judges study

Eight Israeli judges: ~6 min per parole case, 35% overall approval. Approval spiked
to ~65% after food breaks, declining to near zero before next meal — tired, hungry
judges default to denial. High-stakes judgment distorted by depletion.

Implications for calibration work: schedule demanding analytic tasks when System 2
resources are fresh; recognize [lazy System 2](lazy-system-2-and-rationality.md)
isn't only motivational laziness but sometimes depleted capacity. Connect
[cognitive ease](cognitive-ease.md), [habituated System 2](habituated-system-2.md).
