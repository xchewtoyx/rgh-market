A **Learning Review** (or New View Investigation) is an investigative framework that replaces the traditional search for "probable cause" or individual blame with a focus on organizational learning. 

Key characteristics of learning reviews:
* **Inside-the-Tunnel Perspective**: Reconstructs the situation chronologically based on the [local rationality principle](local-rationality-principle.md), showing why choices made sense to operators given the cues available to them in real time.
* **Avoidance of Counterfactuals**: Rejects retrospective warnings (e.g., "they should have known") and evaluates performance without outcome knowledge, avoiding [hindsight and outcome biases](hindsight-and-outcome-biases.md).
* **Explanatory vs. Change Factors**: Distinguishes between factors that explain *why* a specific history occurred (e.g., poor lighting, time pressure) and **change factors**—systemic levers that can be adjusted to prevent future occurrences.
* **Focus on Blunt End**: Investigates distal factors (such as design, staffing budgets, and training checks) rather than focusing solely on the sharp-end operator.

Learning reviews produce high-quality, actionable systemic recommendations that improve [action-item quality](action-item-quality.md), contrasting with traditional [blameless postmortems](blameless-postmortems.md) which often retain a focus on finding a single root cause.
