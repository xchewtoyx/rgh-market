A **CAN report** compresses a status update into three parts: **Conditions**
(current status), **Actions** (what is being done right now), and **Needs**
(what additional people, time, or tasks are required). It is used whenever a
new responder joins mid-incident, whenever the [incident
commander](incident-command-system.md) needs to reset the group's shared
understanding, and whenever briefing executives, vendors, or customers who
weren't present for the preceding discussion.

The format's value is that it's fast to give and fast to absorb under
pressure — a CAN report is the standard unit of communication passed between
an IC and a [liaison officer](liaison-officer-role.md) shuttling information
to another group, and it's the format used at each turn of the [Unified
Command Planning Wheel](unified-command.md) when an incident escalates
beyond a single team. Periodic CAN reports during a long incident also
double as **operational period** checkpoints: a defined cadence (e.g., every
30 minutes) at which the IC reviews progress and asks whether anyone new
needs to be brought onto the channel — see [STAR's Review
step](star-incident-action-plan.md) for where this fits in the overall
response structure.
