---
type: concept
title: Safety Bureaucracy
description: The structural pathologies resulting from compliance-driven safety programs that focus on liability management over operational safety.
sources:
  - title: "The Field Guide to Understanding 'Human Error'"
    resource: "The Field Guide to Understanding 'Human Error' (Sidney Dekker), ch. 6"
---

**Safety Bureaucracy** occurs when safety is isolated in a staff function distant from operations, generating pathologies that erode safety:
* **Liability Management**: The system morphs into proving that management "did something" (rules, reprimands, slogans) to shield the organization from legal liability, rather than supporting operators doing dangerous work.
* **Fantasy Documents**: The creation of complex manuals and risk assessments that bear no resemblance to real operations.
* **Reporting Suppression**: Rate-based safety bonuses and post-incident drug testing penalize injury reporting, creating a climate of fear and suppressing bad news.
* **Structural Secrecy**: The creation of departmental silos that prevent safety signals from crossing organizational boundaries.

Safety bureaucracy widens the gap between [work-as-imagined vs work-as-done](work-as-imagined-vs-work-as-done.md). To prevent it, safety decisions must be driven by operational experts on the line, and safety resources must be maintained independent of economic cycles.
