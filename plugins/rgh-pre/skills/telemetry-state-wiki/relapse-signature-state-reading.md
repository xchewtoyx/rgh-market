  early warning is possible before escalation.

With repeated depressive episodes, depression can gather pace without clear
external triggers. **Relapse signatures** are the individual changes in
mind and body that signal depression developing — unique to each person,
identified in advance while mood is stable.

Example warning signals from one class:

- Negative thoughts/feelings becoming adhesive, hard to dismiss
- Irritability with self and others
- Withdrawal from social participation
- Changing sleep or eating habits
- Getting easily exhausted
- Giving up on exercise
- Not wanting to deal with mail, bills, deadlines

Each person's combination is unique. The balance: become aware of signs
without becoming hypervigilant for them — as mood worsens, hopelessness can
make heeding warnings feel pointless.

Reflection questions when building a signature:

- What has prevented noticing these feelings before (pushing away, denial,
  distraction, self-medication, arguments, blame)?
- Who else can be included in the early-warning system?

Treat adhesive negative thoughts as one possible early signal among bodily
and behavioural changes — see [burnout vs depression state signals](burnout-vs-depression-state-signals.md)
for related discrimination work and [internal barometer](internal-barometer.md)
for moment-to-moment valence reading.
