type: concept
title: Pleasant Experience Awareness Log
description: >
  A daily practice of noticing one pleasant moment and recording its
  thoughts, feelings, and body sensations to tune into valence early and
  deconstruct experience into readable components.
sources:
  - title: "Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy for Depression, 2nd Edition"
    resource: "Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy for Depression, 2nd Edition (Zindel V. Segal, J. Mark G. Williams, John D. Teasdale), ch. 9"
  - title: "The Mindful Way through Depression: Freeing Yourself from Chronic Unhappiness"
    resource: "The Mindful Way through Depression (Mark Williams, John Teasdale, Zindel Segal, Jon Kabat-Zinn), ch. 6"
---

Notice one different pleasant experience each day — ideally while it occurs
— and record as soon as possible afterward: thoughts (as if spoken aloud),
feelings, and body sensations in detail. Example: hearing a bird while
heading home → lightness across face, shoulders dropping, mouth corners
lifting → relief, pleasure → "That's good," "How lovely."

**Why this matters for state reading:**

1. Brings awareness to the earliest valence reaction (pleasant, unpleasant,
   neutral) — a pivotal point before rumination or avoidance begins.
   Much reaction to stimuli is driven by this valence before conscious
   appraisal.
2. Reveals positive moments already present in daily life — the aim is
   not to schedule more pleasant events but to notice existing ones.
3. Deconstructs experience into thoughts, feelings, and sensations rather
   than treating it as one indistinguishable blob — enabling more skillful
   response. Body sensations are recorded because they act as a sensitive
   moment-to-moment barometer of affective state.
4. Offers a nonthreatening entry to the felt dimension for people who have
   developed [experiential avoidance signal blunting](experiential-avoidance-signal-blunting.md).

Pair with the [unpleasant experience awareness log](unpleasant-experience-awareness-log.md)
(same structure, recording reactions to unpleasantness including aversion) and with
[internal barometer](internal-barometer.md) tuning. See also
[emotion tracking journal](emotion-tracking-journal.md) for longer-window
pattern building.
