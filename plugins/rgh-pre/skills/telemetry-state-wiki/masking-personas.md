  presentation a poor guide to internal state.

[Masking](masking-blunts-signal.md) often follows a repeatable pattern: a
trait feared as unacceptable is suppressed, a compensating persona is
performed instead, and specific behaviours maintain the performance.

Common pairings include:

- Arrogant → humble: pretending not to know answers, staying silent when
  others are wrong, softening statements with "if that makes any sense."
- Cold and unfeeling → warm and friendly: smiling regardless of actual
  feeling, asking about others' feelings while not discussing one's own.
- Annoying and loud → agreeable and quiet: only big emotions in private,
  suppressing excitement about good things.
- Childish → mature: acting as confidant to adults, restrained "proper"
  carriage, distancing from peers.
- Awkward → cool: withdrawing from activities not instantly mastered,
  performing aloofness, mentally rehearsing conversations.
- Clueless, pathetic → independent: nodding without understanding,
  private life hacks, keeping life looking "put together" on paper.
- Sensitive → strong: not voicing needs, shame about crying or anger,
  internally fighting "disruptive" emotions.
- Weak → tough: aggression or superiority displays, distaste for anything
  coded feminine or soft.
- Weird → normal: studying what others like, imitating mannerisms and tone,
  mocking those more obviously "different."

The worksheet framing asks what the mask protects against — often shame
labels such as selfish, robotic, awkward, immature, disgusting, cold,
clueless, stupid, cruel, weak, pathetic. The most painful label often
predicts which persona is performed and which internal states get hidden.

For telemetry, the outward persona is **not** the state reading. A warm,
smiling presentation may sit on top of cold or sensitive internal experience;
an independent, put-together surface may sit on confusion or overwhelm.
Calibrating confidence requires knowing which persona is active and treating
performance as a separate channel from [self-attunement](self-attunement.md).
