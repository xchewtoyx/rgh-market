---
type: concept
title: Sensory Under-Responsivity
description: >
  When a sensory stimulus registers too faintly or too late, producing
  stoicism, accidents, or apparent indifference that can make a state
  reading look "stable" when internal needs are unmet.
sources:
  - title: "Is This Autism? A Guide for Clinicians and Everyone Else"
    resource: "Is This Autism? A Guide for Clinicians and Everyone Else (Donna Henderson, Sarah Wayland, Jamell White), ch. 8"
---

Under-responsivity means little to no response to a stimulus, external or
internal. The signal channel is weak, so needs and discomfort may not
surface until they become urgent or someone else notices.

Examples that distort state reading:

- Unusually high pain tolerance or stoicism — injury or illness may not
  register as a problem until it is severe.
- Apparent indifference to temperature extremes.
- Low awareness of toileting needs leading to accidents.
- Low awareness of hunger or satiety — endless eating, never feeling hungry,
  or needing external reminders to eat.
- Low tactile sensitivity — chronically unkempt appearance from not feeling
  messy hair or twisted clothing.
- Low auditory sensitivity — not hearing an approaching car or one's own
  name.
- Poor postural control from low vestibular or proprioceptive registration.
- Insensitivity to visual motion, color differentiation, or distance judgment.
- "Tripping on air" from low vestibular or proprioceptive sensitivity.
- Obliviousness to height or moving-equipment risk; not becoming dizzy despite
  excessive spinning.
- Low [interoceptive](interoception.md) awareness of emotion-linked
  physiological states — e.g. not noticing a rapid heart rate tied to anxiety.

A reading of "nothing is wrong" can be accurate for conscious experience
while homeostatic or emotional needs are already out of band. Pair outward
observation and scheduled [micro interoceptive check-ins](micro-interoceptive-check-ins.md)
with self-report when under-responsivity is part of the profile. See
[sensory profile mapping](sensory-profile-mapping.md) for building a
personal baseline of which channels are likely to under-register.
