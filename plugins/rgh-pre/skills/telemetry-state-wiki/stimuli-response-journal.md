---
type: concept
title: Stimuli-Response Journal
description: >
  A structured log of emotional and physical stimuli paired with stress
  responses over time — scientific-style data collection to reveal which
  inputs push toward burnout, inertia, meltdown, or shutdown.
sources:
  - title: "The Unmasking Workbook for Autistic Adults"
    resource: "The Unmasking Workbook for Autistic Adults (Jessica Penot), ch. 6"
---

Columns per entry:

- **Stimuli** — loud noises, social interaction, work hours, insults, etc.
- **Date / example** — when it occurred.
- **Response** — e.g. "significant fatigue and irritability," "masking led
  to decreased mood," "exhausted; noticed autistic inertia when changing
  tasks."

Goal: identify which stimuli are most exhausting and which
[four autistic stress state signature](four-autistic-stress-state-signatures.md)
you are most prone to enter. Patterns emerge over weeks — e.g. daily hug
therapeutic; more work hours move toward burnout; fatigue, pain, and
irritability accumulating signal need for proactive mood-support activities
before meltdown.

Pair with mood/environment trackers (apps or day charts logging state, what
happened, other factors) and [emotion tracking journal](emotion-tracking-journal.md)
for longer-window emotion patterns. Observation phase should stay
non-judgmental — like [habits scorecard](habits-scorecard.md), notice first,
intervene after patterns are visible.

Especially valuable when [alexithymia](alexithymia.md) delays in-the-moment
reading — external logging catches drift the inner channel misses.
