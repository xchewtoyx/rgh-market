title: Four Corners of Awareness
description: >
  A scanning checklist of four distinct channels to check when reading
  current state — the outside world, the inside of the body, thoughts and
  emotions, and a sense of connection to something larger.
sources:
  - title: "Solve for Happy"
    resource: "Solve for Happy (Mo Gawdat), ch. 10"
---

A useful checklist for what to actually scan when checking in on current
state, since attention defaults to only one or two of these channels
unless deliberately widened:

1. **The outside world** — sensory input: sight, sound, smell, taste,
   touch. See [sensory profile mapping](sensory-profile-mapping.md).
2. **Inside the body** — internal sensations, breath, heartbeat, pain. See
   [interoception](interoception.md) and [body scanning](body-scan.md).
3. **Thoughts and emotions** — the internal dialogue and emotional state
   running underneath. See [the emotional storm](emotional-storm.md) and
   [affect labeling](affect-labeling.md).
4. **Connection to something larger** — a non-sensory, non-cognitive sense
   of belonging to something beyond the immediate self (e.g. a felt
   sympathy for a stranger's situation, or love for a place). Described as
   the least concrete of the four, and the one most often skipped
   entirely during an ordinary state check.

Checking all four in turn, rather than defaulting to whichever is loudest
or most habitual, reduces the chance that a state reading is skewed by
only sampling one channel — for example reading only the outside world
(what's happening) while missing an internal emotional signal running
alongside it, or the reverse.
