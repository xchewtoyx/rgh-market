Lisa Feldman Barrett's **body budget** framing: the brain regulates the
body, but a physically bankrupt body prevents the brain from doing its job
well. Bad decisions, lack of resilience, distraction, fear, and extreme
emotion trace back to the body.

The addiction-recovery acronym **HALT** — Hungry, Angry, Lonely, Tired —
functions as a quick rubric for relapse triggers and, more broadly, for
**state-reading confidence**. Before trusting a mood reading or a reactive
impulse, screen:

- **Hungry** — unmet food need, often missed with [delayed interoception](interoception.md).
- **Angry** — unresolved arousal or conflict load.
- **Lonely** — social depletion or isolation.
- **Tired** — sleep debt or [social calorie](social-calories.md) depletion.

"The body keeps score" — daily physical decisions accumulate in how you feel
and function. Temperance and basic body care are not separate from mental
clarity; physical intemperance prevents the mind from working properly.

Use HALT as a low-effort gate before complex interpretation: if any letter
is active, address the body channel first, then re-read state. Pair with
[establishing a calm baseline](calm-baseline-state.md) to know what rested
non-HALT state feels like for comparison.
