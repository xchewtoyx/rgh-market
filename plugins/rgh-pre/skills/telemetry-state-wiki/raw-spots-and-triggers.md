  emotional reaction beyond what the present-moment trigger alone would
  produce, which matters for judging how much of a reading is genuinely
  about the present situation.

A "raw spot" is a sensitive area or vulnerability left by a past wound that,
when activated, compounds a present-moment stressor with historical pain —
the emotional reading in the moment is real, but its intensity is not
purely a function of the current trigger. This is a specific case of noise
overlaid on signal: the underlying event may be minor, but the reaction is
amplified by an old, deeply ingrained narrative about self, others, or the
world that the current moment happens to touch.

**Identification method.** Reflect on past situations that produced a
strong reaction; document the context, the reaction itself, and the
thoughts present at the time; look for recurring patterns across
situations. Notice bodily and mental reactions to potential triggers as
they occur (see [body scanning](body-scan.md) and
[the physical signature of an emotion](emotional-physical-signature.md)).
Once a pattern is visible, try to identify the underlying story the raw
spot is tied to.

Recognising an activated raw spot in the moment — for example naming it
explicitly as "my abandonment fear is activated right now" — is itself a
calibration step: it flags that the current reading is likely inflated
relative to the present trigger alone, which is useful information even
before any response is decided.
