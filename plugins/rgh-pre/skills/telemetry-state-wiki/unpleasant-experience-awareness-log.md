type: concept
title: Unpleasant Experience Awareness Log
description: >
  A daily practice of noticing one unpleasant moment and recording thoughts,
  feelings, body sensations, and aversion reactions to tune early warning
  and deconstruct experience into readable components.
sources:
  - title: "Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy for Depression, 2nd Edition"
    resource: "Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy for Depression, 2nd Edition (Zindel V. Segal, J. Mark G. Williams, John D. Teasdale), ch. 11"
---

Notice one different unpleasant experience each day — ideally while it
occurs — and record soon afterward: thoughts (as if spoken aloud),
feelings, body sensations, and reactions of aversion. Many people find
this easier than the pleasant calendar and notice more unpleasant moments
per day — which is data about attention bias, not proof life is mostly bad.

**Why this matters for state reading:**

1. Surfaces [feeling tone](internal-barometer.md) and
   [attachment and aversion reactions](attachment-and-aversion-reactions.md)
   at the pivotal point before rumination deepens.
2. Identifies personal patterns of bodily contraction associated with
   aversion for future early alerting — see
   [aversion bodily signature](aversion-bodily-signature.md).
3. Reveals vicious cycles of getting upset about negative thoughts/feelings
   ([second arrow suffering](second-arrow-suffering.md)).
4. Shows events may not be inherently positive or negative — often the
   **mood brought to the event** colors it; see
   [mood-colored interpretation](mood-colored-interpretation.md).

Pair with [pleasant experience awareness log](pleasant-experience-awareness-log.md)
and [emotion tracking journal](emotion-tracking-journal.md) for longer-
window pattern building.
