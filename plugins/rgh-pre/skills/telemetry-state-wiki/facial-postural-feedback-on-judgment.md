type: concept
title: Facial and Postural Feedback on Judgment
description: >
  Activating smiling, frowning, or head-movement muscles unconsciously
  shifts emotional judgments — evidence that bodily configuration feeds
  back into felt state readings the person did not choose.
sources:
  - title: "The Mindful Way through Depression: Freeing Yourself from Chronic Unhappiness"
    resource: "The Mindful Way through Depression (Mark Williams, John Teasdale, Zindel Segal, Jon Kabat-Zinn), ch. 1"
---

Bodily posture and expression are not only outputs of emotion — they can
**input** into it. Experimental manipulations while people rated cartoons or
judged information:

- Holding a pencil in teeth (activates smiling muscles) vs. pursed lips
  (blocks smiling) changed how funny cartoons seemed.
- Activating frowning muscles made the same cartoons seem much less funny.
- Nodding vs. shaking the head while hearing information shifted judgments
  about that information.

Participants were unaware of the physical influence on their judgments.

For telemetry confidence:

- A felt judgment ("this is terrible," "I agree with that") may partly
  reflect current muscle tension, facial set, or posture — not only the
  content being judged.
- Unnoticed bracing (white-knuckle grip, hunched shoulders) can color
  social and self-evaluative readings before the mind names an emotion.
- Pair bodily configuration checks with
  [embodied signals precede conscious reading](embodied-signals-precede-conscious-reading.md)
  and [emotional physical signature](emotional-physical-signature.md).

See [experiential avoidance signal blunting](experiential-avoidance-signal-blunting.md)
when chronic tension is avoided rather than read, and
[internal barometer](internal-barometer.md) for tuning trunk-area contraction
that often accompanies negative judgment states.
