title: Proprioception
description: >
  The sense of body position and pressure in space, via muscle and joint
  receptors, whose over- or under-responsiveness shapes what a person's
  body is actually signalling about touch and pressure.

Proprioception is a "hidden" sense alongside [interoception](interoception.md)
and [the vestibular sense](vestibular-sense.md), read as part of
[sensory profile mapping](sensory-profile-mapping.md). Two response
patterns show up:

- **Under-responders** crave pressure, weight, constrictive clothing, and
  intense movement — a signal that the input currently being received
  reads as too faint rather than too strong.
- **Avoiders** (over-responders) are sensitive to light touch and may
  appear to overreact to accidental contact — the same input that an
  under-responder wouldn't notice registers here as genuinely intrusive.

Recognising which pattern is in play for a given person changes how their
reaction to touch or pressure should be read: an under-responder seeking
more pressure and an avoider flinching from light touch are both accurate
reports of what their proprioceptive system is telling them, not
over- or under-reaction to be second-guessed.
