Underneath the many dimensions of feeling, the mind maintains a single scale
registering experience as pleasant, neutral, or unpleasant — an **internal
barometer** giving a continuous readout of the internal atmosphere. As with
a real barometer, reading it requires practice and attention.

Attending to the chain of reactions to any object, person, place, or event
reveals an instinctive sense of the experience as pleasant, unpleasant, or
neutral. Pleasant registration tends toward wanting to prolong; unpleasant
toward wanting escape. Mostly this runs automatically, below awareness.
Practicing awareness of the chain creates choice points between the basic
gut feeling and automatic downstream reactions — especially **aversion**.

Because unpleasantness underlies all negative emotions (sadness, anger,
disgust, anxiety), tuning the internal barometer provides a generic
early-warning system for uncomfortable emotions as they emerge into
consciousness. Recognising previously screened-out unpleasant feelings as
they arise weakens their influence.

The most reliable way to tune this early-warning system is through the body
— see [body scan](body-scan.md) and
[embodied signals precede conscious reading](embodied-signals-precede-conscious-reading.md).
At first, unpleasant perception and aversion may feel fused; awareness can
be built stepwise: first recognising the fused ensemble as overall bodily
contraction; then detecting unpleasant quality before aversion triggers;
eventually distinguishing unpleasantness (first) from the "get me out of
here" aversion reaction (second). Common bodily expressions of aversion:
contraction in shoulders or lower back, forehead tightening, jaw clenching,
belly tightening — fight-or-flight whether escaping a tiger or one's own
feelings. When the "tiger is inside" and has lingered, these reactions are
often stopped noticing — see [experiential avoidance signal blunting](experiential-avoidance-signal-blunting.md).

**Body Barometer (everyday practice).** Choose a trunk-area body region
(chest, abdomen, or between) especially sensitive to stress for you; place
a hand there if helpful. This becomes your personal barometer. Tuning in
regularly reveals tension, tightness, shakiness, or discomfort varying
with difficulty level — including subtle sensations signaling something
emerging long before the mind notices. Set a daily reminder and practice
for a couple of weeks to make it a readily available resource.

**Breathing into / breathing with.** When intense sensation draws attention,
**breathing into** imagines the breath carrying awareness into the core of
the region — an antidote to experiential avoidance. If overwhelming,
**breathing with** holds awareness of the intensity together with whole-
body breath in the background, like music with a steady beat underneath.
Pair with [pleasant experience awareness log](pleasant-experience-awareness-log.md)
for deliberate valence noticing in daily life.
