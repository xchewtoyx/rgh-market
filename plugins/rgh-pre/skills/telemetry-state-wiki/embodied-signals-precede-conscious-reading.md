title: Embodied Signals Precede Conscious Reading
description: >
  Faster unconscious channels — embodied, emotional, and intuitive — register
  environmental and internal state in milliseconds, while deliberate verbal
  self-report runs hundreds of times slower, so a conscious "how do I feel?"
  answer often lags behind what the body already knows.

State is not assessed through one channel at one speed. Praslova, building on
Kahneman's System 1/System 2 framework, describes four processing layers with
very different latencies:

- **Embodied processing** — reactions to temperature, texture, smell, and
  other physical qualities of the environment; produces goosebumps, tension,
  and other somatic responses. Speed: **under 5 ms**.
- **Emotional processing** — subcortical alerting to potential benefit or
  danger. Speed: **under 10 ms**.
- **Connectionist / intuitive processing** — learned associations between
  environmental cues (layout, accessibility, tone). Speed: **200–500 ms**.
- **Symbolic / deliberate processing** — conscious, language-based
  reasoning about what is happening. Speed: **500 ms to 10 seconds**.

The faster channels shape judgments and felt safety long before the slow,
verbal channel catches up. A person may sense exclusion, discomfort, or
threat from an environment's sensory qualities — blaring music, harsh
lighting, inaccessible layout — while still consciously parsing an
inclusion statement's wording.

For telemetry confidence:

- A conscious reading taken only through the symbolic channel ("I think I'm
  fine; the policy says I'm welcome") may **contradict** what faster channels
  already registered.
- Retroactive surprise — "my body reacted before my mind knew why" — is a
  calibration clue that the lag between channels matters, not evidence the
  body was wrong.
- Mixed signals (inclusive words + exclusionary sensory environment) do not
  cancel; the fast channels tend to win the felt reading.

Pair slow verbal check-ins with [micro interoceptive check-ins](micro-interoceptive-check-ins.md)
and attention to [emotional physical signatures](emotional-physical-signature.md)
to catch what symbolic processing missed. See
[heuristic substitution in state reading](heuristic-substitution-in-state-reading.md)
for another way the slow channel can answer the wrong question, and
[delayed emotional processing](delayed-emotional-processing.md) when the
lag is hours rather than milliseconds.

**Direct sensing vs thinking about the body.** In doing mode, we view the
body from a "citadel in the head" — labeling, judging, planning to fix
("there's a pain there, I must do something"). Direct sensing brings
awareness into the body itself: patterns of pressure, warmth, tingling,
numbness that may not feel "hand-shaped." This turns up the volume on
bodily messages and turns down mental chatter. Emotions start as sensory
signals before being named and evaluated; overthinking skips the early
sensory step and jumps to figuring out what to do. Morning heaviness fed
by negative thoughts about sluggishness strengthens the felt heaviness —
a self-reinforcing loop breakable by [body scan](body-scan.md) attention
to sensation as it is, without trying to change it.
