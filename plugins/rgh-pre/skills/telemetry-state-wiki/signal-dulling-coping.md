title: Signal-Dulling Coping
description: >
  Coping strategies used to maintain a mask — substance use, eating-disorder
  behaviour, dissociation, rigid rules, compulsive people-pleasing — that
  deliberately blunt interoceptive and emotional signal.
sources:
  - title: "Unmasking Autism Audiobook Companion"
    resource: "Unmasking Autism Audiobook Companion (Devon Price, PhD), Masking Mechanics worksheets"
---

Some coping strategies prop up [masking personas](masking-personas.md) by
reducing what reaches conscious awareness:

- **Problem drinking or substance use** — dulls sensory sensitivities;
  provides "liquid courage" for social situations; relaxes inhibitions;
  stimulates or entertains an anxious mind; silences the inner critic.
- **Eating-disordered behaviour** — grounds life in rituals; provides
  physical stimulation via hunger or exercise; distracts from social
  struggles by focusing on the body; defines worth through appearance or
  control; can explain away gender dysphoria or physical dissociation.
- **Detachment and dissociation** — prevents rejection by disengaging first;
  blunts grief, sorrow, regret; removes pressure to learn emotional skills;
  silences needs others find bothersome; preserves limited energy.
- **Rigid rules and belief systems** — makes reality feel concrete; translates
  vague norms into specific expectations; provides structure and soothing
  ritual; soothes self-doubt and fear of being a "bad person."
- **Fawning and compulsive people-pleasing** — earns praise; simplifies
  relationships to "always say yes"; validates ignoring one's own feelings;
  minimizes conflict.

When a state reading is persistently flat, numb, or "fine" despite context
that should register, check whether signal-dulling coping is active. The
reading may be **accurate for what consciousness can access** while still
misleading about underlying load — similar to [freeze or fawn stress
signatures](stress-response-signals.md) and [masking blunts signal](masking-blunts-signal.md).

Recovery of readable signal often requires reducing the dulling strategy
(not always immediately possible) and rebuilding [eroded perception
trust](eroded-perception-trust.md) through low-pressure [micro interoceptive
check-ins](micro-interoceptive-check-ins.md).
