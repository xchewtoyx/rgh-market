  swaying, riding in a car) reads as very different signals in different people.

The vestibular sense is a "hidden" sense alongside
[interoception](interoception.md) and [proprioception](proprioception.md),
read as part of [sensory profile mapping](sensory-profile-mapping.md). Two
response patterns show up:

- **Under-responders** actively seek swinging, rocking, bouncing, or
  jumping — the movement signal they're getting reads as too weak to
  satisfy on its own.
- **Over-responders** get motion sickness easily from cars, elevators,
  escalators, or stairs — ordinary movement input reads as destabilising
  rather than neutral.

As with proprioception, a strong reaction to movement (seeking it out or
avoiding it) is an accurate report of what the vestibular system is
signalling, not a sign of over-reaction to be dismissed.
