title: Social Calories
description: >
  A limited pool of social energy that depletes faster under sensory
  distraction and high-pressure interaction, requiring recovery time alone
  before state readings stabilize.
sources:
  - title: "Knowing Why: Adult-Diagnosed Autistic People on Life and Autism"

Stuart Neilson's **social calories** model: a limited supply of social energy
spent gradually in relaxed activity or burned rapidly under high-pressure
settings, after which recovery time alone is needed. Sensory distraction
competing with social interaction for attention accelerates the spend.

Especially costly activities include breezy gossip, small talk, social
competition, workplace joshing, and reading unspoken subtext. Symptoms may
not appear until social demands exceed limited capacities — the goal becomes
finding settings where social reward exceeds the energy and pain cost of
engaging.

For state reading after social exposure:

- Irritability, shutdown, or flat mood may reflect **depleted social
  calories**, not personality or attitude.
- The same sensory input can be pleasant alone but intolerable layered onto
  social demand — see [context-dependent sensory reading](context-dependent-sensory-reading.md).

Track recovery time after high-spend contexts when calibrating whether a
post-social reading is stable or still in depletion.
