  Metaphorical somatic loci — heart, solar plexus, throat — where attention
  surfaces constriction or openness tied to grief, vitality, or vocalized
  emotion, useful when state is felt regionally rather than as a named mood.

Lying-down [body scan](body-scan.md) can access an **emotional body** —
metaphorical as well as physical. Attention to regions may surface emotions
not yet verbally labeled:

- **Heart region** — constriction, tightness, heaviness alongside grief,
  sadness, loneliness, despair, unworthiness, anger; also love, joy,
  compassion when open.
- **Solar plexus** — sunlike, radiant quality; centeredness (center of
  gravity) and vitality (digestive "fires").
- **Throat** — vocalizes emotion; can be constricted or open. Feelings may
  get "stuck in the throat" even when the heart is open. Mindfulness here
  reveals tonal qualities of speech — explosiveness, speed, harshness,
  volume vs. softness — as well as content.

Practice: tune breath while lying down, moving through feet, legs, pelvis,
belly, chest, back, shoulders, arms, throat, head — allowing flux and
change. Give problematic regions attention to invite them "back into
membership and wholeness" with the rest of the body. **Honor gut feelings**
as part of state reading, not only head-based labels.

See [emotional physical signature](emotional-physical-signature.md),
[interoception](interoception.md), and [affect labeling](affect-labeling.md)
when regional sensation precedes naming.
