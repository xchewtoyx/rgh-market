title: Vague Instructions Amplify Cognitive Demand
description: >
  Imprecise requests force extra computation to infer what, when, and why
  before work can start — a hidden attention cost that literal thinkers
  and anyone under load pay disproportionately.
sources:
  - title: "Unmasked: The Ultimate Guide to ADHD, Autism and Neurodivergence for Women"
    resource: "Unmasked (Middleton), Chapter 3 (Literal Thinking and Core Trait Translations)"
  - title: "Knowing Why: Adult-Diagnosed Autistic People on Life and Autism"
    resource: "Knowing Why (ASAN), Chapter 5 (Kelly Bron Johnson — Working While Autistic)"
---

A request like "read through this document and we'll touch base" leaves
unanswered what format of output is expected, what "touch base" means, and
when the work is due relative to other priorities. For someone who processes
language literally and does not automatically infer unstated constraints,
each gap becomes a separate problem to solve before the substantive task
begins — taxing [working memory as a cognitive capacity
buffer](working-memory-as-cognitive-capacity-buffer.md) and the [limited
attention budget](limited-attention-budget.md) before any real work starts.

Middleton's **What by When and Why** rewrite is a capacity-saving
structure, not mere politeness: (1) What exactly is needed? (2) When is
each piece due? (3) Why does it matter? Answering those upfront removes
the inference loop and lets attention go to the task itself. The same
principle appears in [designing around capacity
limits](designing-around-capacity-limits.md)'s externalization move —
make requirements salient in the environment rather than asking the person
to hold and decode them internally.

Literal interpretation also affects commitment: answering "can you help
with this project?" on capability alone, without weighing implied limits
(capacity, competing priorities, hours, comfort), can produce
over-commitment and later burnout because the unstated qualifiers were
never part of the parsed request. For managers and collaborators, precise
briefs are an accommodation that reduces load; for the person planning their
own week, explicitly stating their own what/when/why limits before agreeing
is the mirror-image pacing move.

Verbal strings of commands can sound understood in the moment yet be
forgotten at the desk — asking for a repeat risks being read as
incompetence. Requesting directions in writing (notes or email) offloads
recall from [working memory](working-memory-as-cognitive-capacity-buffer.md)
and removes the inference-and-remember loop; post-diagnosis this is
framed as a processing difference, not a performance failure.
