Recovery is not one undifferentiated process any more than
[capacity is one undifferentiated pool](dimensions-of-personal-capacity.md).
The source describes a deliberate "sensory detox" ritual — a sequence used
specifically after high-sensory days to rebalance the sensory dimension —
distinct from generic downtime. The ritual's content is individual (some
people need stillness and deep pressure, others an energetic walk with
music), but the principle is the match itself: recovery aimed at the
dimension that actually ran hot restores headroom faster than an
undifferentiated "rest" input aimed at no dimension in particular.

This complements [cross-dimensional saturation
cascade](cross-dimensional-saturation-cascade.md): if strain in one
dimension can leak into others, recovery aimed squarely at the originating
dimension is a more direct way to arrest that leak than trying to recover
"in general." Diagnosing which dimension actually ran hot is a
current-state read, not a modelling question — the recovery match itself
is what belongs here.

For some people, **deep focus on a special interest** — computer graphics,
gaming, building, reading — functions as the restorative match where
generic meditation fails: sensory input dims to a comfortable backdrop,
brain fog lifts, and a wordless sense of self returns. The activity is
individual; the principle is that absorption in a preferred domain can
restore cognitive capacity when undifferentiated "rest" does not. This is
[dimension-matched recovery](dimension-matched-recovery.md) aimed at the
cognitive/sensory stack, not a universal prescription — loud music with
singing along is restorative for some autistic people and overload for
others (see [net capacity effect depends on activity
fit](net-capacity-effect-depends-on-activity-fit.md)).

**Reload** via resurrected interest: when motivation is near zero, the
person may be dopamine-deficient rather than lazy. Filling downtime with
an interest thread — pottery classes plus reading about pottery, or
sourcing materials for a woodworking side project — can restore cognitive
and emotional headroom when willpower alone does not. The activity is
individual; the match is to something absorbing enough to pull attention
in a restorative direction, as in the [maintain-recover-reload
cycle](maintain-recover-reload-cycle.md).
