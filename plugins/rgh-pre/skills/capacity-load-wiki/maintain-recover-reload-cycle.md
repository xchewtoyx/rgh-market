type: concept
title: Maintain-Recover-Reload Cycle
description: >
  A three-part pacing cycle — fund baseline capacity, shed guilt and
  excess demand, then restore motivation through interest-matched input —
  coordinates prevention, demand reduction, and replenishment instead of
  treating any one as sufficient alone.
sources:
  - title: "The Autistic's Guide to Self-Discovery"
    resource: "The Autistic's Guide to Self-Discovery (Smith), Chapter 8 (Beating Burnout — Maintain, Recover, Reload)"
---

Beating overload is framed as three distinct moves, not one box to check:

**Maintain** — [self-maintenance as preventive capacity
work](self-maintenance-as-preventive-capacity-work.md): baseline routines
around movement, food, and sleep that keep the physical substrate funded.
Sleep is often the highest-leverage maintain input because it enables
good decisions about the other two (see [sleep debt compounds across
dimensions](sleep-debt-compounds-across-dimensions.md)). Start from
current baseline; small, sustainable steps beat an all-at-once overhaul
that burns out on the change itself.

**Recover** — take a break from guilt around productivity and reduce
demand where possible. Work-from-home erosion of work/home boundaries
can leave relaxation feeling like slacking even during off hours;
[scheduled work boundaries for guilt-free
rest](scheduled-work-boundaries-for-guilt-free-rest.md) and [delegation
as capacity multiplier](delegation-as-capacity-multiplier.md) address
that side. Shed duties, outsource, or lower standards to "sanitary"
before crisis forces a total stop.

**Reload** — when motivation is flat, the system may need interest-based
input, not more willpower. Resurrecting or discovering an absorbing
interest (pottery, woodworking, a hobby thread to follow mentally through
the day) can restore the cognitive/emotional dimension when generic rest
does not — see [dimension-matched recovery](dimension-matched-recovery.md).
Chosen social contact around shared interests can reload the social
dimension for people who mistake low energy for low motivation (see
[social energy as spendable capacity](social-energy-as-spendable-capacity.md)).

Each step requires real environmental and interpersonal change, not
tick-box compliance. Together they aim to rebuild from a place of control
rather than only after rock-bottom loss of capacity.
