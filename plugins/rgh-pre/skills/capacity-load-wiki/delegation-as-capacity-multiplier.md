---
type: concept
title: Delegation as Capacity Multiplier
description: >
  Handing off tasks others can do better or cheaper frees attention for
  work only you can do — refusing to delegate is often a capacity problem
  disguised as virtue or control.
sources:
  - title: "Discipline Is Destiny: The Power of Self-Control"
    resource: "Discipline Is Destiny (Holiday), Chapter 12 (Share the Load)"
  - title: "The Autistic's Guide to Self-Discovery"
    resource: "The Autistic's Guide to Self-Discovery (Smith), Chapter 8 (Beating Burnout — Maintain, Recover, Reload)"
---

High performers often treat doing everything themselves as discipline, but
Holiday frames the opposite: it takes discipline *not* to insist on owning
every task when standards, enjoyment, guilt about spending money, or fear
of judgment make delegation feel wrong. The outcome is the same — wearing
down the person and the cause — while forfeiting comparative advantage:
someone else may have a better shot at parts of the load, and protecting
your hours buys the reflective space leaders need (King wanted uninterrupted
time to meditate and read, not more mechanics).

Practical moves: identify legacy tasks that can be automated or reassigned;
know what an hour of your time is worth; build systems bigger than yourself;
where affordable, hire a buffer who can say "no" on your behalf — "our
willpower is not enough" and gutting through every demand is not a pacing
plan. This pairs with [precommitted demand-shedding](precommitted-demand-shedding.md)
(dropping greens when headroom is low) and [designing around capacity
limits](designing-around-capacity-limits.md): delegation removes demand
before saturation, not only after.

Privilege limits who can hire staff, but the capacity principle applies at
any scale — passing the ball when a teammate has the better shot is the
same structural idea as paying for household help so energy goes to work
only you can do.

In the **recover** phase of the [maintain-recover-reload
cycle](maintain-recover-reload-cycle.md), "dump off duties" means letting
house cleanliness slide to sanitary, paying for cleaning when affordable,
or asking family and friends for concrete help (walk the dog, sit with you
while you grade) before crisis forces a total stop. Asking is a capacity
move, not weakness — it removes demand from the schedule rather than
waiting until headroom is already gone.
