---
type: concept
title: Environmental Trial Run Before Novel Demands
description: >
  Previewing a new place or route before full exposure converts abstract
  unpredictability into processed detail, lowering the spike load of
  first-time environments.
sources:
  - title: "How to Be You: Simple Ways to Love Who You Are"
    resource: "How to Be You (Middleton), Chapter 5 (Flexibility — Tip 25)"
---

Novel environments stack sensory processing, routine change, navigation
uncertainty, and emotional regulation without the benefit of prior
prediction. For detail-oriented nervous systems, an abstract mental image
of "the supermarket" or "the hospital" may be impossible to construct —
full immersion is the first pass, at maximum load.

Neurodivergent Lou's **trial run** via Google Earth Street View walks the
route and destination virtually first: what the building looks like, where
crowds or sensory hotspots may appear, which alternative path or headphones
plan makes sense. The same information will be processed on arrival; doing
it on your own timeline beforehand spreads demand across time and reduces
anxiety-driven [cross-dimensional saturation
cascade](cross-dimensional-saturation-cascade.md) at the door.

This is preparation load spent deliberately to avoid a larger spike later
— analogous to pre-interaction planning in [camouflaging extends demand
beyond the interaction](camouflaging-extends-demand-beyond-interaction.md),
but aimed at place rather than social performance. See [designing around
capacity limits](designing-around-capacity-limits.md) for building
predictability into commitments that would otherwise be one-shot surprises.
