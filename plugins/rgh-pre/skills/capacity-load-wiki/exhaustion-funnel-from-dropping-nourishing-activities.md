title: Exhaustion Funnel From Dropping Nourishing Activities
description: >
  Under sustained load, people often shed optional nourishing activities
  first while keeping obligatory stressors — narrowing life until only
  depleting demands remain and capacity spirals down further.
sources:
  - title: "Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy for Depression, 2nd Edition"
    resource: "Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy for Depression, 2nd Edition (Segal, Williams, Teasdale), Chapter 16 (Session 7 — Taking Care of Yourself)"
---

The **exhaustion funnel** describes progressive narrowing: as energy falls,
activities that feel optional — hobbies, social pleasure, rest that does
not look "productive" — get cut first, while work and obligations stay.
The remaining schedule is mostly **D** (depleting) entries with few **N**
(nourishing) ones (see [nourishing versus depleting activity
balance](nourishing-versus-depleting-activity-balance.md)), which
accelerates the next drop in headroom.

Conscientious workers whose self-worth tracks performance are especially
prone — the "best" employees, not the lazy ones, may keep shedding
recovery and pleasure while meeting external demands until symptoms
accumulate. The pattern looks like dedication but is a pacing failure:
recovery inputs are treated as negotiable while stressors are not.

Reversing the funnel means deliberately protecting small nourishing and
mastery steps before obligations expand to fill all slack — see
[proactive recovery scheduling](proactive-recovery-scheduling.md) and
[sustainable effort margin](sustainable-effort-margin.md). Micro-spaces
within an already-busy day (a breathing pause while waiting on hold, slow
walk with a trolley) can count as nourishing without requiring a cleared
calendar.
