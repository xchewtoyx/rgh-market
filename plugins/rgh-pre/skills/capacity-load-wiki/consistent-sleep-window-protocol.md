---
type: concept
title: Consistent Sleep Window Protocol
description: >
  Holding a fixed wake time and a deliberately bounded sleep window —
  then expanding bedtime only after sleep quality improves — rebuilds
  sleep as a reliable capacity-restoration channel faster than varying
  schedule by how tired you feel each night.
sources:
  - title: "CBT For Dummies, 4th Edition"
    resource: "CBT For Dummies, 4th Edition (Branch, Willson), Chapter 15 (Sleep Hygiene)"
---

Sleep debt degrades next-day [dimensions of personal
capacity](dimensions-of-personal-capacity.md); rebuilding sleep is a
capacity intervention, not a luxury. A practical protocol:

1. Pick a fixed wake time and set bedtime six hours earlier (a bounded
   **sleep window**).
2. Get up at the wake time regardless of sleep quality; hold the window
   for about two weeks so even six hours becomes more restorative and
   sleep onset speeds up.
3. Over the next one to two weeks, move bedtime earlier by ~30 minutes
   every couple of days until reaching roughly 7–8 hours, keeping wake
   time stable (weekends included — varying wake time by more than ~30
   minutes risks resetting the pattern).

While rebuilding, **restrict napping** so total sleep pressure consolidates
at night; short naps may return once sleep is stable. If unable to sleep
after ~10 minutes, leave bed for a boring low-stimulation activity until
sleepy rather than lying awake — bed stays associated with sleep, not
frustrated effort.

This pairs with [sleep debt compounds across
dimensions](sleep-debt-compounds-across-dimensions.md) and [body budget as
cognitive baseline](body-budget-as-cognitive-baseline.md): daytime
movement (especially outdoors) deepens night sleep, while late caffeine,
alcohol, and stimulating screen use in bed work against the window. A
repeating pre-bed wind-down (~45 minutes) signals shutdown to the nervous
system — see [routine as decision-capacity
preservation](routine-as-decision-capacity-preservation.md) for the same
principle applied to meals.

Pressure to "must sleep eight hours" can itself become cognitive load;
treating quiet rest in bed as legitimate when sleep is slow reduces the
anxiety that blocks restoration.
