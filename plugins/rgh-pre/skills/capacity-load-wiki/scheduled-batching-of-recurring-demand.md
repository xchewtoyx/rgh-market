title: Scheduled Batching of Recurring Demand
description: >
  Confining a recurring, capacity-consuming activity to one fixed time
  window and deferring intrusions outside it prevents that demand from
  draining capacity continuously throughout the day.
sources:
  - title: "Self-Care for Autistic People: 100+ Ways to Recharge, De-Stress, and Unmask"
    resource: "Self-Care for Autistic People (Neff), Chapter 3 (Emotional Self-Care)"
---

Some demands are recurring rather than one-off, and left unbounded they
spend capacity continuously rather than once — worry is the source's
example: unaddressed, it can intrude at any point and draw on capacity
throughout the day. The **Worry Period** technique bounds it instead: a
fixed daily window (10-20 minutes) is set aside for the activity, worries
are sorted there into controllable (turned into concrete steps) and
uncontrollable, and when the same demand intrudes outside the window, it's
deliberately deferred with the assurance it will be addressed at the
scheduled time.

The general mechanism is batching plus deferral: converting a demand that
would otherwise spend capacity in unpredictable, continuous small amounts
into one bounded, predictable expenditure, which both caps the total spend
and makes remaining headroom for the rest of the day easier to estimate in
advance. A related but distinct lever is grouping different demands
together to avoid paying a switching cost between them — see [task-
switching cost](task-switching-cost.md).
