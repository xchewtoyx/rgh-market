type: concept
title: Oscillating Performance Under Overload
description: >
  When total demand exceeds sustainable load, output often alternates
  between hyperfocused overdelivery and bare-minimum shutdown — looking
  like inconsistency but reflecting a capacity problem, not capability.
sources:
  - title: "Knowing Why: Adult-Diagnosed Autistic People on Life and Autism"
    resource: "Knowing Why (ASAN), Chapter 5 (Kelly Bron Johnson — Working While Autistic)"
---

Kelly Bron Johnson describes being labeled an "inconsistent performer":
periods of hyperfocused overdelivery alternate with shutting down to the
bare minimum when panic, overwhelm, and executive dysfunction make it
impossible to figure out where to start. From outside, this looks like
unreliable effort; from inside, it is the same person operating at
opposite ends of a depleted [shared pool of voluntary
effort](shared-pool-of-voluntary-effort.md) — stacking freelance work,
classes, and full-time employment on top of sensory and social load until
the pool empties, then crashing.

Contributors include difficulty saying no, excitement about novelty,
difficulty reading exploitation, and workplace environments that ignore
sensory cost (fluorescent lighting, temperature swings over eight hours).
The capability-versus-capacity distinction matters: skills and work ethic
can remain intact while headroom oscillates. Fixes target total demand,
environment fit, and written/externalized instructions — not "trying
harder" in the crash phase.

mismatch as the source of disabling
