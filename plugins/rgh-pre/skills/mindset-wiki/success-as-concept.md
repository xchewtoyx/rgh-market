title: Success as Concept

  "Success" is a story layered on ordinary standing, sitting, and lying

"We do only three things in life: we stand, we sit, we lie horizontal."
Once "successful," you are still just sitting or standing. Success is a
concept, an illusion — without a story, you're successful wherever you are.
A $3,900 chair and a $39 chair are both sitting.

This reframes [work and money problems as thinking
problems](work-and-money-problems-as-thinking-problems.md): the felt lack
is often attachment to a narrative about where you should be, not a deficit
in the present moment's actual activity. [Inquiry as thought-
testing](inquiry-as-thought-testing.md) targets the success story, not the
furniture.
