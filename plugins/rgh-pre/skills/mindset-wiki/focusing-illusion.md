---
type: concept
title: Focusing Illusion
description: >
  Whatever you are thinking about right now seems more important to your
  life than it actually is — because attention substitutes for the full
  picture of well-being.
sources:
  - title: Thinking, Fast and Slow
    resource: "Thinking, Fast and Slow (Daniel Kahneman), ch. 39"
---

"Nothing in life is as important as you think it is when you are thinking
about it." Whatever aspect of life is in attention looms disproportionately
large in global evaluation — an instance of [heuristic
substitution](heuristic-substitution.md): you answer how important
something is to happiness by how strongly you feel about it when cued.

California vs. Midwest study: climates differed sharply in rated enjoyment,
but overall life satisfaction did not — yet both groups believed
Californians were happier, overweighting climate and underweighting
everything else (WYSIATI).

Car-pleasure test: "How much pleasure do you get from your car?" is
answered as "…when you think about it?" — rare while driving. Same for
climate for long-term residents: "like having ten toes — nice, but not
something one thinks much about."

Adaptation: paraplegics are in fairly good mood more than half the time
within a month; mood is somber mainly when attending to the condition.
"Adaptation consists in large part of thinking less and less about it."
Exceptions with little adaptation: chronic pain, constant loud noise,
severe depression. People without personal acquaintance overestimate bad
mood at one year vs. one month; those who know someone forecast adaptation
better.

Life satisfaction judgments can diverge from experienced well-being:
colostomy patients' moment-to-moment happiness matches healthy controls,
yet they'd trade years of life to avoid the condition — evaluation
invites comparison and focusing on the burden.

When a single factor (move, purchase, diagnosis) feels decisive for future
happiness, ask how much time you will actually spend attending to it, and
whether you're substituting current salience for full life context — see
[availability heuristic](availability-heuristic.md) and [happiness
equation](happiness-equation.md).
