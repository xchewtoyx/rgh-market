---
type: concept
title: No One Can Stop You From Loving
description: >
  Another person's behavior cannot remove your capacity to love unless
  unquestioned thoughts about what they should be doing block it.
sources:
  - title: "Loving What Is, Revised Edition: Four Questions That Can Change Your Life"
    resource: "Loving What Is (Byron Katie), ch. 12"
---

"I want my father to love me, to understand me, to be there for me" —
tested impossible. Reaction: victimhood, heaviness, aloneness. Turn to
self: "I want me to love me..." and "I want me to love my father, to
understand him, to be there for him." "He's violent... he's lost in the
dream... If you were believing what he was believing about you, you would
drag you to the bathroom, too... We're innocent. So have a little mercy."

"No one can stop me from loving them. They don't have a choice... Only my
unquestioned thinking can cost me that." What's believed onto an abusive
parent transfers onto partners ("I need my partner to be a real partner")
— "Then you turn into your father when you believe those thoughts. Not
quite so brutal but just enough."

Turn "I need my father to love me" to needing yourself as a good father to
yourself, and to loving/acknowledging/be there for him — "people only come
to you in your imagination." Pair with [your happiness is your
responsibility](your-happiness-is-your-responsibility.md) and [close
relationships as teachers](close-relationships-as-teachers.md).
