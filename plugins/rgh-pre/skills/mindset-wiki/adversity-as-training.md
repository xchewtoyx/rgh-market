"He has won without glory who has won without peril." Seneca: Mucius tested
by fire, Fabricius by poverty, Rutilius by exile — "One cannot find a great
exemplar except in misfortune." Don't worry whether things will be hard;
they will. Focus that hardship helps you, which is why it needn't be feared.

"Our bruises and scars become armor. Our struggles become experience." If it
were easy, everyone would do it — "The risk is a feature, not a bug."
Malcolm X: "There is no better than adversity... Every defeat, every
heartbreak, every loss, contains its own seed, its own lesson."

Shakespeare: "Plenty and peace breed cowards... Hardness ever of hardiness
is mother." Going through difficulty today makes you better later. Wishing
it were easier is "just the fear talking." "It's good that it's hard. It
deters the cowards and it intrigues the courageous."
