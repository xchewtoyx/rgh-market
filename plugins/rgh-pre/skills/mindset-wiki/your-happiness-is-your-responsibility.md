---
type: concept
title: Your Happiness Is Your Responsibility
description: >
  Treating another person's choices as the cause of your unhappiness keeps
  you fighting what you cannot control — inquiry turns the mechanism back
  toward your own beliefs and reactions.
sources:
  - title: "Loving What Is, Revised Edition: Four Questions That Can Change Your Life"
    resource: "Loving What Is (Byron Katie), ch. 4"
---

"If I think that someone else is causing my problem, I'm insane... Yes,
but only all of them. It's just been a misunderstanding. Your
misunderstanding. Not theirs. Not ever, not even a little. Your happiness
is your responsibility."

On an affair: "He wants to hurt you" can't be absolutely known. Turned:
"I want to hurt myself" / "I want to hurt him" — laying on guilt and
treating him badly when believing the thought. "I want David to express
what he's feeling" → "I want me to express my feelings" — "That's for you
to live. It's your way, not his."

Visualization: picture the partner and the other person "without your
story," just looking at his face — "He wants to be with her" tested:
"Who's he with?" → "Oh! He's with me." "No one's living with the guy"
because you mentally have him with her while he is, in physical reality,
with you. Turn "I want his happiness" to "I want my happiness." "Skip the
middleman and be happy now. He'll follow. He has to, because he's your
projection... His happiness is his responsibility. And yours is your
responsibility. No one can make you happy but you."

This is [agency over interpretation](agency-over-interpretation.md) in
intimate relationships and [staying in your own
business](staying-in-your-own-business.md). Pain when it arises shows what's
left to investigate — "what's blocking you from the awareness of love.
That's what pain is for." See [pain versus suffering](pain-vs-suffering.md).
