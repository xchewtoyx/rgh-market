---
type: concept
title: Honesty Over Spiritual Pretense
description: >
  Pretending to be beyond your own evolution — or acting like a teacher
  when you're still a student — is painful; honest inquiry works from where
  you actually are, not from a performed identity.
sources:
  - title: "Loving What Is, Revised Edition: Four Questions That Can Change Your Life"
    resource: "Loving What Is (Byron Katie), ch. 13"
---

"Don't be spiritual — be honest instead." Pretending to be beyond your own
evolution — living any lie — is painful. Acting like a teacher is often
driven by fear of being the student. Either you are fearless in a moment,
or you're not; performing a spiritual identity adds a second layer of
stress on top of the original belief.

This is the same move as [signs a belief is your
identity](signs-a-belief-is-your-identity.md) applied to practice itself:
when inquiry becomes a badge rather than a test, return to one honest
[stressful feeling](stressful-feeling-as-inquiry-signal.md) and the
specific thought underneath it.
