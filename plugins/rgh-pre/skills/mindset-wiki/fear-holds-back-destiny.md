title: Fear Holds Back Destiny
description: >
  Fear freezes action at the threshold of meaningful work — keeping you from
  what you were put here to do until you inch closer and finally leap.
sources:
  - title: "Courage Is Calling: Fortune Favors the Brave"
    resource: "Courage Is Calling (Ryan Holiday), ch. 2"
---

"Fear does this. It keeps us from our destiny. It holds us back. It freezes
us." Each person receives a call — to service, risk, challenging the status
quo, running toward what others flee — met with pressure to suppress it.
"Fear will make itself felt. It always does." The question is whether you
let it stop you from answering, or steel and prepare until ready.

Florence Nightingale's years of inaction against visible suffering illustrate
how [refusal of the call](refusal-of-the-call.md) can persist even when
stakes are undeniable: "the fear was greater" than horrors she could name.
"How very little can be done under the spirit of fear."

Greatness requires rising above fear — not being without it. The brave are
not fearless; they master what everyone feels. Of cowards, "nothing is
written. Nothing is remembered. Nothing is admired."

Pair with [fear as compass toward action](fear-as-compass-toward-action.md)
and [decision paralysis](decision-paralysis.md).
