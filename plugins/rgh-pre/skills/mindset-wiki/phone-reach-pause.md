title: Phone Reach Pause
description: >
  Noticing the body gearing up before reflexively reaching for a phone — then
  pausing to sense what you actually need — breaks autopilot distraction and
  can open moments of presence or joy.
sources:
  - title: "The Mindful Way through Depression: Freeing Yourself from Chronic Unhappiness"
    resource: "The Mindful Way through Depression (Williams, Teasdale, Segal, Kabat-Zinn), ch. 10"
---

Reflexive phone use in any gap — messages, social media, mail — can feel like
relaxing while the body contracts (tension in shoulders, a frown) as it gears
up for doing. Reaching for the phone while thinking you are resting is a
common [mental autopilot](mental-autopilot.md) pattern.

**Practice:** when you notice reaching for the phone, stop. Sense the body's
"heads-up" about what you actually need. Count to three or take a couple of
breaths, then choose — sometimes you pick it up; often you do not. Stopping
mid-reach can open space around tension; feeling sun on your face, feet on
ground, breath — more than relaxation, a moment of pure presence.

The body's "weather pattern" is a precious information source easily ignored.
Mindfulness reveals changeability of sensations, thoughts, and feelings as
events coming and going without taking them personally.

Pairs with [informal attention practices](informal-attention-practices.md).
