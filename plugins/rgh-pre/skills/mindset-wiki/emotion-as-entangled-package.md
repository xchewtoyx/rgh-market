type: concept
title: Emotion as Entangled Package
description: >
  What we call an emotion is usually a mutually reinforcing package of
  thoughts, feelings, body sensations, and action impulses — not a single
  channel you can trust as reporting the truth about the world.
sources:
  - title: "Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy for Depression, 2nd Edition"
    resource: "Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy for Depression (Segal, Williams, Teasdale), ch. 14"
---

MBCT models experience as feedback loops among thoughts, feelings, body
sensations, and impulses to act, all arising from a trigger in context ("what
happened recently"). Loops run in every direction — not just thoughts→feelings
but feelings→thoughts, body→feelings, impulses back to thoughts.

What we label "an emotion" is this whole entangled package. Seeing that clearly
reduces effort spent treating the package as if it were telling the truth about
the world, yourself, the past, or the future. [Three-minute breathing
space](three-minute-breathing-space.md) deliberately parses the package in Step 1;
[thoughts as mental events](thoughts-as-mental-events.md) decenter from the
thought component; body-first work when charge is high addresses sensation before
story.

Pairs with [thoughts trigger emotions](thoughts-trigger-emotions.md) for the
thought→feeling direction and [distress tolerance](distress-tolerance.md) for
holding the bodily component.
