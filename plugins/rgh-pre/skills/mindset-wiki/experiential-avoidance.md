After painful moods in the past, it is understandable to treat your own
unhappiness as a threat and activate the brain's avoidance system — walling off,
suppressing, numbing, or pretending unwanted experience is not there. This
dampens curiosity, engagement, and goodwill, and disconnects you not only from
negative feelings but possibly from the full range of feeling — reinforcing
being out of touch with being alive.

**Experiential avoidance** is like turning up the car radio to blank out a strange
engine noise — effective at blanking the noise, ineffective at preventing the
engine seizing ten miles later. Research across many studies links emotional
disturbance to unhealthy efforts to escape and avoid emotions. In the long run
avoidance does not work: unpleasant feelings remain and still trigger habitual
reactions that turn passing unpleasantness into persistent suffering.

## The internal barometer

Underneath feeling dimensions is a single scale registering experience as
pleasant, neutral, or negative — an **internal barometer** giving continuous
readout of internal atmosphere. Pleasant registration triggers wanting to prolong;
unpleasant triggers wanting escape. Mostly this happens automatically, below
awareness. Tuning the barometer — especially through body awareness — gives a
generic early-warning system for uncomfortable emotions as they emerge. See
[feeling tone and reactivity](feeling-tone-and-reactivity.md).

Opening body awareness transforms "a cascade of reactions into a series of choice
points." At first unpleasantness and aversion feel fused; with practice you detect
unpleasantness before it triggers aversion, then distinguish the "get me out of
here" reaction itself. Bodily signatures of aversion: shoulder contraction, jaw
clench, belly tightening, forehead tension — fight-or-flight whether escaping a
tiger or your own feelings.

## Motivation shapes what follows

The mouse-in-the-maze experiment: solving a maze to help a mouse **avoid an owl**
(vs. find cheese) left participants **50 percent lower** on a subsequent creativity
test — avoidance-elicited caution, vigilance, and narrowed options lingered from a
two-minute symbolic task. The same action differs depending on whether driven by
[approach versus avoidance](approach-versus-avoidance-in-emotions.md). Infusing
mindfulness with interest, curiosity, warmth, and goodwill counters aversion while
putting you in touch with sensations — cultivating wholesome intention is as much
part of practice as focus.

Sad mood can inhibit sensory processing (default mode network brooding at the
expense of sensation); higher sensory shutdown intensity predicts greater depression
risk — mindfulness training may quiet this pattern.
