---
type: concept
title: Making Extra Unhappiness
description: >
  Driven-doing generates additional layers of distress — unhappiness about
  unhappiness, fear about fear — by focusing on the gap between how you feel
  and how you want to feel; being mode cuts that fuel source.
sources:
  - title: "The Mindful Way through Depression: Freeing Yourself from Chronic Unhappiness"
    resource: "The Mindful Way through Depression (Williams, Teasdale, Segal, Kabat-Zinn), ch. 3"
---

In [driven-doing mode](driven-doing-mode.md), we often generate **making
extra**: additional unhappiness about unhappiness, fear about fear, anger
about anger, frustration at failed attempts to think our way out of
suffering. This stems from focusing on the discrepancy between how we feel
and how we want to feel.

Shifting to [being mode](doing-mode-being-mode.md) "cuts through the source
of much of the additional unhappiness," removing a primary fuel for
escalating cycles of dissatisfaction and depression. [Non-striving in
meditation](non-striving-in-meditation.md) reframes clinging to the goal of
"fixing" an unworthy self — which draws us into rumination — as itself part
of the downward spiral. Non-striving lets us refrain from judging and
condemning moods and trying to escape emotions we do not want, "pulling the
plug" on depressive rumination.

Related: [pain vs. suffering](pain-vs-suffering.md) — suffering as optional
replay layered on pain; making extra is the meta-layer where distress about
distress compounds the original feeling.
