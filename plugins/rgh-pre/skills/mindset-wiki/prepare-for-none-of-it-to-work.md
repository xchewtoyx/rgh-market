type: concept
title: Prepare for None of It to Work
description: >
  Perception and action are yours; outcomes are not — hold yourself to your
  best effort, then accept the verdict instantly and move on.
sources:
  - title: "The Obstacle Is the Way: The Timeless Art of Turning Trials into Triumph, Expanded 10th Anniversary Edition"
    resource: "The Obstacle Is the Way (Ryan Holiday), ch. 26"
---

"You are invincible if nothing outside the will can disconcert you"
(Epictetus). Perceptions can be managed, actions directed, but the world
cannot be controlled. You can perceive well and act rightly and still fail.
Some obstacles are genuinely impossible; some problems recur.

It's to your advantage that life doesn't always go your way — Emerson: a
cushioned life falls asleep; being "pushed, tormented, defeated" puts you
"on your wits," cures "the insanity of conceit," and gains "moderation and
real skill."

Every blocked path is a chance to practice another virtue — forgiveness when
hurt, acceptance when a business fails, helping others when you cannot help
yourself. The standard is your best, not the impossible. Be willing to roll
the dice and lose; at day's end, prepare for none of it to work. No planning
guarantees outcome — try with everything you've got, accept the verdict,
move on. "The world could use fewer martyrs."

Pair with [discipline of the will](discipline-of-will.md) and [prepare for
harder times](discipline-of-will.md) (practice litany). Complements
[acceptance versus resignation](acceptance-vs-resignation.md).
