---
type: concept
title: Breathing Into and With
description: >
  When intense body sensations arise in meditation, breathing into a region
  carries awareness into resistance, while breathing with holds the sensation
  and breath together when intensity threatens to overwhelm.
sources:
  - title: "The Mindful Way through Depression: Freeing Yourself from Chronic Unhappiness"
    resource: "The Mindful Way through Depression (Williams, Teasdale, Segal, Kabat-Zinn), ch. 6"
---

When attention is drawn to a region of intense sensation, the habit of
[experiential avoidance](experiential-avoidance.md) creates resistance to
bringing awareness right into it.

**Breathing into** — sense or imagine the breath as a vehicle carrying
penetrating awareness into the core of the region of intensity. Extends the
body-scan skill of directing breath toward difficult areas.

**Breathing with** — when intensity becomes overwhelming, stabilize attention
by holding awareness of the intensity together with awareness of the whole
breath moving in and out, somewhat in the background. Whatever we experience
is always accompanied by breath; weaving breath awareness with any other
experience steadies the mind. Analogy: listening to music while keeping
breath as a background focus — trial and error finds the balance between
foreground (difficult experience) and steadying background breath.

Especially valuable for turning toward intense physical sensations and
unpleasant feelings without fleeing. Pairs with [working the
edge](working-the-edge.md) and [breath as attention
anchor](breath-as-attention-anchor.md).
