Zen parable: a king blocks the main road with a boulder; subjects curse and
turn back after halfhearted tries. A peasant fashions a lever, dislodges
the stone, and finds beneath it a purse of gold and a note: "The obstacle
in the path becomes the path."

"Never forget, within every obstacle is an opportunity to improve our
condition." When pursuing a goal, obstacles "are actually teaching you how
to get where you want to go — carving you a path." Benjamin Franklin:
"The Things which hurt... instruct."

This is the founding image for [obstacle as fuel](obstacle-as-fuel.md) and
the [perception action will
framework](perception-action-will-framework.md): not denying difficulty but
using it as material. "The Obstacle is the Way" — obstacles are not only to
be expected but embraced as opportunities to test yourself, try new things,
and triumph.

Pair with [adversity as training](adversity-as-training.md) and [prepare
for none of it to work](prepare-for-none-of-it-to-work.md) when the path
truly cannot be forced.
