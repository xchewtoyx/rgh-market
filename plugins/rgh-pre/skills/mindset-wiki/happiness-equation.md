---
type: concept
title: Happiness Equation (Perception Minus Expectation)
description: >
  Emotional response to an event is a function of how the event is perceived
  relative to what was expected, not a fixed property of the event itself.
sources:
  - title: That Little Voice in Your Head
    resource: "That Little Voice in Your Head (Mo Gawdat), Introduction"
---

Happiness ≥ (Perception of the events of your life) − (Expectations of how
life should behave). An event carries no inherent happy/unhappy value on
its own — rain is not sad or joyful in itself, it only becomes so relative
to what was expected of the day. The same event can therefore produce
opposite reactions in different people, or in the same person at different
times, purely because their expectations differ.

This gives two independent levers for working with a difficult moment:
change the perception (see it more accurately — [cognitive
reframing](cognitive-reframing.md)), or examine and adjust the expectation
that made the event feel like a violation. Unrealistic or rigid
expectations manufacture disappointment regardless of how good the actual
event is; noticing an expectation as the source of distress, rather than
the event, is itself an act of separating [pain from
suffering](pain-vs-suffering.md).
