---
type: concept
title: Surfing the Mind's Waves
description: >
  Meditation does not shut off mental activity or stress — it learns to work
  with inevitable inner turbulence rather than suppressing it into false calm.
sources:
  - title: "Wherever You Go, There You Are: Mindfulness Meditation in Everyday Life"
    resource: "Wherever You Go, There You Are (Jon Kabat-Zinn), Part 2: You Can't Stop the Waves but You Can Learn to Surf"
---

Meditation is commonly but incorrectly viewed as a way to shut off pressures
of the world or of one's own mind. It is neither shutting things out nor
off — it is seeing things clearly and deliberately repositioning yourself
in relation to them. Stress is an inevitable part of being human; while
intelligent choices can avoid making things worse, much in life is outside
our control. That does not make us victims — we can learn to work with,
understand, and find meaning in stressful forces, using their energies to
grow in strength, wisdom, and compassion. "A willingness to embrace and
work with what is lies at the core of all meditation practice."

Mind-as-lake analogy: waves (big, small, imperceptible) are always present,
churned by winds of stress and change. A misconception is that meditation
magically flattens the surface — but you cannot put a glass plate on water
to calm waves; trying only creates more tension. Calmness is attainable,
but not by suppressing the mind's natural activity. Meditation offers
shelter from much of the wind that agitates the mind; over time turbulence
may die down from lack of continuous feeding — but ultimately the winds of
life and mind will blow. Meditation is about knowing this and how to work
with it: "You can't stop the waves, but you can learn to surf."

This reframes [distress tolerance](distress-tolerance.md) in attention
practice: the goal is not empty mind but [feeling the way you feel](mindfulness.md)
while relating differently to what arises. It contrasts with chasing
special calm states in [non-striving in meditation](non-striving-in-meditation.md).
