title: Non-Doing in Practice
description: >
  Non-doing is deliberate stopping of outward striving to cultivate stillness
  and effortless activity — not indolence, but letting effort arise from
  presence rather than self-involved doing mode.
sources:
  - title: "Wherever You Go, There You Are: Mindfulness Meditation in Everyday Life"
    resource: "Wherever You Go, There You Are (Jon Kabat-Zinn), Part 2: In Praise of Non-Doing"
---

Sitting to meditate is time for **non-doing** — not the same as doing nothing;
consciousness and intention are key. Formal meditation makes time for stopping
outward activity and cultivating stillness with no agenda beyond being fully
present. "Perhaps such moments of non-doing are the greatest gift one can give
oneself."

The joy of non-doing: nothing else needs to happen for the moment to be complete;
wisdom and equanimity lie in knowing something else surely will happen regardless.
American culture values doing and progress, making non-doing's joy hard to grasp.

**Non-doing paradox:** the only way to do anything of value is to let effort come
out of non-doing, letting go of caring whether it will be of use — otherwise
self-involvement and greediness distort the work.

**Non-doing in action:** inward stillness of the doer merges with outward activity
so action does itself — effortless activity, no small "I/me/mine" claiming a
result, yet nothing left undone. Chuang Tzu's cook cutting an ox: senses idle,
spirit free, cleaver finding its own way through nineteen years without dulling.
Non-doing is a cornerstone of mastery in any realm.

Non-doing is not indolence — it takes great courage and energy. "Letting things
be and allowing them to unfold in their own way" can involve enormous but graceful,
knowledgeable, effortless effort — "doerless doing."

Meditation is synonymous with practicing non-doing — not to make things perfect
but to realize things already are perfectly what they are, then act from knowing
what is what. Some call this [flow state](flow-state.md) — one moment flowing
into the next, cradled in the streambed of [mindfulness](mindfulness.md).

Contrasts [driven-doing mode](driven-doing-mode.md) and complements [doing mode
and being mode](doing-mode-being-mode.md).
