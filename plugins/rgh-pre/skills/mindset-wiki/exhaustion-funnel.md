type: concept
title: Exhaustion Funnel
description: >
  A visual model of how conscientious people progressively drop nourishing
  activities while stressors remain — narrowing life until mainly depleting
  demands remain and exhaustion deepens.
sources:
  - title: "Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy for Depression, 2nd Edition"
    resource: "Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy for Depression (Segal, Williams, Teasdale), ch. 16"
---

As mood lowers, people often give up activities that feel "optional" but
**nourishing** (N) while **depleting** (D) stressors and work demands remain —
progressively narrowing life like concentric circles in a funnel. The people
most likely to continue this spiral are often the most conscientious workers,
whose self-confidence is closely tied to work performance — not the lazy ones.

An activity's nourishing or depleting quality can depend on mood and circumstance
("rushing kids to school feels depleting unless I have time to talk — then it's
nourishing"). The exercise is curiosity: what does it depend on? How might I make
more time for nourishing activities or handle depleting ones more skillfully?

Pairs with [behavioral activation](behavioral-activation.md), [motivation
follows action](motivation-follows-action.md), and the pleasure/mastery
distinction in [three-minute breathing space](three-minute-breathing-space.md).
