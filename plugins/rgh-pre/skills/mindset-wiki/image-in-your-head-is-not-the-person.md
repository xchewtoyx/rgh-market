---
type: concept
title: Image in Your Head Is Not the Person
description: >
  The mental picture you maintain of someone you love — especially when
  frightened for them — is a dream you generate, not the living person
  you can actually relate to.
sources:
  - title: "Loving What Is, Revised Edition: Four Questions That Can Change Your Life"
    resource: "Loving What Is (Byron Katie), ch. 4"
---

Statement 6 willing/look-forward-to turnarounds on seeing loved ones
"endanger their health again" are an ongoing wake-up against mistaking
mental images for real people: "the image of my son in my head isn't my
son... it's all a dream... until I'm awake to the dream, my Work's not
done."

This connects [seeing without your story](seeing-without-your-story.md)
with [noticing the story your mind is
telling](noticing-the-story-your-mind-is-telling.md): the son you argue
with in imagination is not the son on the phone. [Inquiry as thought-
testing](inquiry-as-thought-testing.md) targets the dream version; relating
happens with who is actually there.
