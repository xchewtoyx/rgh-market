---
type: concept
title: Awareness Versus Thinking
description: >
  Awareness holds and contains thinking rather than being the same as it —
  watching thought from a vantage behind the waterfall lets patterns change
  toward integration without positive-thinking substitution.
sources:
  - title: "Wherever You Go, There You Are: Mindfulness Meditation in Everyday Life"
    resource: "Wherever You Go, There You Are (Jon Kabat-Zinn), Part 4: Meditation — Not to Be Confused with Positive Thinking"
---

Thinking differentiates humans and is miraculous, but unchecked it crowds out
other facets of being — wakefulness is often the first casualty. **Awareness is
not the same as thought** — it lies beyond thinking though it makes use of it.
"Awareness is more like a vessel which can hold and contain our thinking, helping
us to see and know our thoughts as thoughts rather than getting caught up in them
as reality."

The thinking mind is almost always fragmented; awareness sustained moment to
moment reveals fundamental nature as integrated and whole. **Soup-pot metaphor:**
awareness cooks chopped fragments of experience into a whole without actively
doing anything — let fragments stir while holding them in awareness.

Meditation is not changing thinking by thinking more — it is **watching** thought;
"the watching is the holding." By watching without being drawn in, thought patterns
change toward integration, understanding, and compassion — not by replacing one
thought with a "purer" one but by understanding thoughts' nature and your
relationship to them.

**Waterfall metaphor:** thinking is a continual cascade; mindfulness finds a
vantage behind or beyond the waterfall — still seeing and hearing water but out
of the torrent. Same move as [sitting beside the thought stream](sitting-beside-the-thought-stream.md).

Explicit distinction from positive thinking: deciding to think positively may be
useful but is not meditation — it is just more thinking. You can be as imprisoned
by positive thinking as negative; transformation requires an element beyond thought's
limits.

**Going inside:** extended inward dwelling teaches the poverty of always looking
outside for happiness — happiness is no deeper than capacity to know oneself
inwardly. Being centered briefly against the outer world's pull lets you be at
home wherever you are.

Pairs with [thought defusion](thought-defusion.md) and [mindfulness](mindfulness.md).
