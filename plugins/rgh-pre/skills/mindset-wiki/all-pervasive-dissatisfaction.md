---
type: concept
title: All-Pervasive Dissatisfaction
description: >
  A background sense that life should be bigger, better, or more —
  independent of any real unmet need — that resets after every achievement
  so satisfaction never actually accumulates.
sources:
  - title: That Little Voice in Your Head
    resource: "That Little Voice in Your Head (Mo Gawdat), Chapter 3: Under Attack"
---

This is the rational-brain member of the [AAA framework](aaa-framework.md):
a persistent "not enough yet" that survives even the fulfillment of a
specific desire. Getting the thing that was supposedly needed produces
only momentary relief before attention moves to the next, bigger target —
a want satisfied one moment simply reveals a new want the next, so the gap
being chased never actually closes.

Because the dissatisfaction is generated by the expectation side of the
[happiness equation](happiness-equation.md) rather than by any real
deficit, the direct countermeasure is not acquiring more but changing what
is being measured against: see [gratitude practice](gratitude-practice.md)
for the specific technique of deliberately attending to what is already
present rather than what is still missing.
