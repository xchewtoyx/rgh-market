type: concept
title: Stressful Feeling as Inquiry Signal
description: >
  A stressful emotion often appears before the untrue thought driving it
  is conscious, so the feeling can point you toward which belief to
  test rather than something to fix only through external change.
sources:
  - title: "Loving What Is, Revised Edition: Four Questions That Can Change Your Life"
    resource: "Loving What Is (Byron Katie), ch. 1"
---

Every stressful feeling traced back to attaching to an untrue thought that
argues with reality. The sequence runs: untrue thought → stressful feeling
→ action on that feeling that creates more stress. The common mistake is
trying to change the feeling externally — changing another person, or
reaching for sex, food, alcohol, drugs, or money for temporary comfort —
instead of finding the originating thought.

A stressful feeling can be read as a compassionate alarm: "You're caught in
the dream." Depression, pain, and fear point at an untrue story. Because
the feeling often arrives before the thought is conscious, the feeling
itself signals which belief to bring to inquiry. Once inquiry reveals a
thought is untrue, you move away from it automatically — like a hand in
fire pulling back without being told. The next time the thought arises, the
mind tends to move away on its own. Before the thought, no suffering; with
the believed thought, suffering; recognizing untruth, no suffering again.

This reframes [catching automatic thoughts](catching-automatic-thoughts.md):
when a strong feeling is present but the triggering belief isn't yet clear,
start from the feeling and ask what you must be believing for this feeling
to make sense — then test that belief (see [inquiry as thought-
testing](inquiry-as-thought-testing.md)).
