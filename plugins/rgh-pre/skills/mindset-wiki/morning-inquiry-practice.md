type: concept
title: Morning Inquiry Practice
description: >
  A daily worksheet session — even twenty to thirty minutes — treats
  stressful beliefs as ongoing teachers rather than one-time problems to
  eliminate.
sources:
  - title: "Loving What Is, Revised Edition: Four Questions That Can Change Your Life"
    resource: "Loving What Is (Byron Katie), ch. 4"
---

After deep Work on a betrayal or loss, the practice continues: 20–30 minutes
each morning with a [judge your neighbor
worksheet](judge-your-neighbor-worksheet.md). "It's like a prayer...
'Keep bringing it on, so I can become enlightened to it every time.'"

Statement 6 willing/look-forward-to turnarounds acknowledge recurrence: "I
don't ever want to feel inferior, scared, dependent, or powerless again" →
"I'm willing to..." / "I look forward to..." — "you could take it that way
again." The goal isn't never feeling those states; it's meeting them with
[inquiry as thought-testing](inquiry-as-thought-testing.md) each time.

One marker of completed Work on such material: being able to send the
other woman "a thank-you note where you really mean it" — not performance,
but genuine [forgiveness through inquiry](forgiveness-through-inquiry.md).
