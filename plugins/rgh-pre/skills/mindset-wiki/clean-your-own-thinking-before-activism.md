---
type: concept
title: Clean Your Own Thinking Before Activism
description: >
  Effective outward action — including social or environmental activism —
  starts with meeting the pollution in your own mind with understanding,
  not with anger used as fuel.
sources:
  - title: "Loving What Is, Revised Edition: Four Questions That Can Change Your Life"
    resource: "Loving What Is (Byron Katie), ch. 6"
---

Just as [fear as a money motivator](fear-as-money-motivator.md) is often
treated as necessary for earning, anger and frustration are often treated as
necessary for activism. Katie's stance: "If I want to act sanely and
effectively while I clean up the earth's environment, let me begin by
cleaning up my own environment" — the trash and pollution in one's own
thinking first, met with love and understanding, so action becomes "truly
effective."

"It takes just one person to help the planet. That one is you." This is
not passivity — it is sequencing [inquiry as thought-testing](inquiry-as-
thought-testing.md) before outward crusade so the action is not [inner
terrorism](inner-terrorism.md) projected onto causes. Even in extreme
settings (prison workshops with violent offenders), the move is to find what
their lives teach about how not to live — and therefore how to live.

"There is nothing we can do that doesn't help the planet. That's the way it
really is" — once action comes from clarity rather than unmanaged rage.
