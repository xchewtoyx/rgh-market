---
type: concept
title: Automatic Writing for Emotional Release
description: >
  Timed, nonstop writing without editing — letting raw emotion pour onto
  the page — surfaces material you were not consciously tracking and
  reduces the perfectionism that blocks honest journaling.
sources:
  - title: "How to Be You: Simple Ways to Love Who You Are"
    resource: "How to Be You (Ellie Middleton), ch. 4, Tip 19"
---

Standard journaling can fail when an imaginary reader makes you monitor
every line for how "regulated" you look. Automatic writing removes that
audience: set a timer (start with two to ten minutes), write continuously
without stopping for the full duration, and allow illegible, repetitive,
contradictory, or "rubbish" output — the only rule is no pausing.

Optional openers: "I want to talk about..." or "I wish somebody understood
that..." If stuck, repeat a placeholder ("I have nothing more to say")
until something new surfaces. The aim is to reveal what is running in the
back of the mind — material you "don't know you know" — not to produce
polished prose.

Use as a daily practice or as an emergency response when emotions start
to dysregulate, before or alongside [grounding
techniques](grounding-techniques.md) and [naming emotions
specifically](naming-emotions-specifically.md). Pair with [brain
dump technique](brain-dump-technique.md) when the goal is clearing volume
rather than timed continuous flow.
