type: concept
title: Curiosity as Scientist Not Judge
description: >
  Treating yourself as a system to explore rather than a problem to fix —
  trying, observing, adjusting — replaces self-blame with data and keeps growth
  possible after setbacks.
sources:
  - title: "The Cherry Tree Theory: A Simple Guide to Stop F*cking Up Your Life"
    resource: "The Cherry Tree Theory (Rox Pink, Rich Pink), Epilogue"
---

"You are not a problem to fix but rather a system to explore." Be a scientist,
not a judge: try something, observe the result, adjust. A failed attempt is
not failure — it is data. Self-blame kills growth; curiosity nurtures it.

This stance is one trait of the broader [gardener mindset for
change](gardener-mindset-for-change.md) and pairs with [non-judging attitude in
meditation](non-judging-attitude-in-meditation.md) — witnessing experience without
condemning it — and with [growth mindset](growth-mindset.md)'s focus on
learning from difficulty rather than proving fixed ability.
