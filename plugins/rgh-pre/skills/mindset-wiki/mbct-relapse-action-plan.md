MBCT Session 8 reviews relapse prevention action plans begun in Session 7:
an **early-warning system** plus a written list of helpful things to do when
needed. The list works only in tandem with early recognition — and only if
translated into action, however small.

**Example — Jennifer's antidepressant activity list** (filed until needed,
with self-instruction: "Although you may not feel like doing any of these,
select at least one and do it anyway"):

1. **Pleasure** — phone a friend; rent a DVD; hot bath; nap; favorite food
   without guilt; favorite hot drink; walk (maybe with dog/friend); visit a
   friend; hobby; time with someone you like; cook; shopping; funny/uplifting
   watch; read for pleasure; music.
2. **Mastery** — clean one part of house (max 20 minutes); clear a cupboard;
   catch up on letters; pay a bill; garden; do a put-off task; exercise.
3. **Break big tasks into small steps** — e.g., just 10 minutes — then
   congratulate yourself.

This parallels [behavioral activation](behavioral-activation.md)'s pleasure/
achievement mix and the [three-minute breathing space](three-minute-breathing-space.md)
relapse structure: breathing space first, then gather past helpful practices,
then skillful action even when futile-feeling. Some participants seal a
letter listing actions for depression onset — pre-loading wisdom inaccessible
when mood drops.

Program reflections often emphasize: learning to slow down and be; an inner
"safe place" without external criticism; tactics for sensed low mood starting;
reduced shame and greater self-acceptance; shifting from future/past worry
toward embracing the present to see what causes depression and prevent relapse.

Pairs with [psychological gardening for relapse prevention](psychological-gardening-for-relapse-prevention.md)
and [maintaining behavior change](maintaining-behavior-change.md).
