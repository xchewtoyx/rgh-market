Realizing you are neurodivergent does not undo years of masking overnight.
After decades of morphing to fit, you may not know "where the mask ended,
and I began." Unmasking is individually variable, depends on safe
environment and support, and can feel like "going backwards" — travel or
cooking harder once you stop forcing robot mode through sensory overload.

Windows/Mac analogy: nothing was wrong with you as a Mac; you were running
programs not designed for your operating system. Learning how your brain
works invites living more authentically and forgiving past struggles —
reframing traits as differences, not deficits.

Starting points: listen to your body ([delayed
interoception](delayed-interoception.md)); lean into special interests;
spend time with other neurodivergent people; focus on strengths. Pair with
[saying goodbye to should](saying-goodbye-to-should.md). Be gentle —
unmask only when safe; society shares responsibility for building
environments where authenticity is possible.
