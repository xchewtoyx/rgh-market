title: Enemies and Critics Are Overestimated
description: >
  Fear of others' opinions distorts reality and paralyzes action — critics
  are fewer, less unified, and often as scared as you are once counted.
sources:
  - title: "Courage Is Calling: Fortune Favors the Brave"
    resource: "Courage Is Calling (Ryan Holiday), ch. 3"
---

"At the root of most fear is what other people will think of us." Cyril
Connolly: "There are many who dare not kill themselves for fear of what
the neighbors will say" — fear of judgment even when others would never
know. Almost everything now admired was done over loud objections; objectors
later pretend they never objected.

Cicero, mocked for nouveau-riche origins and flowery language: "Let other
people worry over what they will say about you. They will say it in any
case." No one who did something that mattered escaped offending someone; no
change went unmocked. Faceless strangers' average opinion should not
outweigh your considered judgment.

**Count the wolves:** Grant, crossing hostile Texas prairie, heard terrifying
wolf howls and hoped his companion would turn back. Asked how many wolves,
he guessed "about twenty" to avoid seeming afraid — there were two. "There
are always more of them before they are counted." Obstacles, enemies, and
critics want you to believe they are legion; advancing often makes them
retreat.

**Mutual fear:** Advancing on a Confederate force in Missouri, Grant's heart
was in his throat — yet he found the enemy had already fled, having heard
*his* troops were coming. "Harris had been as much afraid of me as I had
been of him... I never forgot that he had as much reason to fear my forces
as I had his." From then on he felt anxiety but not trepidation confronting
enemies.

General application: approaching a boss, interview, negotiation, or public
stand — the other side's certainty is often illusion; both sides are scared,
and the mismatch is smaller than it seems. "A little awareness, a little
empathy... gives us confidence." Pair with [fear-setting](fear-setting.md)
("when the wolves are counted, there are fewer of them"), [stop judging them
for judging you](stop-judging-them-for-judging-you.md), and [scare versus
fear as ruling state](scare-vs-fear-as-ruling-state.md).
