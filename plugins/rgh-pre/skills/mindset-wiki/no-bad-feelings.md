type: concept
title: No Bad Feelings
description: >
  Every emotion serves a purpose — sadness, anger, and anxiety are valid
  signals, not defects to eradicate or hide for others' comfort.
sources:
  - title: "How to Be You: Simple Ways to Love Who You Are"
    resource: "How to Be You (Ellie Middleton), ch. 4"
---

"There is no 'bad' or 'wrong' feeling." All emotions are valid and normal,
even uncomfortable ones — sadness responds to upset, (safely expressed)
anger flags misalignment, anxiety signals that something matters. No emotion
should be fully eradicated.

Western "stiff upper lip" culture teaches suppression while paradoxically
asking people to "open up" — an unresolved double bind. Objectivity norms
invalidate "emotional" expression; fear of conflict silences those who raise
issues; patriarchy leaves both women (labelled irrational) and men (denied
soft emotions) with fewer tools to process feeling healthily.

In a genuinely difficult era, distress is often an appropriate reaction.
Regulation aims at manageability, not self-shame — see [emotional
regulation as skill](emotional-regulation-as-skill.md) and [outward
emotion is valid](outward-emotion-is-valid.md).
