---
type: concept
title: Availability Cascade
description: >
  A self-reinforcing media cycle in which a vivid worry becomes easier to
  recall, feels more important, draws more coverage, and resets policy
  priorities — often exaggerating minor risks.
sources:
  - title: Thinking, Fast and Slow
    resource: "Thinking, Fast and Slow (Daniel Kahneman), ch. 14"
---

Kuran and Sunstein's **availability cascade** extends the [availability
heuristic](availability-heuristic.md) into social judgment of *importance*:
how fluently and emotionally an idea comes to mind shapes how much weight
it gets — "all heuristics are equal, but availability is more equal than
the others" in public life.

Mechanism: a media report of a relatively minor event catches attention; a
segment of the public becomes aroused; the emotional reaction itself becomes
a story, prompting more coverage and greater concern — a self-sustaining
cycle. **Availability entrepreneurs** sometimes feed the loop with a steady
stream of worrying news; danger is exaggerated as outlets compete for
headlines. Scientists who dampen fear attract little attention and often
hostility — anyone claiming overstated danger is suspected of cover-up. The
issue becomes politically important because it is on everyone's mind, and
policy follows intensity of sentiment, pushing other risks into the
background.

**Love Canal (1979):** exposed toxic waste, foul smell, activist resident
sustaining daily stories, scientists ignored, dramatic protests — large
relocation and Superfund legislation followed; substantiated health damage
remains disputed.

**Alar scare (1989):** apple-growth chemical reported carcinogenic at
gigantic doses in animals; cascade coverage including congressional
testimony; industry losses; citizen asking whether to pour juice down the
drain or take it to a toxic waste dump; product withdrawn; later research
suggested very small risk — net public-health effect probably negative as
people ate fewer apples.

Terrorists are skilled practitioners: excepting rare mass-casualty events,
terror deaths are tiny next to traffic deaths, but gruesome repeated images
keep everyone on edge — terrorism speaks directly to automatic fear.

For personal judgment: when a risk dominates conversation and imagination,
ask whether you are responding to frequency or to a cascade — pair with
[probability neglect](probability-neglect.md) and [retrieval fluency versus
content](retrieval-fluency-vs-content.md).
