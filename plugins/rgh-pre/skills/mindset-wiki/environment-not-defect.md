type: concept
title: Environment Not Defect
description: >
  When growth stalls, the useful question is what in the environment blocks
  blooming — not what is wrong with you — because you are both the tree and
  the gardener who can change those conditions.
sources:
  - title: "The Cherry Tree Theory: A Simple Guide to Stop F*cking Up Your Life"
    resource: "The Cherry Tree Theory (Rox Pink, Rich Pink), ch. 2 (Spoiler Alert)"
---

A tree that fails to bloom in season is not lazy or defective — something in
its environment (soil, roots, nutrition, pests) is blocking the inevitable.
"When a flower doesn't bloom, you fix the environment in which it grows, not
the flower."

The same move applies to people. Failed dreams, addiction, unhappy
relationships, and self-narratives like "I'm useless" or "dreams don't work
for people like me" are imperfect ways of understanding why blooming has not
happened — not accurate verdicts on your worth. Comparing yourself to a
neighbour's blossoming tree generates shame; no amount of self-hatred or will
can fix what is wrong in the soil.

**Spoiler alert: you are the tree. Double spoiler: you are also the
gardener.** If you are stuck, you are not at fault — given your past and
present environment, you are exactly where we would predict. The good news:
environments can be changed, and blooming becomes inevitable when conditions
support it.
