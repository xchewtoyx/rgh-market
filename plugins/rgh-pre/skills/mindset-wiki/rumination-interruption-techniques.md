type: concept
title: Rumination Interruption Techniques
description: >
  When rumination is a physical feedback loop rather than useful processing,
  interrupt the brain with cognitive overload, endorphin flooding, or safe
  dopamine-seeking — rather than continuing to think it through.
sources:
  - title: "The Autistic's Guide to Self-Discovery"
    resource: "The Autistic's Guide to Self-Discovery (Sol Smith), ch. 9"
---

Rumination can run as a feedback loop: amygdala signals danger → intellectual
obsessing → body feels anxiety about the thoughts → amygdala re-signals,
escalating until shut off. Awareness alone may not stop it; pushing on it can
strengthen it. **Interrupt** rather than continue thinking through.

**Detection signal:** repeated thinking removes enjoyment for a day or two,
disrupts sleep, causes crying/meltdown/shutdown, or re-hurts others by
revisiting resolved misunderstandings — rumination, not problem-solving.

**Short-term interrupts:**

1. **Max cognitive load** — Tetris, sudoku, complex math, jigsaw puzzles,
   card tricks; timed pressure maxes load fastest. The **Stroop effect**
   (naming ink color of mismatched color words aloud) fights strong pattern-
   seeking pathways — useful at meltdown or self-harm verge.
2. **Flood endorphins** — running, jumping jacks, breaking a sweat makes the
   brain unlikely to stay activated. Sprinting may punch the nervous system
   harder than walking (which can support thinking unless in greenery or as
   walking meditation).
3. **Safe dopamine fix** — guilty-pleasure activity that pulls you out of
   your head, avoiding anything resembling a prior addiction.

**Long-term tools:** build [mindfulness](mindfulness.md) during calm periods;
journal longhand to "complete the circuit" by putting cycling thoughts on paper.

For integration after interruption, see [label assess reassign for
rumination](label-assess-reassign-for-rumination.md).
