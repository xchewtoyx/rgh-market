title: Fresh Air of Awareness
description: >
  Stress, fatigue, and distressing emotions thrive in the absence of
  awareness — bringing even a little mindful attention puts space around them
  so constricting frames of mind no longer flourish.
sources:
  - title: "The Mindful Way through Depression: Freeing Yourself from Chronic Unhappiness"
    resource: "The Mindful Way through Depression (Williams, Teasdale, Segal, Kabat-Zinn), ch. 3"
---

Dry rot spores in old timber cannot thrive with constant fresh-air
circulation; surveyors install vents. Spores remain but do not flourish with
ventilation. Similarly, "stress, fatigue, and distressing emotions thrive
in the absence of the fresh air of awareness" — awareness does not make them
cease to exist, but it puts more space around them. That spaciousness creates
an environment where self-diminishing, constricting frames of mind no longer
thrive.

Mindfulness detects difficult states earlier, sees them more clearly, and
notices how they arise and pass — a way to see them without getting caught
in them. The solution to mood problems may not require heroic changes to
inner feelings or outer circumstances — "it may simply involve a shift in
the way we pay attention to all of them."

Even a little bit of mindfulness brought to a single moment can break the
chain of events that leads to persistent unhappiness. The dimension of
awareness itself is a powerful capacity we mostly ignore.
