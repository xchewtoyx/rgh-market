  Happiness is not a future balance-sheet outcome but the capacity to work

"Happiness is a clear mind." A clear mind knows how to work and act without
fear. This is Katie's operational definition tied to [work and money
problems as thinking problems](work-and-money-problems-as-thinking-
problems.md): the obstacle is not the job or the account but uninvestigated
beliefs about what money must do for you.

When [fear as a money motivator](fear-as-money-motivator.md) drops, security
can be recognized in what you already have for right now — and money often
becomes easier to make from that position. The parallel in activism: anger
and frustration used to fuel change are the same pattern — see [clean your
own thinking before activism](clean-your-own-thinking-before-activism.md).

Pairs with [your happiness is your
responsibility](your-happiness-is-your-responsibility.md) and [agency over
interpretation](agency-over-interpretation.md).
