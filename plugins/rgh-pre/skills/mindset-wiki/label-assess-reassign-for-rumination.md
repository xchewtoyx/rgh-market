type: concept
title: Label Assess Reassign for Rumination
description: >
  To integrate a painful memory and stop recurring rumination, collapse
  granular emotional detail into one dominant label, assess it in isolation,
  then reassign an ordinary ending with one concrete positive since.
sources:
  - title: "The Autistic's Guide to Self-Discovery"
    resource: "The Autistic's Guide to Self-Discovery (Sol Smith), ch. 9"
---

Autistic rumination often grips because emotional situations are seen in
granular detail that resists collapsing into umbrella concepts needed to set
them down. The **marble metaphor**: a marble swirls many colors — no clean
threshold for calling it "the yellow marble." Even happy milestones carry
co-present nervousness, boredom, and fear alongside joy.

Ruminating sorts the full color mix, often trying to smother one emotion with
another (rationalizing to escape guilt, gathering "evidence" to fight feeling
misunderstood) — spreading damage outward instead of resolving it.

**Four-step process:**

1. **Ruminate** — recognize rumination for what it is (starting point).
2. **Label** — pick one dominant emotion for the moment/conflict; disempower
   nuance the way a simplified neurotypical label ("you're angry") is often
   more navigable than an accurate multi-emotion account. "You have been
   looking at all the sand; now see the castle." Push the labeled moment back
   onto its shelf.
3. **Assess** — study the moment under its new label, separated from larger
   life patterns. Affirm discarding it: "I need to stop thinking about the
   car disagreement my wife and I had." View the label in isolation, not as
   a clue container.
4. **Reassign** — (a) contextualize as ordinary human messiness — "shitty things
   happen to people all the time"; (b) name exactly one good thing since,
   related or coincidental, not redemptive (finding five bucks counts).

Example: "I'm sad because of the divorce. It was shitty, and shitty things
happen. My new place is pretty nice, and I can keep my Transformers on a
display shelf now."

Flattens months of rumination into one feeling, one object, one integration.
Use [rumination interruption techniques](rumination-interruption-techniques.md)
when the loop escalates before integration work.
