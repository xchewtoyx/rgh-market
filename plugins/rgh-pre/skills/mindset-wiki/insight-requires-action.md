Sitting in a chair with a great realization is not the end of the process.
Self-realization "is not complete until it lives as action." Until an
insight lives as action — picking up the socks you judged others for
leaving, speaking an apology, joining a conversation you were avoiding —
it is not fully yours. [Inquiry as thought-testing](inquiry-as-thought-testing.md)
naturally leads to clear, kind, fearless action when beliefs release; the
body "begins to move by itself" after understanding meets thinking.

Turnarounds are not only mental: "He should pick up the socks" becomes "I
should pick up the socks" — then do it, staying in the flow of your own
truth rather than waiting for others to change first. Judgments of others,
once turned around, become prescriptions for how *you* should live; advice
you give family becomes advice for yourself.

**Living the turnarounds:**

- **Making amends** — after a turnaround like "I lied to him" (from "He
  lied to me"), go back to people you judged, make amends, and admit how you
  manipulated, used anger, sex, money, or guilt to get what you wanted —
  without ever mentioning their part.
- **Reporting** — go to people you've judged and report only your own
  turnarounds and realizations, never their faults, "so that I could hear
  it in the presence of at least two witnesses: the other person and
  myself."
- **Living amends** — broader than one incident: once inquiry reveals a
  pattern of harm, stop hurting anyone that way going forward; if you slip,
  tell them immediately why, and begin again "with a clean slate." If
  people on your worksheet are dead, "make living amends through the rest
  of us."

Apology and amends are done "for your own sake... It's all about your own
peace," not to be "a talking saint." Anonymous kindness counts only when
you're not found out — if discovered, start over.

The Work is about noticing thoughts, not forcing them away; action follows
when the mind is no longer arguing with reality. Pair with [being right
versus being free](being-right-vs-being-free.md) and [inquiry
turnarounds](inquiry-turnarounds.md) when moving from worksheet to behavior.
