title: Watch the Watcher
description: >
  Labeling thoughts as they arise ("Thinking," "Worrying") and returning to
  the breath widens the gap between you and your mental content — the first
  step toward choosing how much of yourself to show in each context.
sources:
  - title: "The Autistic's Guide to Self-Discovery"
    resource: "The Autistic's Guide to Self-Discovery (Sol Smith), ch. 8"
---

**Mindfulness** is the first step in unmasking — gaining agency over how much
of yourself shows up rather than imitating who you think you should be. Core
claim: thoughts, feelings, and therefore selfhood are **pliable** once noticed.

**Watch the watcher:** sit 10–15 minutes, focus on breath; when the mind
produces anxieties, plans, regrets — notice you started thinking, label
silently ("Thinking," "Planning," "Worrying"), let go, return to breath.
Avoid guilt at noticing (builds negative association); small positive
reinforcement for noticing and releasing. Goal is not absence of thought but
indifference to automatic thoughts and mindful choice when to engage one.

Interrupting thoughts often speak for **the mask** — anxiety replaying parents',
teachers', coaches' voices trying to socialize you into someone you're not.
Separate mask-anxiety from evolution's built-in **desire** dissatisfaction
(noise that says you're not satisfied even when basic needs are met).

thoughts](catching-automatic-thoughts.md), and [masking coping
strategies](masking-coping-strategies.md).
