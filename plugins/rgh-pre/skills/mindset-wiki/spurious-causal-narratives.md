---
type: concept
title: Spurious Causal Narratives
description: >
  System 1 automatically constructs coherent cause-and-effect stories from
  available fragments — even when the same "explanation" would fit opposite
  outcomes and explains nothing.
sources:
  - title: Thinking, Fast and Slow
    resource: "Thinking, Fast and Slow (Daniel Kahneman), ch. 7"
---

"Fred's parents arrived late. The caterers were expected soon. Fred was
angry." Readers instantly know Fred is angry about late parents, not
expected caterers — anger and lateness link as effect and cause in the
associative network. Finding causal connections is automatic; System 2 is
offered the interpretation and accepts it.

On the day Saddam Hussein was captured, U.S. Treasury prices first rose —
headline: capture may not curb terrorism. Half an hour later prices fell —
headline: capture boosts allure of risky assets. A major event was destined
to explain whatever the market did; one story accounting for opposite moves
explains nothing.

Jane's wallet: after reading about beautiful sights in crowded New York,
participants recalled "pickpocket" more strongly than "sights" — though only
"sights" appeared in the text. Associative coherence displaces actual wording
in memory.

This is automatic coherence-seeking beneath deliberate reasoning. Counter
with [scout mindset](scout-mindset.md) habits: ask what would falsify the
story, and whether you'd accept the same cause for the reverse outcome.
