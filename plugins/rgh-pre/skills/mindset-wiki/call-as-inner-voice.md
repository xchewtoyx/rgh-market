---
type: concept
title: Call as Inner Voice
description: >
  A sense that something is expected of you — a summons toward who you
  wish to become — is common; following it is optional and often opposed
  by inherited fears and limitations.
sources:
  - title: "Courage Is Calling: Fortune Favors the Brave"
    resource: "Courage Is Calling (Ryan Holiday), ch. 2"
---

"Somewhere inside, we hear a voice... Our voice leads us in the direction
of the person we wish to become, but it is up to us whether or not to
follow." Adults often "foist upon us their own fears, their own limitations"
under the guise of preparing us for the future — puncturing confidence that
was fearless in childhood.

What you are called to do "comes from somewhere beyond us; it's bigger than
us. We are each called to be something... but will we choose to accept
this?" Years of [refusal of the call](refusal-of-the-call.md) can be read
as ignoring the summons or as preparation — either way, [fear holds back
destiny](fear-holds-back-destiny.md) until the leap.

"There is nothing worth doing that is not scary." Post-leap, the same person
may need to become lobbyist, whistleblower, administrator — no longer
intimidated by bureaucracy because the defining battle with fear was already
fought.
