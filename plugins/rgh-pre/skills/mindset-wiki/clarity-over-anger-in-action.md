---
type: concept
title: Clarity Over Anger in Action
description: >
  Effective social action can come from love and clarity rather than stress
  and anger — confrontation without blame lets facts be heard where
  self-righteousness triggers defensiveness.
sources:
  - title: "Loving What Is, Revised Edition: Four Questions That Can Change Your Life"
    resource: "Loving What Is (Byron Katie), ch. 6"
---

"If I do The Work and I'm no longer fearful for the planet's welfare, why
would I get involved?" Answer: "Because that's what love does." The fear
of not being fearful — believing without stress you'd "just sit around in
bliss" — blocks beginners. "I am entirely motivated without anger. The
truth sets us free, and freedom acts."

Picking up a tin can in the desert can happen with anger about pollution or
"without any stress or judgment" — the latter is "spontaneous, clear, and
infinitely kind." "Clarity moves much more efficiently than violence and
stress. It doesn't make enemies along the way." Confronting a corporate
official with blame triggers fear and denial; speaking without stress, in
confidence that everything is as it should be right now, invites
collaboration: "How can the two of us make it better?"

"War teaches only war. You clean up your mental environment, and we'll
clean up our physical one much more quickly." Extends [clean your own
thinking before activism](clean-your-own-thinking-before-activism.md) and
[violence teaches violence](violence-teaches-violence.md). "Would you
rather be right or free?"
