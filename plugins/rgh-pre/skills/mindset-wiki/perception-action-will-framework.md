Marcus Aurelius: "Objective judgment, now at this very moment. Unselfish
action, now at this very moment. Willing acceptance — now at this very
moment — of all external events. That's all you need."

Overcoming obstacles is a discipline of three critical steps:

1. **Perception** — how you look at your specific problems (attitude and
   approach).
2. **Action** — the energy and creativity with which you break obstacles
   into opportunities.
3. **Will** — cultivating inner fortitude to handle defeat and difficulty
   (see [discipline of the will](discipline-of-will.md)).

These are interdependent, interconnected, and fluidly contingent — simple
but never easy. Great individuals find a way to transform weakness into
strength; obstacles become fuel for ambition "like oxygen to a fire" (see
[obstacle as fuel](obstacle-as-fuel.md)).

The reframed goal is not "How can I think this is not so bad?" but "how to
will yourself to see that this must be good" — not gushing optimism but
ceaseless creativity and opportunism: "I can make this good."

Pair with [paralysis before obstacles](paralysis-before-obstacles.md) and
[obstacle in the path becomes the path](obstacle-in-the-path-becomes-the-path.md).
