The Greek concepts apatheia and ataraxia — rendered as stillness or
equanimity — describe self-command through absence of irrational or
extreme emotions. This is not loss of feeling altogether, only loss of the
harmful, unhelpful kind: "Don't let the negativity in, don't let those
emotions even get started. Just say: No, thank you. I can't afford to
panic." Freedom from disturbance lets energy focus on solving problems
rather than reacting to them.

Real strength lies in domestication of emotions, not pretending they don't
exist — if you need a moment, take it. Crisis-trained calm is learnable:
Rockefeller treated the 1857 panic as a school — saving quietly, watching
others' mistakes, refusing seductive speculation — because the market's
vicious unpredictability was already assumed. The perceptual skill is
choosing not to give in to panic, fear, or powerlessness when biology
pushes toward fight-or-flight on problems that are not literal predators. The skill is keeping an "even
strain" when stakes are high: can you fight the urge to panic and focus
only on what you can change, on the task at hand?

Uncertainty and fear are relieved by authority — and training is
authority, a release valve. NASA astronauts rehearsed launch day hundreds
of times in graded exposures until unfamiliar sights and sounds became
natural; panic must be trained out. Unfamiliarity is simple (not easy) to
fix, and doing so increases tolerance for stress. See [distress
tolerance](distress-tolerance.md) for building this capacity in smaller
steps.

When anxiety rises, logic can help if used as questions toward root causes:
Is this catastrophic? Is it totally unexpected? Why be worked up over
something that at least occasionally happens? You've dealt with worse
before — resourcefulness beats anger. Gavin de Becker's check when
worrying: "What am I choosing to not see right now?" — what important
things are missed by choosing worry over alertness or wisdom?

Marcus Aurelius's filter: does what happened keep you from acting with
justice, generosity, self-control, sanity, prudence, honesty, humility,
straightforwardness? If not, get back to work. Pair with [does upset add
options test](does-upset-add-options-test.md) before indulging a strong
reaction.
