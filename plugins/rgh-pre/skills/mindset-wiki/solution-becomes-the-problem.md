---
type: concept
title: Solution Becomes the Problem
description: >
  Short-term coping strategies aimed at immediate relief — avoidance,
  suppression, reassurance-seeking, withdrawal — often maintain or worsen
  the emotional problem they were meant to solve.
sources:
  - title: "CBT For Dummies, 4th Edition"
    resource: "CBT For Dummies (Rhena Branch, Rob Willson), ch. 8: Problem-Maintaining Solutions"
---

Windy Dryden's summary: **"Feel better, get worse; feel worse, get better."**
Strategies driven by reducing distress now often reinforce the beliefs and
behaviors underpinning the problem. The route out is often the opposite of
the established coping strategy — exposure instead of avoidance, tolerating
discomfort instead of controlling the uncontrollable.

Common problem-maintaining solutions:
- **Avoidance** of feared situations — never learns you can cope.
- **Substances** to block feelings — adds dependence and hangovers.
- **Concealing shameful aspects** — chronic fear of being found out.
- **Procrastination** waiting to be "in the mood" — tasks weigh on the mind.

**Depression:** withdrawal, rumination on "why do I feel this way" or "if
only," daytime napping — reduce reinforcement and disrupt sleep. Alternative:
gradually increase scheduled activity.

**Control grip** (anxiety, OCD, anger, eating disorders): trying to limit
sensations, suppress thoughts, or monitor excessively increases focus on
being out of control. Experiment: try harder to stop blushing or a thought —
effort typically amplifies it.

**Intolerance of uncertainty:** reassurance-seeking, repeated checking,
superstitious rituals, risk avoidance, controlling others — each temporarily
relieves but erodes confidence and security long-term. Make friends with
uncertainty rather than eliminate it.

**Safety behaviors** in anxiety disorders prevent discovering that feared
catastrophes often don't happen, that you could cope if they did, and that
anxiety diminishes on its own when not fed. List avoidances and safety
behaviors as exposure targets.
