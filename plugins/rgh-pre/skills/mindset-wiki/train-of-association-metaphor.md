title: Train of Association Metaphor
description: >
  Mind-wandering is like hopping a train of association without noticing —
  you may wake up in a very different state of mind from where you boarded.
sources:
  - title: "Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy for Depression, 2nd Edition"
    resource: "Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy for Depression (Segal, Williams, Teasdale), ch. 14"
---

Joseph Goldstein's image for how thought carries us: we hop a train of
association not knowing we have boarded; somewhere down the line we wake up
and realize we have been thinking — taken for a ride — and when we step off,
we may be in a very different state of mind from where we jumped on.

This is one metaphor for [thoughts as mental events](thoughts-as-mental-events.md):
the practice is noticing you are on the train and stepping down, not arguing
with each station along the route. Related images include cinema screen, stage
with wings, and [sitting beside the thought stream](sitting-beside-the-thought-stream.md).
