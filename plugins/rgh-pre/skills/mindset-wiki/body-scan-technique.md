title: Body Scan Technique
description: >
  Sequentially directing attention through the body from head to toe,
  noticing sensation without trying to fix anything, is a structured entry
  point into present-moment attention and a way to surface physical
  tension before it's consciously registered.

Sitting upright with feet grounded, attention moves in sequence through
the body — forehead and scalp tension, eyes, jaw, throat, stomach, chest
and breath capacity, neck, shoulders, back, legs, feet — pausing briefly
at each to notice whatever sensation is present without judging it or
trying to change it. Running the full sequence a second time immediately
after the first often surfaces sensations missed on the first pass, once
attention has settled into the practice.

This is a concrete, structured version of the more general capacity
[mindfulness](mindfulness.md) and [deliberate attention
practice](deliberate-attention-practice.md) describe, applied specifically
to bodily sensation rather than to thought or surroundings. It also
functions diagnostically: because tension can accumulate without being
consciously noticed until it's already significant (a clenched jaw, a
raised shoulder held for hours), a regular scan catches it earlier than
waiting to notice discomfort on its own. Once a specific area of tension
or discomfort is located, the natural extension is to ask what that part
of the body actually needs — a stretch, a change of position, warmth,
rest — and to act on the simple answer directly, rather than only noting
the sensation and moving on; this pairs the scan with the same [somatic
regulation of emotion](somatic-regulation-of-emotion.md) principle that
deliberately changing physical state can shift what follows from it.

For [alexithymia as emotional
blindspot](alexithymia-as-emotional-blindspot.md), a bottom-up variant
helps: sit comfortably, let go of reasoning, focus on breathing, then scan
region by region (feet through face) noting temperature, tension, softness,
soreness, breathing quality, heartbeat, and overall body state — then attach
**any** word to the resulting mood, standard or not. Difficulty completing
the scan or shutting down at naming is informative, not failure.
