title: Beginning Again in Meditation
description: >
  Mind-wandering during meditation is not failure — each return of attention
  to the breath is the heart of the practice, to be repeated good-naturedly
  as many times as needed.
sources:
  - title: "The Mindful Way through Depression: Freeing Yourself from Chronic Unhappiness"
    resource: "The Mindful Way through Depression (Williams, Teasdale, Segal, Kabat-Zinn), ch. 4"
  - title: "Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy for Depression, 2nd Edition"
    resource: "Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy for Depression (Segal, Williams, Teasdale), ch. 10"
---

You cannot force the mind — suppression backfires (white-bear effect; effort
to keep negatives out correlates with more depression later). The route is
**intention, not force**: gently refocus on a neutral anchor like [breath as
attention anchor](breath-as-attention-anchor.md), congratulating yourself when
you notice wandering, briefly labeling ("thinking, thinking"), and escorting
attention back.

Each drift is a chance to recognize slipping from [being mode](doing-mode-being-mode.md)
into [driven-doing mode](driven-doing-mode.md) and release back. Self-critical
reactions to wandering are themselves just more weather patterns — see
[labeling judgment in MBCT](labeling-judgment-in-mbct.md) for the relational
shift of naming standards-thoughts as judgment rather than fighting them.
Muddy water clears when you stop stirring — direct control often stirs the mind
further.

**Trying to control thoughts** is a common misreading: pushing thoughts away
or squashing them tends to make them bounce back more strongly. Develop gentle
awareness — recognize "Here is thinking," let go, refocus on breath. The
practice is feeling comfortable letting things be as they are, then returning —
not control. Struggling to maintain breath focus is itself a feeling to notice
before gently returning.

**Beginning again** means each in-breath is a new beginning, each out-breath a
letting go — not an error but the practice itself. If the mind wanders a hundred
times, bring it back a hundred times. Expectations of calm every session co-opt
practice into doing mode (Tyra's irritation at thwarted "feeling good" goal).

Pairs with [coming back to mindfulness](coming-back-to-mindfulness.md) and
[thoughts need engagement to persist](thoughts-need-engagement-to-persist.md).
