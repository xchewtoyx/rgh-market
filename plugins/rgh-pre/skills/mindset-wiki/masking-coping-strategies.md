title: Masking Coping Strategies
description: >
  When chronic camouflage exhausts capacity, masked autistic people often
  adopt secondary coping — substances, disordered eating, dissociation,
  rigid rules, or fawning — each serving predictable psychological functions.
sources:
  - title: "Unmasking Autism Audiobook Companion"
    resource: "Unmasking Autism Audiobook Companion (Devon Price, PhD), Masking Mechanics worksheets (p. 10)"
  - title: "The Unmasking Workbook for Autistic Adults"
    resource: "The Unmasking Workbook for Autistic Adults (Jessica Penot), ch. 5"
---

**Problem drinking or substance use:** dulls sensory overload; "liquid
courage" for social threat; relaxes inhibitions; supplies energy for an
overdemanding world; stimulates senses; entertains anxious rumination;
silences inner critic.

**Eating-disordered behavior:** grounds life in rituals; physical stimulation
via hunger, exercise, or purging; distracts from social struggle; defines
worthiness through appearance or control; explains away gender dysphoria or
dissociation from the body.

**Detachment and dissociation:** reject others before being rejected; blunt
grief and regret; focus only on narrow strengths; avoid learning difficult
emotional skills; silence needs others find bothersome; preserve limited
energy. See [grounding techniques](grounding-techniques.md) and [window
of tolerance](window-of-tolerance.md) for regulated alternatives.

**Rigid rules and belief systems:** make confusing reality concrete;
translate vague norms into checklists; create in-group belonging; daily
soothing ritual; soothe fear of being a bad person; promise escape from an
unjust present.

**Fawning and compulsive people-pleasing:** earns praise; false promise of
acceptance; simplifies relationships to always say yes; validates ignoring
own needs; minimizes conflict and others' anger. With [rejection
sensitivity](rejection-sensitivity.md), this can become extreme — doing
miserable activities to avoid dislike rather than tolerating disapproval
(see [courage to be disliked](courage-to-be-disliked.md)).

These are **propping up the mask** when [masking persona
compensation](masking-persona-compensation.md) alone is unsustainable —
often precursors to [autistic burnout as wellbeing
deficit](autistic-burnout-as-wellbeing-deficit.md). Recovery paths include
[reducing demands over trying harder](reducing-demands-over-trying-harder.md)
and [trait label reframing](trait-label-reframing.md).
