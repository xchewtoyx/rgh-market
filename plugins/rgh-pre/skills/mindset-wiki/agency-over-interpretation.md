---
type: concept
title: Agency Over Interpretation
description: >
  Even when circumstances are not chosen, the interpretation applied to
  them remains yours — and treating a situation as hopeless is itself a
  decision, not an automatic consequence of the event.
sources:
  - title: "The Obstacle Is the Way: The Timeless Art of Turning Trials into Triumph, Expanded 10th Anniversary Edition"
    resource: "The Obstacle Is the Way (Ryan Holiday), ch. 5"
---

We decide whether to break or resist, assent or reject. No one can force
belief that a situation is hopeless. Perceptions are what remain fully
within reach even when externals are not — captors can jail, label, and
strip possessions, but not control thoughts, beliefs, or reactions.
Having little power is not the same as being powerless: even in prison,
mind and time can remain one's own.

Rubin "Hurricane" Carter, wrongly convicted, told the warden he would not
surrender the last thing he controlled: himself. He refused uniform, prison
food, visitors, parole hearings, and commissary work to shorten his
sentence — redirecting energy into law, philosophy, and history. Nineteen
years later, freed, he sought no apology or civil suit, because that would
imply the ordeal had taken something owed: "I decide how it will affect
me. No one else has the right."

Situations by themselves are neither good nor bad — judgment is brought
via perception. "There is nothing either good or bad, but thinking makes
it so." The same hardship can read as adventure (Laura Ingalls Wilder on
pioneer life) or as being "screwed" — which is how obstacles become
obstacles. Through perception, people are complicit in creating and
destroying their own obstacles.

This is the agency side of [the happiness
equation](happiness-equation.md) and [noticing the story your mind is
telling](noticing-the-story-your-mind-is-telling.md): the event and the
story about it are separable. An employee's costly mistake can be
catastrophe or training — identical event, different evaluation. That the
mind or others label something awful does not obligate agreement. The
power of interpretation applies in every situation, cannot be obstructed
by others, and can only be relinquished — and relinquishing it is itself a
decision. Pair with [equanimity under
pressure](equanimity-under-pressure.md) for keeping that agency when
emotions surge.
