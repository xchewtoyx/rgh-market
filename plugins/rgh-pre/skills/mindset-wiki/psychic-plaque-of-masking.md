"Long-term camouflaging and masking leaves behind a kind of psychic plaque
in the mental and emotional arteries. Like the buildup of physical plaque
over time can result in a heart attack or stroke, the buildup of this
psychic plaque over time can result in burnout."

Masking was often a necessary survival tool — monitoring body language,
tone, word choice, topics, suppressing movements, internalizing sensory
struggle on top of the actual task. A "just a chat" can cost more energy
than hours of professional performance. Studies link increased masking with
exhaustion, depression, anxiety, and suicidality.

Extends [masking as a burnout
mechanism](masking-as-burnout-mechanism.md) and [autistic burnout as
wellbeing deficit](autistic-burnout-as-wellbeing-deficit.md). Unmasking is
not instant — see [unmasking as ongoing
process](unmasking-as-ongoing-process.md).
