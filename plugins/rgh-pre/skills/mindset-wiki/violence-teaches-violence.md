---
type: concept
title: Violence Teaches Violence
description: >
  Anger-driven action models the very pattern it opposes — stress teaches
  stress, while peace teaches peace; internal imbalance undermines outward
  reform.
sources:
  - title: "Loving What Is, Revised Edition: Four Questions That Can Change Your Life"
    resource: "Loving What Is (Byron Katie), ch. 6"
---

"Violence teaches only violence. Stress teaches stress. And peace teaches
peace." Coming at people self-righteously reads as "the enemy coming" and
triggers defensiveness; coming clearly lets them hear what they already
know and see you as "loving and attractive," "someone to be trusted."

"How can an internally imbalanced, frustrated woman teach others how to
clean up their act? We have to learn that ourselves first, and that begins
from within." The belief "if we don't suffer, we won't care" makes
champions of suffering — only for a good cause. Turnaround: take care of
yourself; when your mental environment is balanced, then be the expert who
can balance the planet "fearlessly, caringly, and effectively."

Extends [clean your own thinking before
activism](clean-your-own-thinking-before-activism.md) and [clarity over
anger in action](clarity-over-anger-in-action.md). "For me, peace is
entirely efficient."
