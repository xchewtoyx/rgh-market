type: concept
title: Personal Fiction of Knowing
description: >
  The unexamined assumption that current thoughts and opinions are the truth
  about inner and outer reality — a fiction that veils the ground you are
  standing on and blocks learning from the present.
sources:
  - title: "Wherever You Go, There You Are: Mindfulness Meditation in Everyday Life"
    resource: "Wherever You Go, There You Are (Jon Kabat-Zinn), Part 1"
---

A key unexamined assumption: we fall into believing that what we are thinking —
our ideas and opinions at any given time — is **the truth** about what is
"out there" in the world and "in here" in our minds. "Most of the time, it
just isn't so."

We lock ourselves into a **personal fiction** that we already know who we are,
where we are, where we're going, and what's happening — while enshrouded in
thoughts, fantasies, and impulses about past and future, about what we want
and fear, that veil our direction and "the very ground we are standing on."

The fallout of ignoring the present accumulates silently, coloring life without
our knowing. Meditation is about waking from such dreams — and from the
nightmares they can become. Paying attention to the present is the only time
we have to live, grow, feel, and change.

versus thinking](awareness-versus-thinking.md).
