title: Universal Stressful Thoughts
description: >
  Stressful thoughts recycle across minds, languages, and cultures — there
  are no new stories — so anyone's inquiry can be your inquiry when you
  find where the same pattern lives in you.
sources:
  - title: "Loving What Is, Revised Edition: Four Questions That Can Change Your Life"
    resource: "Loving What Is (Byron Katie), ch. 3"
---

The same stressful thoughts arise in every mind, in every language and
country — "there are no new stories. They're all recycled." That is why
anyone's Work can be your Work: a dialogue about a mother, boss, spouse, or
stranger is not about their unique situation but about a thought pattern
you also carry.

This underpins [reading dialogues as your own
work](reading-dialogues-as-your-own-work.md): when you cannot relate to a
participant's example, substitute someone significant in your own life —
friend, partner, parent, child, colleague — and the inquiry often lands the
same way. Doing a whole worksheet on your mother can later improve your
relationship with your daughter because the same unexamined thoughts were
attached to both, even when you were not aware of it.

The practical implication: you never need the "perfect" triggering person
to begin [inquiry as thought-testing](inquiry-as-thought-testing.md) —
the thought is the material, not the cast of characters.
