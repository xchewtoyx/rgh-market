type: concept
title: Mindfulness Pot for Difficult Feelings
description: >
  Holding anger or other strong emotions in mindful awareness — like food
  slowly cooking in a pot — lets them digest without immediately projecting
  them outward or forcing the world to conform.
sources:
  - title: "Wherever You Go, There You Are: Mindfulness Meditation in Everyday Life"
    resource: "Wherever You Go, There You Are (Jon Kabat-Zinn), Part 8"
---

When annoyance or anger arises, notice that language like something "made"
you angry surrenders power to others. Reframe the occasion as a chance to
use mindfulness as **a pot into which you can put all your feelings and
just be with them** — you don't have to do anything with them right away;
they become "more cooked, more easily digested and understood" by being held.

Key moves: observe that feelings are creations of your mind's view, which
may be incomplete; ask whether you can allow this state without making
yourself right or wrong; build patience to put stronger emotions in the pot
rather than projecting them outward; know yourself in new ways and free
yourself from old limiting views.

This extends [awareness versus thinking](awareness-versus-thinking.md)'s
waterfall image — awareness larger than anger can hold it the way a pot
contains food — and [anger and self-righteous attachment](anger-and-self-righteous-attachment.md)'s
cost of indulging righteousness. See [distress tolerance](distress-tolerance.md)
for bearing discomfort without immediate action.
