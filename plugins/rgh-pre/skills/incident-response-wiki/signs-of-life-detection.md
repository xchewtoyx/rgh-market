  Diagnostic procedure for human dormancy during crisis: finding one remaining
  anchor of care, hope, or purpose that confirms recovery is still possible.

"Dormancy is not death; it only looks like it." During acute crisis — burnout,
grief, addiction collapse, suicidal drive — a person can feel dead inside:
numb, hopeless, joyless; best part of the day is falling asleep, worst is
waking; moving like ghosts, present but disconnected. That state is human
dormancy, not terminal absence. If there is still breath, the story is not
over — but dormancy feels so much like death that people believe there is no
way back. [Incident detection thresholds](incident-detection-thresholds.md)
must distinguish dormancy from giving up.

**Five-step gardener's diagnostic:**

1. **Check beneath the surface.** Like a twig that looks brittle but shows
   green when scratched: is there still something you care about, however
   small — a partner, child, dream, hope, creative spark?
2. **Notice the conditions.** Dormancy often follows shock — grief, burnout,
   addiction, loss. Having been through something big does not mean you are
   gone; it may mean you are recovering.
3. **Watch for small shoots.** Any laughter, intrigue, joy, or happiness
   counts — a chance meeting, the sky looking bluer, a paramedic's offhand
   suggestion, children's faces breaking through at the moment of intent to
   crash.
4. **Remember seasons change.** Not feeling joy today does not mean it will
   not return; numbness often sits atop unprocessed grief, stress, or past
   material.
5. **Hold on to your sign of life.** Rox: passion for writing songs after a
   self-harm crisis. Rich: his children's faces at 120mph on the motorway.
   A sign can be a face, phrase, song, or simply having woken up today —
   cling to it as proof you are still here and ready for something different.

**Operational use:** signs of life are stabilisation anchors, not substitutes
for treatment. Rich disclosed addiction and stopped gambling; Rox negotiated
therapy terms after self-harm — both needed escalation and ongoing recovery.
When no sign of life can be found despite searching, or intent to act on
self-harm is present, follow [incident escalation path](incident-escalation-path.md)
immediately. When a sign appears, use it to choose [post-incident
recovery](post-incident-recovery.md) and [relapse early warning and action
plan](relapse-early-warning-and-action-plan.md) over irreversible action in
the acute moment.
