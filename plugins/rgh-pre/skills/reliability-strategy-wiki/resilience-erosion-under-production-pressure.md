---
type: concept
title: Resilience Erosion Under Production Pressure
description: >
  When acute production demands override the buffer that should protect
  chronic safety margins — through absent expertise, fear of delay, or
  culture that punishes sacrifice — the system drifts toward catastrophe
  while everyone acts riskier than they would consciously choose.
sources:
  - title: "Resilience Engineering: Concepts and Precepts"
    resource: "Resilience Engineering: Concepts and Precepts (Hollnagel, Woods, Leveson, eds.), ch. 14"
---

Organisational resilience is managing activities to anticipate threats and
**balance safety against primary performance goals**. At the individual
level this becomes managerial (or personal) ability to make
[sacrifice judgements](sacrifice-judgements.md). Erosion happens when:

- the protective buffer disappears (expert dies, authority overrides, no one
  dares delay launch — Vasa, Challenger, Columbia),
- threats are not diagnosed (risk-blind despite warnings),
- threats are seen but not acted on (Piper Alpha platforms kept feeding a
  fire),
- assertiveness fails upward ("take off your engineering hat and put on your
  management hat").

**Three component skills** when production and safety conflict:

1. **Diagnosis** — notice drift toward the risk boundary; situation
   awareness, not optimism.
2. **Decision** — choose the action that reduces threat once diagnosed
   (abort approach, dump mud to save rig, personal slowdown).
3. **Assertiveness** — persuade seniors or peers that production must yield;
   hardest when culture penalizes challenge.

Skills require **commitment to safety as priority** when goals clash — stated
policy ("safety has priority") is worthless if climate surveys show most
workers feel production wins and speaking up is hassle.

For personal PRE, erosion is the pattern where
[downward vs. upward resilience](downward-vs-upward-resilience.md) fails:
no named chronic priorities, heroism compensating for missing structure, and
penalty when sacrifice looked unnecessary in hindsight. Counter with explicit
[acute vs. chronic objectives](acute-vs-chronic-objectives.md),
[chronic-objective measurement asymmetry](chronic-objective-measurement-asymmetry.md)
proxies, and culture that rewards the courageous no even when nothing broke.
