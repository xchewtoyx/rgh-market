---
type: concept
title: Chronic-Objective Measurement Asymmetry
description: >
  Acute objectives broadcast strong, familiar metrics while chronic
  protection lacks leading indicators — making chronic goals easier to
  defer until crisis, and making "invest in safety when least affordable"
  the default trap under production pressure.
sources:
  - title: "Resilience Engineering in Practice: A Guidebook"
    resource: "Resilience Engineering in Practice (Hollnagel, Pariès, Woods, Wreathall, eds.), ch. 12"
---

Production and efficiency have long measurement histories: throughput, cost,
schedule, visible output. **Safety and other chronic objectives** send
weaker signals — fewer leading indicators, harder to relate resources
invested to margin gained (Gaba). Economic performance shouts; erosion of
recovery capacity, relationship slack, or health margins whispers until
something breaks.

This asymmetry worsens trade-off problems. Without good chronic indicators,
managers (and future-you under stress) optimize what is measured — cost
reduction, timeliness, clearing the queue — absent warnings that safety
margins are thinning. "Best practice" imports from high-performing peers
can stack competing goals until the system is less resilient, not more
(Woods).

The efficiency–thoroughness trade-off (ETTO): given scarce time, workforce,
and money, it is never possible to be fully thorough and fully efficient;
dominant concern tilts the balance. Under rising acute tempo, **resilience
investment is most needed when least affordable** — precisely when pressure
to economize makes chronic spending feel like waste.

For PRE, countermeasures include: deliberate chronic proxies (sleep debt,
irritability, skipped meals, connection time) even when crude; treating
them as yardsticks whose deviation is a warning, not noise; and expecting
[sacrifice judgements](sacrifice-judgements.md) to look costly on acute
dashboards. Pair with
[downward vs. upward resilience](downward-vs-upward-resilience.md) so
chronic priorities are named before the asymmetry bites, and
[outcomes vs. activity as the evaluation signal](outcomes-vs-activity-signal.md)
so visible busyness does not substitute for chronic health.
