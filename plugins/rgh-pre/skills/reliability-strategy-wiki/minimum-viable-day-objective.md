---
type: concept
title: Minimum Viable Day Objective
description: >
  Under reduced capacity, set the day's goal at what is realistically
  achievable — not the full aspirational list — so chronic protection does
  not collapse into dread, freeze, and deadline-only action.
sources:
  - title: "How to Be You: Simple Ways to Love Who You Are"
    resource: "How to Be You (Ellie Middleton), ch. 6, Tip 28"
---

When burnout or low functioning means only ~20% of a ten-item list will
actually happen, judging against all ten guarantees failure, Sunday panic,
Monday dread, and action only when deadlines force it — worsening stress
without improving output.

**Bare minimum** reframing: declare the realistic subset (e.g., two tasks) as
the *entire* goal for the day. Completing them is 100%, not 20% of a fantasy
list; momentum may then carry optional extras as bonus, not as the bar for
self-worth.

This is a sacrifice judgement in miniature — explicitly relaxing acute
throughput expectations to protect chronic sustainability. It pairs with
[non-negotiable protection targets](non-negotiable-protection-targets.md)
(which define what still must happen) and
[demand triage by droppability](demand-triage-by-droppability.md) when
capacity is structurally reduced, not just mood-low.
