type: concept
title: Personal Priority Over Social Default
description: >
  "Struggling to prioritize" often means refusing society's default ranking
  of work, relationships, or hustle — valid PRE objective-setting starts
  from what genuinely matters to this person, not expected hierarchies.
sources:
  - title: "How to Be You: Simple Ways to Love Who You Are"
    resource: "How to Be You (Ellie Middleton), ch. 6, Tip 29"
---

When neurodivergent people are told they "can't prioritize," the problem may
not be executive dysfunction alone — they may simply **not want** work as top
priority, not rank a given relationship above their own wellbeing, or weigh
tasks differently from capitalist or heteronormative defaults. Autistic people
especially often place less weight on societal norms, so career-above-all or
a single relationship as the happiness source may not match their actual
objective set.

Prioritization is perspective-dependent. The reliability-strategy move is to
make that perspective explicit before judging interventions: what is worth
keeping available *for this person* — safety, basic care, relationships,
meaningful work, autonomy, recovery — not what others expect. Techniques that
help without prescribing content:

- **Word of the month** — one theme (rest, connections, balance) guides
  trade-offs for a bounded period instead of pretending to optimize everything
  at once.
- **Horizon cascade** — five-year vision → one year → three months → this
  month, so the immediate priority connects to stated long-term objectives
  rather than whatever shouted today.

For social life specifically, see
[chosen relationship objectives](chosen-relationship-objectives.md) and
[minimum-masking trade-off](minimum-masking-trade-off.md).

Pair with [main thing as priority anchor](main-thing-as-priority-anchor.md)
and [goal attainability as a satisfaction driver](goal-attainability-as-satisfaction-driver.md):
objectives should be both personally chosen and attainable enough to sustain
life evaluation without chronic shortfall.
