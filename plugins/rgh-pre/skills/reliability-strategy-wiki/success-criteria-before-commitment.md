---
type: concept
title: Success Criteria Before Commitment
description: >
  Clarify why a project exists and what done looks like before scheduling
  work — then map backward from the end state so trade-offs serve stated
  objectives instead of drifting task-by-task.
sources:
  - title: "How to Be You: Simple Ways to Love Who You Are"
    resource: "How to Be You (Ellie Middleton), ch. 6, Tip 30"
---

Before accepting or extending commitment to a project, answer:

- Why are we doing this? Why this way?
- What are the objectives?
- What does success look like?

**Start with the end**: visualize the final deliverable, then work backward
through milestones and dependencies (e.g., marketing needs the printed
artifact; printing needs two weeks — slot that before the launch date). Stumbling
blocks will still appear; the end vision keeps prioritization honest when
distraction or scope creep arrives.

For PRE this is objective-setting before controls: you cannot judge whether an
intervention improved sustainable quality of life without naming the target
state first. Pair with [main thing as priority anchor](main-thing-as-priority-anchor.md)
and [outcomes vs. activity as the evaluation signal](outcomes-vs-activity-signal.md)
— backward planning ties daily activity to an outcome definition, not busyness.
