---
type: concept
title: Non-Negotiable Protection Targets
description: >
  Named baseline needs or practices that stay protected regardless of other
  demands — explicit chronic objectives that prevent "temporary" sacrifice
  from becoming permanent erosion.
sources:
  - title: "How to Be You: Simple Ways to Love Who You Are"
    resource: "How to Be You (Ellie Middleton), ch. 6, Tip 29"
---

Some protection targets are **non-negotiable**: what you need to feel your
best, kept on the calendar even when work "should" take priority. Example
pattern: daily reading and movement that prevent frazzle and burnout — pushed
aside during a busy launch, then restored with explicit priority (including
declining a meeting to protect step-count time), reframed as protecting the
career long-term, not indulgence.

Non-negotiables are a personal downward-resilience rule: they communicate
intent to future-you under acute pressure so every demand does not reopen the
full trade-off debate. They implement
[acute vs. chronic objectives](acute-vs-chronic-objectives.md) at the
individual level — chronic protection named before the inbox arrives.

Choosing them requires honesty about what actually sustains the system (see
[self-care domains as protection targets](self-care-domains-as-protection-targets.md)),
not what looks virtuous. When capacity is genuinely reduced, scale the day's
commitment (see [minimum viable day objective](minimum-viable-day-objective.md))
rather than abandoning non-negotiables silently without a conscious decision.
