---
type: concept
title: You Cannot Escape Yourself
description: >
  The claim that changing jobs, places, or relationships cannot outrun your
  own patterns of seeing and behaving — transformation requires facing the
  present situation, not romantic escape elsewhere.
sources:
  - title: "Wherever You Go, There You Are: Mindfulness Meditation in Everyday Life"
    resource: "Jon Kabat-Zinn, Part 6"
---

There is no running away from anything — sooner or later, things you do not
want to deal with catch up, especially old patterns and fears. The romantic
notion is that the problem is external — the job, spouse, town, children —
so changing circumstance will let you start over. This ignores that you carry
your head, heart, and [karma](karma-as-accumulated-tendency.md) with you.
You cannot escape yourself. If problems stem largely from your own patterns of
seeing, thinking, and behaving, the same problems arise wherever you go. Lives
cease working because we cease working at life — unwillingness to take
responsibility and work with difficulties, even though clarity and
transformation are possible right in the middle of what is here and now.

Meditation confers no immunity: people hop techniques, teachers, traditions,
chasing a special high — serious delusion, an unending quest to escape what is
closest to home. Retreats can become retreat from life in the world; the
resulting transformation only skin deep before reverting to the same pattern.
There is no successful escaping from yourself in the long run, only
transformation — whether the vehicle is drugs, meditation, alcohol, divorce,
or quitting your job. Growth requires the present situation to be faced
completely, opened to with mindfulness, allowing the roughness of the
situation to sand down your own rough edges.

If you imagine a cave, monastery, beach, or retreat would be better — when you
arrived, there you would be, with the same mind, body, and breath. The cave
gets lonely; the beach turns rainy; the retreat brings dislike of teachers or
food. Let go and admit you might as well be at home wherever you are. Right in
that moment, touch the core of your being — then every place offers its true
richness. Pair with [non-doing and wu wei](non-doing-and-wu-wei.md) and
[impermanence of self and non-attachment](impermanence-of-self-and-non-attachment.md).
