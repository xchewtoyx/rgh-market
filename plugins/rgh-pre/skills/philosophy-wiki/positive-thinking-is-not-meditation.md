title: Positive Thinking Is Not Meditation
description: >
  The claim that deciding to think positively may be useful but is not
  meditation — it is just more thinking, and can be as confining and
  imprisoning as negative thinking unless something beyond thought transforms
  the relationship to mind.
sources:
  - title: "Wherever You Go, There You Are: Mindfulness Meditation in Everyday Life"
    resource: "Jon Kabat-Zinn, Part 4"
---

If we decide to think positively, that may be useful, but it is not meditation.
It is just more thinking. You can become as much a prisoner of positive
thinking as of negative thinking — it too can be confining, fragmented,
inaccurate, illusory, self-serving, and wrong. Transformation requires another
element altogether, beyond the limits of thought.

This distinction matters because [awareness beyond thinking](awareness-beyond-thinking.md)
is the vessel that holds thought without being identical to it — watching
without being drawn in, letting patterns change through understanding rather
than substituting one narrative for another. The Stoic discipline of
[discipline of perception](discipline-of-perception.md) similarly filters
prejudice and excitement to see events as they are, but Kabat-Zinn's frame
emphasizes a non-thinking awareness that is not another thought-strategy.

Pair with [world as self-projection](world-as-self-projection.md) on uninvestigated
stories operating as theories, and [arguing with reality causes
suffering](arguing-with-reality-causes-suffering.md) on the layer added to bare
fact — positive stories can be as untested as negative ones.
