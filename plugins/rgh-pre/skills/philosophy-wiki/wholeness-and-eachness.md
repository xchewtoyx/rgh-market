---
type: concept
title: Wholeness and Eachness
description: >
  The claim that direct experience of oneness does not flatten difference —
  wholeness and the unique suchness of each particular are interwoven, and
  the particulars count.
sources:
  - title: "Wherever You Go, There You Are: Mindfulness Meditation in Everyday Life"
    resource: "Jon Kabat-Zinn, Part 7"
---

When we are in touch with being whole, we feel at one with everything; when we
feel at one with everything, we feel whole ourselves. A taste of
[interconnectedness](interconnectedness.md) brings deep knowledge of belonging,
a sense of being an intimate part of things, at home wherever we are — alongside
the fleeting brevity of this life. Wholeness is the root of health, healing,
and holy in our language. When we perceive intrinsic wholeness, there is truly
no place to go and nothing to do, freeing us to choose our own path.

Wholeness experienced firsthand cannot be tyrannical, for it is infinite in
diversity and finds itself mirrored in each particular — like Indra's net,
each jewel reflecting and containing the whole. Using unity to steamroller and
flatten differences misses the point; it is in the unique qualities of
this-and-that — their eachness and suchness — that all poetry, art, science,
wonder, grace, and richness reside. The ocean is whole yet has countless unique
waves; life on earth is whole yet expresses itself in unique time-bound bodies.

There can be no one place to be, no one way to be, practice, learn, love,
grow, heal, live, feel, or know. The particulars count. Pair with [soul versus
spirit](soul-versus-spirit.md) (descent into eachness as well as ascent toward
unity) and [spirituality as wholeness](spirituality-as-wholeness.md).
