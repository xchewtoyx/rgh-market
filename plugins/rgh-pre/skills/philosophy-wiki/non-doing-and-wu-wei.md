type: concept
title: Non-Doing and Wu Wei
description: >
  The Taoist and Zen claim that the highest mastery — in stillness or action —
  arises from non-doing: effortless activity in which the doer does not force
  outcomes yet nothing is left undone.
sources:
  - title: "Wherever You Go, There You Are: Mindfulness Meditation in Everyday Life"
    resource: "Jon Kabat-Zinn, Part 2"
---

Sitting to meditate is a time for non-doing — not the same as doing nothing;
consciousness and intention are key. Formal meditation makes time for stopping
outward activity and cultivating stillness, with no agenda beyond being fully
present. The joy of non-doing: nothing else needs to happen for the moment to
be complete; wisdom lies in knowing something else surely will happen regardless.
American culture values doing and progress, making this hard to grasp — even
leisure tends to be busy and mindless.

Non-doing arises within action as well as stillness: the inward stillness of the
doer merges with outward activity so the action does itself — effortless
activity, nothing forced, no exertion of will, no small "I/me/mine" claiming a
result, yet nothing left undone. Chuang Tzu's cook explains he no longer sees
the ox as a mass; his whole being apprehends, spirit free to work without plan,
following the natural line so the cleaver finds its own way — the blade stays
sharp after nineteen years. Prince Wen Hui: "This is it! My cook has shown me
how I ought to live my own life!"

Non-doing is not indolence — it takes great courage and energy to cultivate.
It means letting things be and allowing them to unfold in their own way:
graceful, knowledgeable, effortless effort, a "doerless doing" cultivated over
a lifetime. Meditation is synonymous with practicing non-doing — not to make
things perfect, but to grasp that things already are perfectly what they are,
then act from clarity. Thoreau: "To affect the quality of the day, that is the
highest of arts." Pair with [soul versus spirit](soul-versus-spirit.md) (Zen's
return to the ordinary) and [you cannot escape yourself](you-cannot-escape-yourself.md)
(transformation here, not elsewhere).
