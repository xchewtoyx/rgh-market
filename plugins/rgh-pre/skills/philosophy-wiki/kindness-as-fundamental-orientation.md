type: concept
title: Kindness as Fundamental Orientation
description: >
  The claim that kindness is not a technique for changing the world but an
  uncovering of love and compassion already present below fear, hurt, greed,
  and the illusion of separateness — an orientation that can stretch to all
  beings without natural limit.
sources:
  - title: "Wherever You Go, There You Are: Mindfulness Meditation in Everyday Life"
    resource: "Jon Kabat-Zinn, Part 6"
---

Loving kindness practice can extend beyond individuals to whole groups — toward
all who are oppressed, who suffer, or whose lives are caught up in war,
violence, or hatred — recognizing they too have loved ones, hopes, and needs for
shelter, food, and peace. It can extend further to the planet itself: its
environment, streams, rivers, air, oceans, forests, plants, and animals,
collectively or singly. There is really no natural limit to the practice — it is
an ongoing, ever-expanding realization of [interconnectedness](interconnectedness.md);
it is also its embodiment.

Loving one tree, flower, dog, place, or person — or oneself, for even one
moment — lets you find all people, all places, all suffering, all harmony in
that one moment. The practice is not about trying to change anything or get
anywhere, even though it can look that way. It is uncovering what is always
present: love and kindness are here all the time, buried below our own fears
and hurts, below our greed and our hatreds, below our desperate clinging to the
illusion that we are truly separate and alone.

Invoking these feelings stretches against the edges of our own ignorance, just
as yoga stretches against the resistance of muscle, ligament, and tendon — and
in the stretching, we expand, we grow, we change ourselves, we change the world.
The Dalai Lama: "My religion is kindness." Important caution: you are not
trying to help anyone or the planet — you are holding them in awareness,
honoring them, wishing them well, opening to their pain with kindness,
compassion, and acceptance. If the practice calls you to act differently in the
world, let those actions too embody kindness and [mindfulness as the art of
living](mindfulness-as-art-of-living.md). Pair with [ahimsa (non-harming)](ahimsa-non-harming.md)
on living so as to cause as little damage as possible.
