---
type: concept
title: Immediate Start Reward
description: >
  Attach a small pleasure available only once work has begun, because
  distant future payoffs do not motivate brains that experience time as now
  or not now.
sources:
  - title: "How to Be You: Simple Ways to Love Who You Are"
    resource: "How to Be You (Ellie Middleton), ch. 7"
---

Finishing a six-week fitness goal is too abstract to start today's session.
Pair starting with an immediate reinforcer: coffee allowed only after opening
email; comfort show only while working; snack only once tidying has begun.
The reward triggers at initiation, not completion — bridging the gap until
task engagement supplies its own momentum.

A personal-scale [motivational prosthesis](motivational-prosthesis.md).
Combine with interest, novelty, challenge, or urgency (playlist timers,
accountability, artificial deadlines) when one reinforcer is not enough.
