---
type: concept
title: Master Storage Directory
description: >
  Maintain a single written index of where important but hidden items are
  stored, so location tracking does not consume working memory.
sources:
  - title: "How to Be You: Simple Ways to Love Who You Are"
    resource: "How to Be You (Ellie Middleton), ch. 1"
---

Passports, birth certificates, spare keys, and similar items cannot stay on
the counter. Without an external index, their locations must be held in
working memory — and get lost after a "safe place" move. A master directory
(spreadsheet, phone note, or dedicated notepad) records each item and its
exact storage spot, kept somewhere memorable itself.

Be specific ("blue folder, spare-room shelf") and minimize the number of
distinct hiding places. The directory externalizes the "where is it?" layer
so brain capacity can go to other demands — the same principle as
[tiered to-do structures](tiered-todo-structure.md) for tasks and [visible
storage](visible-storage-for-object-permanence.md) for daily-use items.
