type: concept
title: Personal Accommodation Planning List
description: >
  Draft a written list of specific workplace accommodations to discuss with
  a manager, prepared before the conversation, when lifelong without support
  makes it hard to know what to ask for.
sources:
  - title: "Unmasked: The Ultimate Guide to ADHD, Autism and Neurodivergence for Women"
    resource: "Unmasked (Ellie Middleton), ch. 9"
---

Late-diagnosed or long-undiagnosed people often lack a mental catalogue of
reasonable supports — they've compensated without naming needs. Imposter
thoughts ("I made it this far without accommodations") block even
considering options. Reframe accommodations as access to support always
needed, not a special advantage.

Before a manager meeting, write a personal list starting from example
categories: timing flex (breaks, compressed weeks, shifted commute),
environment (quiet desk, permanent seat, decompression room, hybrid work),
communication (written agendas, [what-by-when-and-why
instructions](what-by-when-and-why-instructions.md)), and tools (captions,
noise-cancelling headphones, visual timers, dictation). The list is a
working document, not a demand — it lowers the in-meeting executive load of
inventing options on the spot. A [shareable needs profile](shareable-needs-profile.md)
can carry the same content to future managers; a [pre-scripted accommodation
request](pre-scripted-accommodation-request.md) stocks phrases for asking.

Where employers publish a [proactive support menu](proactive-support-menu.md),
the individual list maps onto offered options instead of starting from zero.
