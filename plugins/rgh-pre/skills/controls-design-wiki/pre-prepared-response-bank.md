type: concept
title: Pre-Prepared Response Bank
description: >
  Build a stock of ready-made stories or answers, plus a scripted
  delay phrase, usable across a range of spontaneous-verbal-demand
  situations (interviews, unexpected questions), so responding doesn't
  require generating content on the spot.
sources:
  - title: "Self-Care for Autistic People: 100+ Ways to Recharge, De-Stress, and Unmask"
    resource: "Self-Care for Autistic People (Megan Anna Neff), Chapter 5: Social Self-Care; Chapter 6: Professional Self-Care"
---

Situations that demand a fast, well-formed verbal answer (a job interview,
a put-on-the-spot question in a meeting, small talk) are hard precisely
because there's no time to compose a good answer from nothing. Three
complementary controls address this:
- **A stock of ready stories** — a small set of pre-prepared narratives
  (e.g. illustrating problem-solving, creativity, determination) rehearsed
  in advance and reusable across many different specific questions, so
  answering becomes selecting and adapting rather than generating from
  scratch.
- **A scripted stalling phrase** ("that's an insightful question, let me
  gather my thoughts for a moment") that buys processing time without
  looking unprepared — deciding the phrase in advance means it's available
  even when the pressure of the moment would otherwise make it hard to
  produce a graceful pause.
- **A set of pre-prepared conversation openers or pivot phrases** for
  open-ended social prompts that are themselves hard to answer from
  scratch — e.g. object-focused starters ("Tell me about an interesting
  idea you've learned recently") to substitute for improvised small talk,
  or a pivot phrase for a vague question ("That's a broad question — what
  specifically would you like to know?", "Would it be better if I followed
  up over email?") that redirects to a lower-demand format instead of
  attempting to answer the vague version as asked.

Rehearsal (mock runs with a trusted person, or reviewing a self-recording)
builds the response bank's reliability the same way rehearsing a
[pre-scripted accommodation request](pre-scripted-accommodation-request.md)
does for a shorter, single-purpose script. Where the demand is to *stop* an
interaction rather than sustain one, a [pre-rehearsed boundary
script](pre-rehearsed-boundary-script.md) is the counterpart tool.
