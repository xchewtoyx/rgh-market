---
type: concept
title: Weekly Brain Dump Planning
description: >
  Once per week, externalize every upcoming obligation onto one master view,
  then each evening pull the next day's subset so mornings start with a
  decided plan instead of decision freeze.
sources:
  - title: "How to Be You: Simple Ways to Love Who You Are"
    resource: "How to Be You (Ellie Middleton), ch. 6"
---

An unplanned morning invites phone spirals or overwhelm from not knowing
where to start. A weekly brain dump — large paper or whiteboard, everything
for the coming week (appointments, tasks, gym, social plans) — makes the
full load visible once. Each evening, cross off completed items, circle
tomorrow's achievable subset, assign rough timings, cluster similar work,
and move circled items to the next day's list.

Benefits: every task gets scheduled somewhere; mornings begin with a plan
already chosen; less lying awake rehearsing what needs doing. Pair with
[visual checklists for multi-step routines](visual-checklist-for-multistep-routines.md)
or a wall planner filled during the dump. Works inside fixed employment
hours by deciding which tasks fit the available window and how to use
surrounding time.
