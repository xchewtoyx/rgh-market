---
type: concept
title: Constant Across Task Transition
description: >
  Carry one unchanged element (audio, fidget, call) from task A into task B
  to soften autistic inertia at state switches.
sources:
  - title: "How to Be You: Simple Ways to Love Who You Are"
    resource: "How to Be You (Ellie Middleton), ch. 5"
---

Autistic inertia makes both starting and stopping hard — the system resists
change of state. Find something constant across the transition: continue the
same audiobook, song, podcast, fidget, or phone call from the old task into
the new one so part of the experience does not change while the activity
does.

The brain registers less forced switching when continuity masks the
boundary. Distinct from [habit stacking](habit-stacking.md), which chains
sequential routines by cue; here one sensory or social thread bridges
unrelated tasks.
