---
type: concept
title: Performance Versus Knowledge Gap
description: >
  A design assumption that the limiting factor is often execution at the
  critical moment, not missing information about what to do.
sources:
  - title: "Attention-Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder: A Handbook for Diagnosis and Treatment, 4th Edition"
    resource: "Attention-Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (Russell A. Barkley, Ed.), ch. 16"
---

When self-regulation fails, the default response is often more explanation,
coaching, or skill training — treating the problem as not knowing what to
do. In many executive-function profiles the bottleneck is different:
information is present but not used at the point of performance. "Information
is not self-regulation."

Design implications: stop adding knowledge layers when the failure mode is
timing and execution. Shift effort from conveying what to do toward
engineering where and when it happens — [point-of-performance
intervention](point-of-performance-intervention.md), externalized cues, and
[motivational prostheses](motivational-prosthesis.md). Nagging someone to
try harder or remember harder targets the wrong layer; salient,
task-relevant cues in the immediate context outcompete distractors instead.
