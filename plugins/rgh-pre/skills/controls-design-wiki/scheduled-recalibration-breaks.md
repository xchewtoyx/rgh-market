---
type: concept
title: Scheduled Recalibration Breaks
description: >
  Build short regulation breaks into the schedule at fixed intervals as a
  standing feature of the day, rather than only taking a break reactively
  once overwhelm has already set in.
sources:
  - title: "Self-Care for Autistic People: 100+ Ways to Recharge, De-Stress, and Unmask"
    resource: "Self-Care for Autistic People (Megan Anna Neff), Chapter 6: Professional Self-Care"
---

Waiting until stimulation has already tipped past a comfortable range (the
"Goldilocks zone" of neither under- nor overstimulated) before taking a
break means the break is always playing catch-up. Scheduling short breaks
at fixed intervals ahead of time, regardless of how things feel in the
moment, keeps load from accumulating in the first place — the same
preventive logic as a [daily sensory load budget](daily-sensory-load-budget.md),
applied within a single day's structure rather than across a day's task
list.
