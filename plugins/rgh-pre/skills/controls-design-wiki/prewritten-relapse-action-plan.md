title: Prewritten Relapse Action Plan
description: >
  Document concrete steps to take when mood warning signs appear — often
  sealed until needed — so future-you executes pre-decided care instead of
  improvising under depression's narrowed judgment.
sources:
  - title: "Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy for Depression, 2nd Edition"
    resource: "Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy for Depression (Segal, Williams, Teasdale), ch. 16"
---

Building on identified triggers and early warning signs, a relapse **action
plan** has three steps:

1. **Always** take a [breathing-space action scaffold](breathing-space-action-scaffold.md)
   pause first.
2. **Gather** — replay a helpful mindfulness track, recall class practices,
   remind yourself feelings are intense now but needs are unchanged.
3. **Act** — do something that historically gave pleasure or mastery, even
   when it feels futile; shrink to one part of a task or a time limit.

Some participants write themselves a letter listing actions ("choose at
least one, even though you won't feel like it"), seal it, and open only
when depression starts — pre-loading wisdom for a state where it would not
otherwise be accessible. A parallel plan for family/friends can phrase
requests anticipating resistance ("I know you probably won't be keen, but…")
and list mindful walking, breathing spaces, or reading that reconnects with
a wiser perspective.

Morning vulnerability: self-statement "Just because I am depressed now does
not mean I have to stay depressed" plus breathing space before catastrophizing
rumination wins. Questions to hold: "How can I best be kind to myself right
now?" and "How can I look after myself until this passes?"

Structural analogue to [habit contract](habit-contract.md) and [concrete
implementation plan](concrete-implementation-plan.md) — decisions made in
a clearer state, executed by default when capacity drops.
