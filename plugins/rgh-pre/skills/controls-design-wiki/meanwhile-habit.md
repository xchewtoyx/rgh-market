---
type: concept
title: Meanwhile Habit
description: >
  Insert a tiny beneficial behavior into an existing dead pocket of waiting
  time — shower warm-up, red lights, checkout lines — instead of letting the
  pause default to annoyance or distraction.
sources:
  - title: "Tiny Habits: The Small Changes That Change Everything"
    resource: "Tiny Habits (BJ Fogg), ch. 5"
---

A **Meanwhile Habit** occupies time you are already waiting inside a routine.
The wait becomes the [anchor moment](anchor-moment-design.md); no new slot in
the day is required.

Examples: one body-gratitude thought while shower water warms (~20 seconds);
balance on one foot in a grocery line; press play on an audiobook [after
buckling a seatbelt](habit-stacking.md) to convert commute dead time into
reading. Windows are usually short, so behaviors stay tiny — but consistent
micro-actions compound.

Design move: inventory where you already wait (traffic lights, watering a
plant, software loading) and pair each pocket with one small behavior.
Complements [waiting mode mitigation](waiting-mode-mitigation.md), which
addresses losing whole days before appointments — Meanwhile Habits harvest
seconds-scale pockets instead.
