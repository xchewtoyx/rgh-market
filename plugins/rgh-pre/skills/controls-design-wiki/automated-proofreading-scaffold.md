type: concept
title: Automated Proofreading Scaffold
description: >
  Route fast-produced writing through spellcheck, grammar tools, and human
  editors so output quality does not depend on catching errors in real time
  while thoughts outpace typing.
sources:
  - title: "Unmasked: The Ultimate Guide to ADHD, Autism and Neurodivergence for Women"
    resource: "Unmasked (Ellie Middleton), ch. 4"
---

When thoughts move faster than typing or proofreading can follow, "careless
mistakes" in spelling, phrasing, or appointment details reflect a
processing-speed mismatch — not lack of care. Expecting real-time
self-correction under that load sets up repeated shame and misread
incompetence.

Externalize error-catching: spellcheck, grammar assistants, read-aloud
review, and human editors or proofreaders as standard pipeline steps, not
emergency fixes after damage is done. The control accepts that first-pass
output will be rough and builds a second pass into the workflow. Pair with
[what-by-when-and-why instructions](what-by-when-and-why-instructions.md)
when giving directions to others, and with [full calendar
capture](full-calendar-capture.md) when time-blind rushing contributes to
missed details.
