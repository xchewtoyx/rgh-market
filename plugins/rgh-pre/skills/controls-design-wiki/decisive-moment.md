title: Decisive Moment
description: >
  A small fork-in-the-road choice that constrains what options remain for
  hours afterward — the cab to the gym, not the workout itself.
sources:
  - title: "Atomic Habits: Tiny Changes, Remarkable Results"
    resource: "Atomic Habits (James Clear), ch. 13"
---

Habits are the entrance ramp: 40–50% of daily actions are habitual, and
habits shape the conscious choices that follow. Twyla Tharp's ritual isn't
the gym workout — it's hailing the taxi; telling the driver where to go
completes the ritual that makes the workout likely. Around 5:15 p.m., changing
into workout clothes versus crashing on the couch determines the evening.

These **decisive moments** are forks that send a day productive or unproductive.
Walking into a steakhouse, you can order sirloin or rib eye — not sushi. Each
day is shaped by a few habitual entry-point choices, not the many moments
between. Master decisive moments because habits are the entry point, not the
endpoint.

Pair with [two-minute rule](two-minute-rule.md) to make the decisive moment
as easy as possible to cross, and [habit shaping](habit-shaping.md) to ladder
from gateway behaviors toward full goals.
