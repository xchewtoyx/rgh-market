---
type: concept
title: Demand Triage Traffic-Light System
description: >
  Pre-categorize commitments and demands into a traffic-light system (green
  droppable, yellow conditionally droppable, red hard-to-drop) while capacity
  is available, so a demand can be triaged by lookup rather than fresh
  judgment when capacity is already low.
sources:
  - title: "Self-Care for Autistic People: 100+ Ways to Recharge, De-Stress, and Unmask"
    resource: "Self-Care for Autistic People (Megan Anna Neff), Chapter 3: Emotional Self-Care"
---

Deciding, in the moment and under strain, which commitments can be safely
dropped is itself demanding — it requires weighing consequences with
exactly the cognitive resources that are already depleted. All-or-nothing
thinking under strain also tends to blur essential and nonessential demands
together, adding pressure that isn't actually load-bearing. The fix is to do
the categorization work in advance, while capacity is available: sort
demands into green (droppable without real consequence), yellow (droppable
depending on circumstances), and red (hard to drop, real consequences).
When load needs to be shed, the pre-made list is consulted rather than
re-litigated — "is this demand droppable" becomes a lookup instead of a
judgment call. [Importance-urgency triage criteria](importance-urgency-triage-criteria.md)
is the equivalent control applied upstream, to requests as they arrive,
rather than to the commitments already on the list.
