title: Body-Doubling Work Setup
description: >
  Work alongside another person (in person or on video) as an external
  presence that makes starting and sustaining focus easier, without the
  other person managing the task.

For some people, appearing inattentive while listening — moving, using a
fidget, with background noise, or even scrolling a phone — is a **lower-load
focus modality**, not evidence of failing to absorb content. The
neurotypical still-and-silent posture can consume the same resources needed
to process what is being said. Choosing a sustainable modality is a
[lower-load modality choice](lower-load-modality-choice.md); holding it
when others misread movement as disrespect is a [personal bright-line
rule](personal-bright-line-rule.md) or [pre-rehearsed boundary
script](pre-rehearsed-boundary-script.md).

Body doubling uses another human's parallel presence as scaffolding for
task initiation and persistence — the partner is working on their own
thing, not supervising. Sharing an office instead of a solo room, or joining
a silent co-working video call, are workplace-scale versions; the mechanism
is accountability-by-witness rather than instruction or monitoring.

Design choices: agree the session is for parallel work (not conversation),
pick a partner who won't interrupt, and use a format that fits sensory
needs (quiet room, cameras optional). This complements [protected
uninterrupted time](protected-uninterrupted-time.md) — body doubling helps
*start* and *stay*, while protected blocks defend the slot from external
interruption.

Standing weekly co-working sessions (virtual or café) apply the same
scaffold to personal task initiation, not only workplace parallel work.
