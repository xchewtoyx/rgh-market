type: concept
title: Self-Regulation Resource Replenishment
description: >
  Arrange periodic replenishment during or after self-regulation-heavy
  work because executive effort temporarily depletes a limited resource pool.
sources:
  - title: "Attention-Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder: A Handbook for Diagnosis and Treatment, 4th Edition"
    resource: "Attention-Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (Russell A. Barkley, Ed.), ch. 16"
---

Acts of self-regulation and executive function — inhibition, planning,
working memory, reasoning — draw on a limited resource pool that temporarily
depletes with use. Subsequent self-regulation worsens until the pool
replenishes. Depletion is exacerbated by stress, illness, low blood glucose,
and substance use.

Controls that speed replenishment or prevent deep depletion:

- Routine physical exercise (may also increase pool capacity over weeks)
- [Scheduled recalibration breaks](scheduled-recalibration-breaks.md) during
  strenuous self-regulation (for example ten-minute pauses)
- Brief relaxation or meditation (roughly three minutes or more) after
  exertion
- Visualizing rewards or outcomes during demanding tasks
- Periodic small rewards during the task itself
- Self-affirming statements of self-efficacy before or during work
- Deliberately generating positive emotions
- Glucose-rich beverages during sustained executive effort

Design demanding days assuming the pool will run down: interleave
replenishment controls rather than stacking back-to-back high-effort blocks
and expecting uniform performance throughout.
