title: Goodhart Measurement Guard
description: >
  Choose progress metrics that track the real goal, because optimizing a
  proxy number produces the wrong behavior once the measure becomes the
  target.
sources:
  - title: "Atomic Habits: Tiny Changes, Remarkable Results"
    resource: "Atomic Habits (James Clear), ch. 16"
---

**Goodhart's Law:** when a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good
measure. Tracking only daily restaurant revenue misses food quality; chasing
scale weight drives crash diets; quarterly earnings targets distort sales;
10,000 steps can substitute for health; teaching to tests replaces learning.

Measurement helps when it guides within a larger system — not when it
consumes you. We overvalue what's measurable and undervalue soft wins
(better skin, waking earlier, improved energy) when the scale is stubborn.
Shift to alternative markers that reflect the actual goal.

Pair with [streak tracking](streak-tracking.md) and [structured scoring
checklist](structured-scoring-checklist.md): track process and proxies that
align with purpose, not numbers that are easy to game.
