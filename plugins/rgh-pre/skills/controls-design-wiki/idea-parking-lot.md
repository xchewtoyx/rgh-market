---
type: concept
title: Idea Parking Lot
description: >
  Capture every urgent new idea in one external list so only one "car" is
  driven at a time while the rest wait safely parked.
sources:
  - title: "How to Be You: Simple Ways to Love Who You Are"
    resource: "How to Be You (Ellie Middleton), ch. 3"
---

Hyperfocus and interest-based drive produce a stream of must-do-right-now
ideas. Acting on each immediately prevents finishing any. Externalize them
to a single parking lot (notebook, app, board) with enough detail to trust
they won't be lost.

Choose one idea to pursue; plan its journey including a clear endpoint
(how you'll know it's finished). When done, park it and optionally select
another. Removes fear of losing ideas and pressure to chase every dopamine
spike — a control on impulse, not a willpower lecture. Related to
[tiered to-do structures](tiered-todo-structure.md) "later" buckets and
[running decision logs](running-decision-log.md) for deferred choices.
