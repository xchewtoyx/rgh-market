---
type: concept
title: Identity Aligned Reward Selection
description: >
  Pick immediate habit rewards that vote for the identity you're building,
  not one that conflicts with it — otherwise extrinsic reinforcement
  cancels the habit it was meant to sustain.
sources:
  - title: "Atomic Habits: Tiny Changes, Remarkable Results"
    resource: "Atomic Habits (James Clear), ch. 15"
---

Extrinsic rewards can start a habit, but the reward must reinforce the
target identity. A new jacket may fit a "reader" or "fit person" identity;
for budgeting, a bubble bath or leisurely walk aligns better than a purchase.
Ice cream after exercise casts votes for conflicting identities and "ends up
being a wash"; a massage reinforces being a healthy person.

Apply whenever designing [habit completion
reinforcement](habit-completion-reinforcement.md) or [avoidance habit
savings transfer](avoidance-habit-savings-transfer.md). Eventually
intrinsic outcomes (energy, mood, competence) and identity itself become
the reinforcer — "you do it because it's who you are" — but until evidence
accumulates, the immediate reward must not undermine the long-term self
you're trying to become.
