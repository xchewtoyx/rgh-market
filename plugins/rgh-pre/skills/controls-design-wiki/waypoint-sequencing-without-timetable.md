---
type: concept
title: Waypoint Sequencing Without Timetable
description: >
  Pre-build an unordered menu of concrete waypoints for a trip or outing,
  then visit them in whatever sequence fits the day, instead of requiring
  a fixed timetable for unfamiliar places.
sources:
  - title: "Knowing Why: Adult-Diagnosed Autistic People on Life and Autism"
    resource: "Knowing Why (Autistic Self Advocacy Network), ch. 4 (Stuart Neilson)"
---

Unfamiliar travel often fails at the planning step: a rigid minute-by-minute
itinerary is brittle, but no structure at all leaves every decision to be
made under load in a strange place. The middle path is to front-load a
**menu of known-good waypoints** — meal break, museum, hotel, quiet shop
section — gathered ahead from brochures, search, and [virtual route
preview](virtual-route-preview.md), without committing to order or exact
times.

During the trip, waypoints are visited in whatever sequence the day allows
(a "Smartie trail" pattern: collect the next stop when ready, not at a
predetermined clock time). Fear of unstructured strange places converts
into anticipation of a finite set of building blocks already chosen while
capacity was higher. Pair with [curated activity menu](curated-activity-menu.md)
when the waypoints are leisure options rather than travel logistics.
