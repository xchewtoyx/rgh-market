title: Launch Pad Bag
description: >
  Keep one bag permanently stocked with departure essentials so leaving the
  house does not depend on remembering each item every time.
sources:
  - title: "How to Be You: Simple Ways to Love Who You Are"
    resource: "How to Be You (Ellie Middleton), ch. 1"
  - title: "Knowing Why: Adult-Diagnosed Autistic People on Life and Autism"
    resource: "Knowing Why (Autistic Self Advocacy Network), ch. 10 (A.C. Buchanan)"

Re-packing the same charger, wallet, headphones, and toiletries before every
exit multiplies failure points. A launch pad is a bag that never fully
unpacks — duplicate items live there permanently (dedicated phone charger,
mini toiletries, fidget, earplugs) so departure is grab-and-go.

Extend to a permanently stocked overnight bag for frequent travel (spare
toothbrush, charger, wipes). For sensory overload in emergencies, a spare
pair of noise-cancelling headphones can rank above other grab-and-run items
(e.g. an extra water bottle) — shelters address dehydration before sensory
overload, but the individual kit should match the likeliest personal failure
mode. Same pattern as [pre-stocked low-energy
fallback](pre-stocked-low-energy-fallback.md): front-load preparation when
capacity exists; the degraded path still works under time pressure.
