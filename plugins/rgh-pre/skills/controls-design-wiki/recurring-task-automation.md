---
type: concept
title: Recurring Task Automation
description: >
  Remove recurring obligations from working memory via subscriptions,
  standing orders, calendar reminders, and rule-based transfers so action
  happens only when prompted or not at all.
sources:
  - title: "How to Be You: Simple Ways to Love Who You Are"
    resource: "How to Be You (Ellie Middleton), ch. 8"
---

Each recurring bill, refill, prescription, or savings transfer held in mind
competes with everything else. Automate what can run without a decision:
subscribe-and-save for consumables, standing orders and direct debits for
payments, calendar reminders for tasks that still need a human step (repeat
prescriptions), smart-speaker capture for spontaneous reminders, rule-based
savings (round-ups, spend triggers). Meal subscription services automate
planning and shopping when cooking decisions are the bottleneck.

What cannot be automated, [delegate](task-delegation-scaffold.md) — standardized
message replies, cleaners, prescription pickup by a family member. The design
goal is shrinking the active plate count, not proving independence by juggling
every recurrence manually.
