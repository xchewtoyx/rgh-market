---
type: concept
title: Mentor as Decoding Resource
description: >
  Designate an ongoing go-to person who can translate ambiguous social or
  contextual signals into explicit terms, so decoding a new environment's
  unwritten rules doesn't have to be done alone, from scratch, one
  observation at a time.
sources:
  - title: "Self-Care for Autistic People: 100+ Ways to Recharge, De-Stress, and Unmask"
    resource: "Self-Care for Autistic People (Megan Anna Neff), Chapter 6: Professional Self-Care"
---

A new environment (a new job, in the source example) carries a layer of
unwritten cultural norms usually learned only through prolonged passive
observation. A mentor — formal (requested as an accommodation) or informal
(someone already naturally trusted) — shortcuts that by making the unwritten
codes explicit on an ongoing basis, rather than requiring the newcomer to
reverse-engineer each norm independently from scattered cues. This is the
standing-relationship counterpart to
[requesting explicit norms](request-explicit-norms.md) for the specific
questions that come up along the way.
