title: Scheduled Self Treat Routine
description: >
  Build small, regular pampering into the week as a preventive control —
  inexpensive treats on a fixed rhythm remind you to value yourself before
  burnout or relapse, not only as a reward after crisis.

Self-care framed only as recovery-after-collapse arrives too late. A
**scheduled self-treat routine** bakes inexpensive pleasure into the week as
part of relapse prevention: bath oils, a special meal, cut flowers, a
favorite film, music, or a Friday indulgence after the working week.

Treats need not be expensive. The control is the **rhythm** — regular,
anticipated self-valuing that doesn't depend on earning it through
overwork first. This parallels [habit completion
reinforcement](habit-completion-reinforcement.md) but targets wellbeing
maintenance rather than wiring a specific new behavior.

When designing treats for recovery from compulsive behavior, plan them in
advance so a lapse isn't mislabeled as a "deserved reward" — see [hard loss
recovery scheduling](hard-loss-recovery-scheduling.md) (Reward). Choose
options aligned with the identity you're building per [identity-aligned
reward selection](identity-aligned-reward-selection.md).
