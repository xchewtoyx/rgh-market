type: concept
title: Shareable Needs Profile
description: >
  Write down a person's strengths, triggers, sensory sensitivities, and
  communication preferences once, in a document meant to be handed to new
  teachers, providers, or caregivers, so each new person doesn't have to
  independently rediscover the same information from scratch.

Every new teacher, babysitter, therapist, or care provider who doesn't
already have this information has to learn it the slow way — through trial,
error, and observation — before they can actually adapt to it, and the
person (or their parent/advocate, when the person can't yet self-advocate)
ends up re-explaining the same ground to each new person in turn.
Documenting it once — strengths, known triggers, sensory sensitivities,
favored learning/communication methods — turns that repeated, effortful
re-explanation into a single handoff: a new provider starts from the
profile instead of from zero, and the document keeps working as a
standing resource across every future transition, not just the current
one.

This is the third-party-facing counterpart to a [sensory safety
plan](sensory-safety-plan.md), which is authored the same way (in advance,
while capacity is available) but for the person's own use in an overwhelm
moment rather than for handing to someone new who will be caring for or
teaching them.

Worked example from a workplace setting: tech company Ultranauts has every
employee fill out a "Biodex" — a standing personal-user-manual document
covering preferred learning/work styles, productivity habits,
social-interaction preferences, and personal triggers/distractions — that
colleagues consult instead of learning each person's working style by
trial and error. The same document format works for a coworker relationship
as for a caregiving or teaching one; what changes is only who it's handed
to and when.

A **portable credential variant** carries the same information in
high-stress encounters where verbal fluency may fail — e.g. a photo ID card
noting a diagnosis plus a first-responder information leaflet for airport
security or similar checkpoints. The card does not replace the fuller
profile for ongoing relationships, but supplies a pre-written fact sheet
when improvised self-advocacy under anxiety is unreliable.
