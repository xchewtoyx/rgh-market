---
type: concept
title: Habit Shaping
description: >
  After a gateway habit sticks, advance one small stage at a time while
  always mastering the first two minutes of the current stage before adding
  difficulty.
sources:
  - title: "Atomic Habits: Tiny Changes, Remarkable Results"
    resource: "Atomic Habits (James Clear), ch. 13"
---

Combine [two-minute rule](two-minute-rule.md) with staged expansion:

- **Early riser:** home by 10 p.m. → devices off by 10 → in bed by 10 →
  lights off by 10 → wake at 6 a.m.
- **Vegan:** vegetables at each meal → no four-legged animals → no two-legged
  → no fish → no animal products.
- **Exercise:** change into workout clothes → step outside → gym 5 min →
  15 min weekly → 3× weekly.

Master the first two minutes of the smallest version, advance to an
intermediate step, master its first two minutes, repeat until reaching the
target behavior. Any life goal chains down: healthy long life → stay in shape
→ exercise → change into workout clothes. Distinct from [habit stacking](habit-stacking.md),
which chains behaviors within a day; shaping ladders difficulty across weeks.
