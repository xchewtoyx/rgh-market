---
type: concept
title: Success Momentum
description: >
  Frequent small felt successes raise overall motivation more reliably than
  rare large ones — the size of the win barely matters compared to how often
  it happens.
sources:
  - title: "Tiny Habits: The Small Changes That Change Everything"
    resource: "Tiny Habits (BJ Fogg), ch. 7"
---

"Success leads to success." What matters is **frequency** of successes, not
their magnitude — which is why Tiny Habits targets many tiny wins quickly
rather than one big win that takes months.

Mechanism via [Fogg behavior model](fogg-behavior-model.md): many behaviors
carry opposing motivation vectors (hope vs. fear). The first attempt is a
critical formation moment — feeling like a failure strengthens the fear
vector; feeling successful weakens it and can push harder behaviors above
the Action Line. Vanquishing fear of a small meeting makes leading larger
meetings more likely because hope now dominates.

Design implications:

- Start tiny so success is the default ([starter step scaling
  back](starter-step-scaling-back.md), [bare minimum day
  goal](bare-minimum-day-goal.md)).
- Celebrate each instance per [Fogg behavior design
  maxims](fogg-behavior-design-maxims.md) maxim 2.
- Build skills in low-stakes domains before attacking uphill habits
  ([behavior change masterplan](behavior-change-masterplan.md) phase 1).

"Start where you want to on your path to change. Allow yourself to feel
successful. Then trust the process."
