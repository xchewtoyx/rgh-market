---
type: concept
title: Demand-Softening Schedule Design
description: >
  Structure plans as flexible option pools rather than fixed date-time
  assignments when perceived demands trigger avoidance even for tasks the
  person intended to do.
sources:
  - title: "How to Be You: Simple Ways to Love Who You Are"
    resource: "How to Be You (Ellie Middleton), ch. 2"
---

Demand avoidance turns an expectation — even a self-imposed one — into
something the nervous system resists. A fixed calendar slot ("post the quote
Monday at 9am," "cook chili Tuesday") can block completion of work the person
was about to start voluntarily once the slot itself reads as a demand.

Soften the structure while keeping forward progress:

- Replace fixed posting or publishing schedules with "when it feels right"
  within a loose rhythm, if consistency pressure is the trigger.
- Move tasks from specific date-time calendar entries to a weekly pool
  chosen day-by-day, so the list supplies options without commanding a
  moment.
- Replace fixed weekly meal plans (specific meal on specific day) with a
  list of meals for the week plus ingredients on hand, then choose at
  mealtime — same nutrition planning, less imposed obligation.

The control preserves preparation ([curated activity menus](curated-activity-menu.md),
shopping, ingredient availability) while removing the perceived command
that activates fight-or-flight avoidance. Pair with [demand triage traffic-
light systems](demand-triage-traffic-light-system.md) when external
commitments cannot be fully flexed.
