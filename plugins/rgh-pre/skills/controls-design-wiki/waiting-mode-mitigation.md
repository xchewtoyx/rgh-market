title: Waiting Mode Mitigation
description: >
  Reduce the lost day before a scheduled appointment by early scheduling,
  task-duration records, and hard external reminders.
sources:
  - title: "How to Be You: Simple Ways to Love Who You Are"
    resource: "How to Be You (Ellie Middleton), ch. 5"
---

An afternoon appointment can trap the whole day in "waiting mode" — unable
to start other work from fear of forgetting, hyperfocus risk, time blindness,
or decision paralysis about what fits before the slot.

Mitigations: schedule commitments as early as possible so the remainder of
the day is free; maintain a personal table of how long routine tasks
actually take; set alarms before leave-time and before start-time so
hyperfocus cannot erase the appointment. Complements [full calendar
capture](full-calendar-capture.md) and [fixed schedules for unreliable
body cues](fixed-schedule-for-unreliable-body-cues.md).
