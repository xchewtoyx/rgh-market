---
type: concept
title: Pre-Authorized Refusal Boundary
description: >
  Establish in advance an explicit right to refuse a duty, commitment, or
  exposure when predicted risk is too high — without treating each refusal
  as a fresh negotiation.
sources:
  - title: "Resilience Engineering in Practice: A Guidebook"
    resource: "Resilience Engineering in Practice (Hollnagel, Pariès, Woods, Wreathall, eds.), ch. 6 (Philippe Cabon et al.)"
---

When overload or fatigue risk is already elevated, the highest-priority
operational mitigation is **suppression**: do not take on the exposure.
That only works if refusal is pre-authorized — policy, contract, or personal
bright-line agreed before the moment — rather than improvised under social
or economic pressure.

Design the boundary explicitly: what triggers refusal (a [factor
combination risk matrix](factor-combination-risk-matrix.md) threshold, a
partner check-in, a sleep-debt rule), who must be notified, and what
alternatives exist. Link remuneration and schedule design so people are not
incentivized to stack duty for "free time" at the cost of safety. At
personal scale, this is a [personal bright-line
rule](personal-bright-line-rule.md) plus [pre-rehearsed boundary
script](pre-rehearsed-boundary-script.md) for voicing "I will not take this
on today." Without pre-authorization, [suppression-reduction-barrier
mitigation hierarchy](suppression-reduction-barrier-mitigation-hierarchy.md)
collapses to barriers-only — managing harm after exposure was already
accepted.
