---
type: concept
title: Golden Behavior Selection
description: >
  Choose target behaviors that simultaneously score high on aspiration
  impact, personal motivation, and ability — using Focus Mapping instead of
  guessing, internet inspiration, or copying friends.
sources:
  - title: "Tiny Habits: The Small Changes That Change Everything"
    resource: "Tiny Habits (BJ Fogg), ch. 3"
---

Behavior Design steps 1–3 replace roulette with criteria-based matching.

**Step 1 — Clarify aspiration.** Write the outcome as a draft and interrogate
it: "lose weight" may mean feel better in clothes, control diabetes, or
paddleboard-ready. Prefer flexible aspirations ("reduce stress") over
intimidating ones ("be more mindful") when they serve the same underlying
want.

**Step 2 — Explore behavior options (Swarm of Behaviors).** Generate many
specific candidates without judging feasibility. Tools: **Magic Wanding**
("if you could wave a magic wand, what behavior would achieve this?") and
prompt categories — one-time actions, new habits to start, habits to stop.
Revise each option to be maximally specific ("crispy"): "play with dog" →
"play fetch each evening at home." Avoid three flawed picks: guessing,
internet inspiration from non-representative models, and copying a friend's
habit without fit evidence.

**Step 3 — Match golden behaviors.** A **golden behavior** meets three
criteria at once:

- **Impact** — effective for the aspiration.
- **Motivation** — you want to do it (not merely "should").
- **Ability** — you can do it, including on a rushed morning ("Real-You,"
  not "Fantasy-You").

**Focus Mapping** (~10 minutes): place swarm cards on impact (up/down) then
feasibility (left/right) without moving impact position. Upper-right =
golden behaviors. Shortcut: star high-impact options, circle easy ones;
star-and-circle overlap wins. If the corner is empty, generate more options.

Golden behaviors can be one-time setup ([one-time setup
lock-in](one-time-setup-lock-in.md)) or repeated habits. Starting with
wanted-and-doable behaviors plants "a good seed in the right spot" per
[Fogg behavior design maxims](fogg-behavior-design-maxims.md) maxim 1. Next
step: make chosen behaviors tiny ([starter step scaling
back](starter-step-scaling-back.md)).
