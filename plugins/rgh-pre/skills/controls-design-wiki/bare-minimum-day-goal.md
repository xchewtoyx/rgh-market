---
type: concept
title: Bare Minimum Day Goal
description: >
  Set the day's success threshold at a small, realistic task count you
  will likely complete, instead of judging against a full list that triggers
  dread and avoidance.
sources:
  - title: "How to Be You: Simple Ways to Love Who You Are"
    resource: "How to Be You (Ellie Middleton), ch. 6"
---

When capacity is low, a ten-item list with only two completions feels like
failure — producing Monday dread, freeze, and action only when deadlines
force it. If you reliably complete about twenty percent of a long list,
make that twenty percent the entire goal (two tasks on a ten-item day).

Completing the bare minimum lifts the weight of the other eight items,
often producing momentum for extra work afterward — "two hundred percent
of goal" beats chronic underperformance against an impossible bar. A
companion to [graded task assignment](graded-task-assignment.md) and
[tiered to-do structures](tiered-todo-structure.md), sized for daily
morale rather than step decomposition.
