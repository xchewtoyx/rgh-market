---
type: concept
title: Proactive Recovery Leave
description: >
  Schedule extended recovery time (leave, a sabbatical, a planned pause) on
  a standing cadence proactively, rather than treating time off as
  something only taken reactively after burnout has already set in.
sources:
  - title: "The Canary Code: A Guide to Neurodiversity, Dignity, and Intersectional Belonging at Work"
    resource: "The Canary Code (Ludmila N. Praslova, PhD), Chapter 7: Work Organization — Productivity and Purpose"
---

Recovery time taken only after burnout has already occurred is playing
catch-up: the damage is already done, and the leave is now recovery from a
failure rather than prevention of one. Treating extended time off as a
standing, scheduled feature — planned at intervals before there's a crisis
forcing it — keeps accumulated load from ever reaching the point where
recovery has to be reactive. This is the large-grain version of
[scheduled recalibration breaks](scheduled-recalibration-breaks.md): the
same preventive logic, applied on a much longer cycle.
