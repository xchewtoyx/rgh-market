---
type: concept
title: Day Quadrant Planning
description: >
  Split the day into four bounded segments so one bad period does not write
  off the entire day under all-or-nothing thinking.
sources:
  - title: "How to Be You: Simple Ways to Love Who You Are"
    resource: "How to Be You (Ellie Middleton), ch. 5"
---

Missing a 9:00 start or losing a morning can feel like the whole day is
ruined. Divide the day into four quadrants (e.g. morning, midday, afternoon,
evening — timings adjustable). Assign tasks to a quadrant when planning; if
one quadrant goes poorly, three fresh starts remain.

Where possible, cluster one activity type per quadrant to reduce switching
cost — admin in one block, creative work in another — per [task
batching](task-batching.md). A lightweight reframe control for perfectionism
without requiring a full calendar rebuild.
