type: concept
title: Graduated Self-Disclosure Script
description: >
  Prepare a small set of disclosure statements ranging from vague to
  specific, so how much personal information to share in a new or
  developing relationship can be chosen deliberately and paced to match
  closeness, rather than defaulting to full disclosure or improvised
  evasion under social pressure.
sources:
  - title: "Self-Care for Autistic People: 100+ Ways to Recharge, De-Stress, and Unmask"
    resource: "Self-Care for Autistic People (Megan Anna Neff), Chapter 5: Social Self-Care"
  - title: "Knowing Why: Adult-Diagnosed Autistic People on Life and Autism"
    resource: "Knowing Why (Autistic Self Advocacy Network), ch. 5 (Kelly Bron Johnson)"
---

Disclosing a personal difference (being neurodivergent, a chronic
condition, a sensitive history) is not all-or-nothing. Preparing a small
ladder of statements in advance — from vague ("I'm neurodivergent") through
partial ("I sometimes need extra time to process information," "I struggle
with unexpected changes in plans") to full — turns disclosure into a
deliberate choice made ahead of time about how much to say, rather than a
decision improvised in the moment under whatever social pressure the
situation happens to apply. The vaguer end of the ladder can be used first
to gauge the other person's response before deciding whether to share more.

The relevant control variable is pacing, not just content: matching the
statement's specificity to the relationship's actual closeness (what's
said on a first date differs from what's said once trust is established)
keeps disclosure calibrated over time instead of front-loaded or withheld
by default.

In **hiring and workplace filtering**, disclosure can be front-loaded
deliberately: stating autism in an interview (or making it visible in a
public profile employers will see) filters out environments that react
poorly, and a curious "what do we need to know?" response signals fit for
[pre-scripted accommodation request](pre-scripted-accommodation-request.md)
negotiation. That trades pacing for environment selection — a different
disclosure goal than calibrating trust in an ongoing relationship.

This is a distinct concern from [pre-scripted accommodation
request](pre-scripted-accommodation-request.md): that note pairs
disclosure with a specific environmental ask in the moment it's needed;
this one is about pacing identity disclosure in an ongoing relationship
independent of any particular request.
