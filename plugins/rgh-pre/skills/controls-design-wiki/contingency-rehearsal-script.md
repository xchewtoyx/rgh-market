type: concept
title: Contingency Rehearsal Script
description: >
  Pre-write paired "If X happens, then I will Y" responses for plausible
  failure modes, so setbacks trigger a known script instead of improvisation
  under stress.
sources:
  - title: "The Obstacle Is the Way: The Timeless Art of Turning Trials into Triumph, Expanded 10th Anniversary Edition"
    resource: "The Obstacle Is the Way (Ryan Holiday), ch. 29"
  - title: "Thinking, Fast and Slow"
    resource: "Thinking, Fast and Slow (Daniel Kahneman), ch. 26"
---

After a [premortem procedure](premortem-procedure.md) or solo planning pass
surfaces what could go wrong, convert each credible failure mode into a
**rehearsed pair**: "What if the launch slips? → Then I will notify stakeholders
with revised dates by Friday." "What if I am booed? → Then I will finish the
core message and exit calmly." Stoic *premeditatio malorum* uses the same
shape: before travel or a speech, mentally walk storms, delays, hostile
audiences, and exile — not to pessimism for its own sake, but so nothing
that can happen is wholly unanticipated.

When no response can prevent the outcome, the script still sets
**expectation management**: "It will be hard; we will endure; we will not be
broken by it." The worst cost is often surprise plus discouragement, not the
bad event itself — someone who has rehearsed the range of outcomes is less
likely to abandon the task on first contact with difficulty. Return focus to
the next executable step once the contingency is acknowledged.
