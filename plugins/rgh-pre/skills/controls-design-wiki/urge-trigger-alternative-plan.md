title: Urge Trigger Alternative Plan
description: >
  Map personal triggers and highest-risk times for unwanted behavior, then
  pre-decide a specific alternative action for each — so an urge finds a
  ready script instead of improvisation under craving.
sources:
  - title: "CBT For Dummies, 4th Edition"
    resource: "CBT For Dummies (Rhena Branch, Rob Willson), ch. 21"
---

Cravings are normal; acting on them is optional. **Urge trigger alternative
planning** makes the optional part easier:

1. **List triggers** — advertisements, environments, people, negative
   feelings or events.
2. **Identify highest-risk times** when urges cluster.
3. **Pre-assign an alternative** for each — phone call, crossword, walk the
   dog, a run, any absorbing distraction that doesn't require deciding in
   the moment.

This is steady-state prevention scaffolding, not crisis response. Pair with
[friction shaping](friction-shaping.md) to make the unwanted action harder
and [prewritten relapse action plan](prewritten-relapse-action-plan.md)
for mood-warning-sign protocols. Repeatedly experiencing urges without
acting builds tolerance; the plan supplies what to do instead while that
threshold rises.

For cash-dependent compulsions, add [urge delay friction
controls](urge-delay-friction-controls.md) — structural delay between urge
and ability to act.
