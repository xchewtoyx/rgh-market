The template itself is the control: a standing grid (days as columns, time
blocks as rows) that already exists before planning starts, so the planning
act is reduced to dropping items into cells rather than inventing a
structure. Items placed in the grid should be a mix of pleasurable activities
and daily responsibilities/duties/errands — see
[balanced fun-and-achievement activity scheduling](balanced-activity-scheduling.md)
— pulled from a [curated activity menu](curated-activity-menu.md) rather than
generated on the spot, and sized per [graded task assignment](graded-task-assignment.md)
so each slot is actually achievable.

High-value recharge activities (e.g. a special interest that reliably
regulates mood and energy) should get a reserved, protected block on the
grid, placed proactively rather than left to fill whatever time happens to
be left over — a slot that's only claimed by leftovers tends not to survive
a busy week.
