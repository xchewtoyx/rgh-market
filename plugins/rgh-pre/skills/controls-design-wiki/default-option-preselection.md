  Pre-organize a small, fixed set of default options for a recurring
  decision (what to eat, which care products to use) so the in-the-moment
  choice becomes picking from a short pre-approved list instead of an

Open-ended recurring decisions (what to cook tonight, which shampoo to buy)
cost willpower and attention every time they're made from scratch. Cutting
that down means deciding once, in advance, on a small set of acceptable
defaults, then only choosing among them later — e.g. pre-organizing food or
care-product options down to a handful of known-good choices, or assigning a
specific meal to a specific day of the week (a "Taco Tuesday" pattern) so
that day's meal decision is removed entirely rather than merely narrowed.

This is the same move as a [curated activity menu](curated-activity-menu.md)
applied to routine, lower-stakes choices: front-load the option-generation
work once, so it never has to happen at the point of use. A
[pre-stocked low-energy fallback](pre-stocked-low-energy-fallback.md) is a
special case reserved specifically for low-capacity days.

At organizational scale, opt-out defaults exploit the same mechanism: the
preselected option is perceived as the normal choice; deviating requires an
act of commission, more effortful deliberation, and more anticipated regret
— pension auto-enrollment is the canonical example. See [choice
architecture](choice-architecture.md) for the broader design frame.

Cross-country organ-donation forms illustrate the same mechanism at scale:
opt-out defaults yield near-universal enrollment; opt-in defaults yield
single-digit uptake — not because preferences differ, but because checking
a box to deviate requires effort and deliberate counterfactual thinking most
people skip when unprepared. Any important recurring choice with a sensible
default (savings, consent, enrollment) should be pre-set to the desired
path with an explicit opt-out, not left to an active opt-in moment.

Wearing near-identical outfits daily removes a recurring low-stakes decision
entirely — the same preselection move applied to clothing rather than meals.
