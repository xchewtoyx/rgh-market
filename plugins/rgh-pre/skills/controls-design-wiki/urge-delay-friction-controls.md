---
type: concept
title: Urge Delay Friction Controls
description: >
  In early recovery from compulsive spending or similar habits, impose
  temporary structural barriers — limited cash, removed cards, deleted apps
  — that force a delay between urge and action.
sources:
  - title: "CBT For Dummies, 4th Edition"
    resource: "CBT For Dummies (Rhena Branch, Rob Willson), ch. 21"
---

"Put at least half an hour between yourself and your money." When addiction
requires cash or quick digital access, make relapse **structurally harder**:

- Withdraw a fixed amount at the ATM before shopping; don't carry open-ended
  payment access.
- Remove credit cards to block online gambling or impulse buys.
- Delete addiction-related numbers and apps from your phone.

Frame this as temporary **patronizing yourself** in early recovery — acceptable
loss of autonomy while the habit weakens. This is [friction shaping](friction-shaping.md)
applied to urge-to-action latency, not just removing cues. Combine with
[urge trigger alternative plan](urge-trigger-alternative-plan.md) so the
forced pause has a pre-decided fill activity.

Won't replace professional treatment for severe addiction, but tips the
balance when urges are surmountable with environmental support.
