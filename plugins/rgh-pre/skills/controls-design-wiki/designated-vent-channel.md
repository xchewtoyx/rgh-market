---
type: concept
title: Designated Vent Channel
description: >
  Send emotionally charged messages to a pre-agreed trusted person instead
  of the real recipient, preserving catharsis without irreversible
  consequences.
sources:
  - title: "How to Be You: Simple Ways to Love Who You Are"
    resource: "How to Be You (Ellie Middleton), ch. 3"
---

Impulsive messaging during emotional escalation delivers the thrill of send
and risk that private journaling cannot. A message monitor — a friend briefed
in advance — receives the exact text you would have sent to the manager,
ex, or colleague. You still type and press send; consequences stay contained.

Set up context with the monitor before it's needed. Pair with [friction
shaping](friction-shaping.md) (removing saved payment details, notification
limits) for other impulse domains. This is structural harm reduction, not
suppression of feeling.
