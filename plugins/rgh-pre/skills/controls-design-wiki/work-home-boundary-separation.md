---
type: concept
title: Work Home Boundary Separation
description: >
  Physically and digitally separate work from personal life — separate
  devices, logins, or notification rules — so relaxation time doesn't carry
  productivity guilt from ambient work cues.
sources:
  - title: "The Autistic's Guide to Self-Discovery"
    resource: "The Autistic's Guide to Self-Discovery (Sol Smith), ch. 8"
---

Work-from-home blurred boundaries that many never restored. Without
separation, a phone vibration triggers "I'm slacking off at work" even
during intentional rest — relaxation carries guilt.

Controls: never put work email on phone/tablet; use a separate computer login
for work versus personal time; don't take work texts on personal devices.
Schedule work blocks explicitly ([full calendar capture](full-calendar-capture.md))
so a movie in off-hours isn't competing with an unmarked obligation. Pair
with [one space, one use](one-space-one-use.md) when a dedicated work zone
exists, and [cue exposure reduction](cue-exposure-reduction.md) when work
notifications must stay off personal surfaces.
