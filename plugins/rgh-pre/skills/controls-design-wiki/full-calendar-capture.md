---
type: concept
title: Full Calendar Capture
description: >
  Put every time-bound commitment — including solo work, chores, travel, and
  self-care — on one calendar so true capacity is visible before accepting
  new obligations.
sources:
  - title: "How to Be You: Simple Ways to Love Who You Are"
    resource: "How to Be You (Ellie Middleton), ch. 8"
---

Calendars that list only meetings hide writing blocks, laundry, gym, commute,
and recovery — making empty-looking days falsely available for new work.
Rule: if it needs to happen, it gets a calendar block with notifications;
otherwise it likely will not.

Include [time-blocked task batches](task-batching.md), non-negotiable
self-care, and travel time. Digital calendars can be rearranged when [demand-
softening schedule design](demand-softening-schedule-design.md) makes fixed
slots feel like demands; the capture matters more than immovable timing.
Revealing real load prevents overcommitment upstream.
