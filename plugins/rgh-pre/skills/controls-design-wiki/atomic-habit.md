An **atomic habit** is both (1) a tiny marginal gain — a 1% improvement —
and (2) a fundamental unit of a larger system, like atoms in a molecule.
"Habits are the atoms of our lives." Each one is small and easy; together
they compound to outweigh their initial cost.

Definition: a regular practice or routine that is not only small and easy
to do, but also the source of incredible power — a component of compound
growth. Design habits as inputs; results are outputs that follow when the
system runs ([systems over goals](systems-over-goals.md)).

Pair with [starter step scaling back](starter-step-scaling-back.md) and
[two-minute rule](two-minute-rule.md) for sizing, and [four laws of
behavior change](four-laws-of-behavior-change.md) for wiring each unit into
the loop.
