---
type: concept
title: Running Decision Log
description: >
  Keep an ongoing written record of key conversations, agreements, and
  decisions as they happen (notes, follow-up recap emails) rather than
  relying on memory to reconstruct them later.
sources:
  - title: "Self-Care for Autistic People: 100+ Ways to Recharge, De-Stress, and Unmask"
    resource: "Self-Care for Autistic People (Megan Anna Neff), Chapter 6: Professional Self-Care"
---

Ongoing, lightweight documentation — a running notes file, or a short recap
email sent after a key conversation confirming what was discussed and
agreed — turns memory of "what did we decide" into a lookup instead of a
recollection. Capture: what was said, key decisions, and date/time/location
for chronological context. Beyond the memory-offload benefit, a written
record also serves as a factual reference point if a conversation's outcome
is later disputed or misremembered by someone else — protection that pure
memory can't provide regardless of how accurate the memory actually is.
