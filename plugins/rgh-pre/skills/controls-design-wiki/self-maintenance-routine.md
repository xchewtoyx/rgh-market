Self-maintenance differs from "self-care" as leisure-after-obligations. It's
the fan-belt work that prevents breakdown en route — done *before* the day
starts. Beating burnout is threefold: **maintain, recover, reload**.

**Maintain:** compare against your own baseline and make just-enough
improvements — don't overhaul everything at once. Walking ~20 minutes
optimizes thinking; running adds circulation and post-run clarity. Make the
bar so low you'll accidentally exceed expectations ([bare minimum day
goal](bare-minimum-day-goal.md), [starter step scaling back](starter-step-scaling-back.md)).
Balance work (yoga, one-foot stands, balance board while watching TV) thickens
cerebellum structures linked to self-control. Food: cooking most meals at home
or eating around hunger cues beats fad chasing; repeatable breakfast/lunch
rituals reduce decision load ([preference-aligned routine design](preference-aligned-routine-design.md)).
**Sleep is foundational** — executive function suffers first; aim 6–8 hours
at night ([sleep restriction protocol](sleep-restriction-protocol.md),
[wind-down buffer](wind-down-buffer.md)).

**Recover:** schedule stressful obligations explicitly so relaxation doesn't
carry guilt — earmark work time elsewhere. **Reload:** when motivation is
zero, resurrect an interest to think about through the day ([interest novelty
challenge urgency injection](interest-novelty-challenge-urgency-injection.md))
or seek shared-activity social contact.
