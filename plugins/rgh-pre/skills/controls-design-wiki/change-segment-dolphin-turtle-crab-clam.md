---
type: concept
title: Change Segment Dolphin Turtle Crab Clam
description: >
  When a group won't adopt a behavior, segment members by motivation and
  ability — help Dolphins and Turtles first, redirect Crabs and Clams to
  different activities instead of converting everyone at once.
sources:
  - title: "Tiny Habits: The Small Changes That Change Everything"
    resource: "Tiny Habits (BJ Fogg), ch. 9"
---

After a group intervention fails, plot members on motivation (high/low) and
ability (high/low):

| Segment | Motivation | Ability | Strategy |
|---------|------------|---------|----------|
| **Dolphin** | High | High | Already doing it — reinforce |
| **Turtle** | High | Low | Simplify via [ability chain diagnosis](ability-chain-diagnosis.md) |
| **Crab** | Low | High | Redirect to a different activity they'd enjoy |
| **Clam** | Low | Low | Redirect; do not spend first effort here |

Common mistake: one-size-fits-all messaging or targeting Clams first. Correct
posture in [group behavior design process](group-behavior-design-process.md):
make the behavior easy for those who already want it; set unmotivated members
aside rather than burning credibility on conversion.

**Exception — mandatory/high-stakes behaviors** (flu shot): also target Crabs
with the **Queen B Solution** — keep the required behavior but attach it to
an aspiration they care about (concert passes, face time with leadership,
free gear) instead of swapping the task.

Pair with [prompt-ability-motivation troubleshooting
order](prompt-ability-motivation-troubleshooting-order.md) at the individual
level inside each segment.
