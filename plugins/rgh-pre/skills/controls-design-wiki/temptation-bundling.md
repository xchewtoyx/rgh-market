---
type: concept
title: Temptation Bundling
description: >
  Pair an action you need to do with one you want to do, so the needed
  behavior becomes the gateway to the wanted reward and anticipation drives
  action.
sources:
  - title: "Atomic Habits: Tiny Changes, Remarkable Results"
    resource: "Atomic Habits (James Clear), ch. 8"
---

Ronan Byrne wired his bike so Netflix only played while he pedaled at speed
— wanted viewing bundled with needed exercise. Premack's Principle: more
probable behaviors reinforce less probable ones — you'll do the low-desire
task if it's the gateway to the high-desire one.

**Combined formula** with [habit stacking](habit-stacking.md):
1. After [CURRENT HABIT], I will [HABIT I NEED].
2. After [HABIT I NEED], I will [HABIT I WANT].

Examples: after morning coffee, say one grateful thing → then read news;
after lunch break, call three clients → then check ESPN; after pulling out
phone, ten burpees → then Facebook. Only read tabloids at the gym; only get
a pedicure while processing overdue email.

Dopamine spikes on **anticipation**, not fulfillment — the expectation of
the wanted reward makes the needed action attractive. Eventually you may
look forward to the needed action itself because it reliably opens the
wanted one. Distinct from engineering supernormal stimuli (junk food bliss
points) you can't replicate; bundling heightens any habit by linking it to
existing desire. Part of the 2nd Law in [four laws of behavior change](four-laws-of-behavior-change.md).
