---
type: concept
title: What-by-When-and-Why Instructions
description: >
  State delegated work as what exactly is needed, by when, and why, instead
  of vague requests that force the recipient to infer missing context.
sources:
  - title: "Unmasked: The Ultimate Guide to ADHD, Autism and Neurodivergence for Women"
    resource: "Unmasked (Ellie Middleton), ch. 9"
---

Vague instructions ("touch base," "circle back," "have a look at this")
leave three separate inference jobs — scope, deadline, and purpose — on
the person least able to spare working memory for guesswork. A
what-by-when-and-why structure front-loads all three: *what* deliverable or
action is required, *by when* it is due, and *why* it matters to the
broader goal.

Use for manager delegations, meeting action items, and self-assigned tasks
alike. Pair with [multi-format information provision](multi-format-information-provision.md)
when instructions are given verbally in meetings — follow with a written
summary of the same three fields so nothing must be reconstructed from
memory. This is an environmental control on communication clarity, not a
motivation technique: it removes ambiguity that otherwise becomes missed
deadlines and anxiety spirals.
