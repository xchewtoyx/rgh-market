title: Habit Contract
description: >
  A written or verbal agreement naming a target habit, daily actions, and
  concrete penalties for lapses — signed with one or two accountability
  partners who enforce the social cost of breaking it.
sources:
  - title: "Atomic Habits: Tiny Changes, Remarkable Results"
    resource: "Atomic Habits (James Clear), ch. 17"
---

The inversion of "make it satisfying" for breaking bad habits is **make it
unsatisfying**: attach immediate, tangible cost to lapses so failure gets
fixed instead of ignored. A **habit contract** states the commitment, the
specific daily behaviors, and penalties — signed by you and one or two
accountability partners.

Worked example (Bryan Harris): weight goal with phased diet plan; daily food
logging and weigh-ins; penalties for missed logging (dress formally at work,
$200 to trainer); escalated fines for missed targets and rival-university hat
for skipped sprints. Signatures matter — skipping the formal sign-off predicts
slippage.

**Mechanism:** people manage behavior to protect reputation. Knowing someone
watches converts private lapse into broken public promise. Lighter forms:
daily "song a day" challenge with a friend; automated public tweet plus PayPal
offer if you fail to wake by 6:10.

**Design constraints:** punishment must exceed the benefit of the bad habit
("cost of procrastination greater than cost of action"); immediacy and
concreteness beat distant vague consequences. Contrast [behavior change
masterplan](behavior-change-masterplan.md) — Fogg treats demotivators and
guilt-based penalties as last-resort and often counterproductive; habit
contracts are a deliberate social-cost lever when structural [friction
shaping](friction-shaping.md) and [cue exposure
reduction](cue-exposure-reduction.md) are insufficient. Pair with [external
commitment declaration](external-commitment-declaration.md) for goals without
formal penalties.

Government seat-belt and smoking laws are large-scale social contracts —
collective enforcement of immediate consequence.
