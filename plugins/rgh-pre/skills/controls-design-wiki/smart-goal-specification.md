title: SMART Goal Specification
description: >
  Specify a goal as Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and
  Time-bound so it's concrete enough to act on, instead of leaving it as a
  vague aspiration that gives no clear starting point.
sources:
  - title: "Self-Care for Autistic People: 100+ Ways to Recharge, De-Stress, and Unmask"
    resource: "Self-Care for Autistic People (Megan Anna Neff), Chapter 6: Professional Self-Care"
---

A vague goal ("be better at my job") gives no actionable entry point —
there's nothing concrete to start doing. Running it through five checks
before treating it as a working goal makes it usable: **Specific**
("learn how to use a new software," not "improve"); **Measurable** (attach
a number, e.g. "complete three online courses"); **Achievable** (realistic
given current capability and resources); **Relevant** (actually tied to
real values or plans, not adopted because it sounds good); **Time-bound**
(a deadline that creates urgency, e.g. "five job applications in a week").
This specification step comes before [graded task assignment](graded-task-assignment.md)
decomposes the goal into small steps, and before deciding whether any of
those steps should be [time-boxed](time-boxed-goal-setting.md) rather than
amount-based.
