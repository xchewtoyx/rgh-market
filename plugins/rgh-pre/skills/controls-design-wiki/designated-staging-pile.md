---
type: concept
title: Designated Staging Pile
description: >
  Give each room an agreed temporary holding spot for items not yet returned
  to permanent storage, instead of expecting immediate put-away every time.
sources:
  - title: "How to Be You: Simple Ways to Love Who You Are"
    resource: "How to Be You (Ellie Middleton), ch. 8"
---

"Everything in its home immediately" assumes executive capacity that is not
always available. Without a sanctioned interim step, clutter spreads or
items vanish into random surfaces. A designated staging pile per room — a
bucket for clean clothes awaiting the wardrobe, hooks for towels, a bathroom
basket for miscellany — collects deferred sorting in one visible place.

Clear staging piles on a fixed weekly cadence so they do not become
permanent dumps. Apply [visible storage for object permanence](visible-storage-for-object-permanence.md)
selectively: some contexts need everything on display; others (e.g. a kitchen
with too many visible options) need closed storage plus staging instead.
