title: Interest Novelty Challenge Urgency Injection
description: >
  Attach at least one of four stimulation sources — interest, novelty,
  challenge, or urgency — to a mundane task so an interest-led brain can
  engage it without relying on importance alone.
sources:
  - title: "Unmasked: The Ultimate Guide to ADHD, Autism and Neurodivergence for Women"

  - title: "How to Be You: Simple Ways to Love Who You Are"

Some brains are not primarily motivated by importance or other people's
urgency. Without at least one of **interest** (genuine enthusiasm),
**novelty** (new or game-like elements), **challenge** (difficulty or
competition), or **urgency** (a tight time-based deadline), a task
under-stimulates and attention wanders — not from laziness but from a
mismatch between wiring and stimulation profile. Wanting to do something
and being able to start it are different problems; the control targets the
stimulation gap, not willpower.

Inject motivators at design time, before the task must happen:
- **Challenge** — podcasts at 1.5–2× speed while doing housework; race a
  song's runtime against a tidying task.
- **Novelty** — work from a new café or change the physical context.
- **Urgency** — artificial deadlines, accountability partners, playlist
  timers ([immediate start reward](immediate-start-reward.md)).
- **Interest** — pair the task with a special-interest element ([preference-
  aligned routine design](preference-aligned-routine-design.md)).

This is a personal-scale [motivational prosthesis](motivational-prosthesis.md)
placed at the [point of performance](point-of-performance-intervention.md).
When stimulation is high enough, focus can exceed typical levels
([hyperfocus bumper rails](hyperfocus-bumper-rails.md) then bound how long
absorption runs). For tasks that cannot be stimulated into engagement,
[tiered to-do structures](tiered-todo-structure.md) and [task
delegation](task-delegation-scaffold.md) are the fallback rather than
repeated shame cycles.
