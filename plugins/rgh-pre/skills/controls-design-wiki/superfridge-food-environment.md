  Redesign the home fridge to stock only ready-to-eat healthy food and
  nothing that conflicts with the eating plan — eat freely from it without
  willpower battles against the rest of the food environment.
sources:
  - title: "Tiny Habits: The Small Changes That Change Everything"
    resource: "Tiny Habits (BJ Fogg), ch. 7"
---

Weight change is driven mainly by eating, not exercise alone. Willpower-based
eating fails when work, travel, and dining environments work against you —
so redesign the one environment you control daily: the fridge.

**Superfridge method:**

- Stock only ready-to-eat healthy food (washed/cut vegetables in glass
  containers, cooked grains, fruit, boiled eggs, plain yogurt, etc.).
- Eat freely from the fridge at any time — no portion policing inside it.
- Never stock anything that conflicts with the plan.
- Refresh weekly (e.g. Sunday prep); actively eat through contents rather
  than waste.

This is [friction shaping](friction-shaping.md) and [proactive environment
priming](proactive-environment-priming.md) applied to nutrition — the
default option is the wanted behavior ([default option
preselection](default-option-preselection.md)). Fogg reports >15% sustained
weight loss without relying on willpower; framed as one practitioner's
technique, not a universal prescription.
