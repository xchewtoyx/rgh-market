---
type: concept
title: Sleep Restriction Protocol
description: >
  Rebuild sleep by fixing wake time, starting with a short sleep window and
  holding it for two weeks, then gradually extending bedtime in 30-minute
  steps until reaching a sustainable nightly duration.
sources:
  - title: "CBT For Dummies, 4th Edition"
    resource: "CBT For Dummies, 4th Edition (Rhena Branch, Rob Willson), ch. 15"
---

**Step 1:** Pick a fixed wake time and set bedtime six hours earlier (e.g.
wake 7 a.m. → bed 1 a.m.). Get up at the target time regardless of sleep
quality.

**Step 2:** Hold that schedule for two weeks. Sleep quality should improve and
onset should speed up because even six hours becomes more restorative when
consolidated.

**Step 3:** Over the next one to one-and-a-half weeks, move bedtime earlier
by 30 minutes every couple of days until reaching 7–8 hours nightly (e.g.
ending at 11 p.m. for a 7 a.m. wake). Stay roughly synced with natural
light cycles. Keep weekends consistent — don't vary wake time by more than
30 minutes or the pattern drifts.

While rebuilding, restrict total sleep quantity: no napping; if a siesta urge
hits at a predictable time, plan to be out of the house or busy then. Pair
with [bed stimulus control](bed-stimulus-control.md) (leave bed if awake more
than ten minutes; bed for sleep and sex only) and [wind-down buffer](wind-down-buffer.md)
for the pre-bed runway.
