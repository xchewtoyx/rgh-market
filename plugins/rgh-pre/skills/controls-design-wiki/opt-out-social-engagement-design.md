---
type: concept
title: Opt-Out Social Engagement Design
description: >
  Design group or social activities with an explicit, judgment-free
  opt-out by default, letting each individual set their own pace of
  relational engagement rather than presuming a uniform comfort level.
sources:
  - title: "The Canary Code: A Guide to Neurodiversity, Dignity, and Intersectional Belonging at Work"
    resource: "The Canary Code (Ludmila N. Praslova, PhD), Chapter 5: Starting Off Right — Onboarding, Training, Accommodations, and Compensation"
---

A mandatory-by-default social event (a surprise party, a team-building
outing) assumes everyone wants the same kind and pace of social contact.
Building an explicit, no-questions-asked opt-out into the design of such
activities — and offering a range of engagement levels (e.g. a 1:1 coffee
as an alternative to a group gathering) — lets the actual variation in
comfort and capacity determine participation, instead of forcing a uniform
default that fits some people and quietly costs others.
