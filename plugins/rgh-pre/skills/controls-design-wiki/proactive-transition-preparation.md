---
type: concept
title: Proactive Transition Preparation
description: >
  Use the lead-up period before a known major transition (a new job, a big
  change) to front-load orientation information, arrange needed
  accommodations, and establish support relationships in advance, rather
  than leaving all of it to be handled at the transition point itself.
sources:
  - title: "The Canary Code: A Guide to Neurodiversity, Dignity, and Intersectional Belonging at Work"
    resource: "The Canary Code (Ludmila N. Praslova, PhD), Chapter 5: Starting Off Right — Onboarding, Training, Accommodations, and Compensation"
---

The period between a transition being confirmed and the transition actually
starting (e.g. between a job offer and the first day) is often treated as
dead time or pure paperwork. Treated instead as active preparation time, it
can absorb work that would otherwise land all at once on day one — exactly
when capacity is already strained by the transition itself: understanding
what's needed, easing procedural burden, providing orientation information
in advance, connecting the person with a support contact, and arranging any
known accommodations ahead of time rather than after the need has already
surfaced under pressure.
