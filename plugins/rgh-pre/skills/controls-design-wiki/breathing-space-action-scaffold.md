title: Breathing-Space Action Scaffold
description: >
  When mood drops, always run a brief grounding pause first, then choose one
  concrete pleasurable, mastery, or mindful action — because depression
  demands specificity, not vague "cheer up."
sources:
  - title: "Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy for Depression, 2nd Edition"
    resource: "Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy for Depression (Segal, Williams, Teasdale), ch. 16"
---

MBCT Session 7 adds an **action step** after the three-minute breathing
space (acknowledge experience → attend to breath → expand to body). The
pause is never the whole intervention — it precedes a deliberate choice
about what to do next.

After the breathing space, ask: "What do I need for myself right now? How
can I best take care of myself?" Pick one of three lanes:
1. **Pleasurable** — hot bath, favorite drink, walk, call a friend, music.
2. **Mastery** — one small completed task (pay a bill, clear a drawer);
   break work into steps and congratulate partial completion.
3. **Mindful** — narrate the current action ("now I am walking down the
   stairs"); attend to feet on floor or breath during ordinary activity.

Depressed mood cannot be overlooked — specificity beats willpower. Waking
is a high-vulnerability window; old catastrophic thoughts ("back to square
one") undermine action unless the body is softened first, then a single
next step chosen. Even a 1% improvement in one difficult moment compounds
forward.

This is steady-state prevention scaffolding, not crisis response — pair
with a [prewritten relapse action plan](prewritten-relapse-action-plan.md)
for when early warning signs appear and in-the-moment judgment is unreliable.
