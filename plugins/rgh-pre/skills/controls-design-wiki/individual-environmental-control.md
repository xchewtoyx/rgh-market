---
type: concept
title: Individual Environmental Control
description: >
  Prefer personal-level control over a shared environmental variable
  (temperature, lighting) via an individual device, rather than one
  centralized setting imposed on everyone, since the optimal level
  genuinely varies by person.
sources:
  - title: "The Canary Code: A Guide to Neurodiversity, Dignity, and Intersectional Belonging at Work"
    resource: "The Canary Code (Ludmila N. Praslova, PhD), Chapter 6: Work Environment — Flexible Spaces; ch. 6 proximity bias and remote work"
  - title: "Knowing Why: Adult-Diagnosed Autistic People on Life and Autism"
    resource: "Knowing Why (Autistic Self Advocacy Network), ch. 5 (Kelly Bron Johnson)"
---

A single centralized setting for a shared environmental variable can only
ever match one preference, and in practice tends to default to whoever has
the most influence over it — leaving everyone else to absorb the mismatch
individually and quietly (extra layers, a hidden personal fan, discomfort
tolerated without complaint). Where a cheap individual-level control exists
(a desk fan or heater, task lighting instead of relying solely on
overheads), providing it directly avoids the negotiation entirely: each
person adjusts their own local conditions instead of everyone contesting a
single shared setting. This is a shared-environment-specific case of the
same substitution logic in [environmental sensory accommodation](environmental-sensory-accommodation.md)
— replace a one-size-fits-all default with a variant matched to the
individual, rather than expecting the individual to adapt to the default.

**Remote or hybrid work** is the strongest form of this control when commute
and shared open-plan variables cannot be individually tuned: the person
chooses lighting, temperature, seating, fidgets, and scent exposure directly.
For many disabled and neurodivergent workers, working from home part or
full time is not a perk but the only viable way to preserve enough energy
for the job itself — a control that removes entire categories of mismatch
rather than negotiating each one locally. Without [outcome-based work
accountability](outcome-based-work-accountability.md) that rewards results
over face time, remote arrangement becomes an **included-ish** trap:
workers produce more but miss promotions, stretch assignments, and
watercooler wages — a progression tax from proximity bias, not from remote
work itself.
