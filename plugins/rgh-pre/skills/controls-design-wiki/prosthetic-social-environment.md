type: concept
title: Prosthetic Social Environment
description: >
  Treat long-term environmental accommodations as permanent prosthetic
  support, not temporary fixes to be withdrawn once behavior improves.
sources:
  - title: "Attention-Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder: A Handbook for Diagnosis and Treatment, 4th Edition"
    resource: "Attention-Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (Russell A. Barkley, Ed.), ch. 16"
---

Some self-regulation deficits are chronic — analogous to diabetes rather
than a skill gap that closes with practice. Symptom relief comes from daily
supports (medication where applicable, environmental and lifestyle changes)
maintained over the long term; periodic breakthroughs may require
reintervention or new control packages.

Environmental accommodations should be understood as a permanent prosthetic
social environment — like hearing aids, wheelchairs, or artificial limbs —
not as correcting faulty learning that later permits treatment withdrawal.
Catalog deficits at each level they appear (instrumental self-direction,
methodical self-reliance, social reciprocity) and build a prosthetic
environment around the person: externalized information, bridged temporal
gaps, [motivational prostheses](motivational-prosthesis.md), and
[point-of-performance](point-of-performance-intervention.md) scaffolds
designed to stay in place.
