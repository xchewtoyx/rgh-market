---
type: concept
title: Backward Milestone Planning
description: >
  Start from the finished deliverable and deadline, then map milestones
  backward to the first action so nothing essential is missed.
sources:
  - title: "How to Be You: Simple Ways to Love Who You Are"
    resource: "How to Be You (Ellie Middleton), ch. 6"
---

Forward planning from "what's next" loses sight of dependencies — marketing
needs the printed product, printing needs two weeks, the manuscript needs to
exist before that. Begin with success criteria: what must be true at the
end? Work backward to intermediate deadlines (launch site live, collateral
ready, print submitted) until the starting task is clear.

Clarifying questions up front ("why this way?", "what does success look
like?") reduce miscommunication before mapping. A visual project board
tracks milestones and check-off motivation. Complements [weekly brain dump
planning](weekly-brain-dump-planning.md) for multi-week efforts and
[concrete implementation plans](concrete-implementation-plan.md) for single
actions.
