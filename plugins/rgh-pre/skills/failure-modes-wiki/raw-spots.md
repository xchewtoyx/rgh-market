## Recognizing it

- **Mechanism**: a present-moment stressor lands on an existing
  vulnerability from past experience, and the resulting reaction is the sum
  of both — not a proportionate response to the present trigger alone.
- **Indicator**: a pattern, across otherwise-unrelated situations, of
  reactions whose intensity is out of proportion to the immediate trigger.
  A single instance is ambiguous; the recurring pattern across situations
  that share an underlying theme (e.g. all involve feeling dismissed, or
  all involve being left waiting) is the tell.
- **What it is commonly confused with**: an overreaction to the immediate
  situation, or a character flaw ("too sensitive"), when the actual
  mechanism is a present stressor compounding an older, unresolved one. The
  present trigger is often genuinely minor on its own — the disproportion
  is the signal that a raw spot is involved, not evidence the reaction is
  invalid.

## Identifying a specific raw spot

Reviewing past strong-reaction situations for a shared underlying "story"
(e.g. an abandonment narrative, a not-good-enough narrative) — rather than
treating each incident as isolated — is how a specific raw spot gets
identified rather than just noticing that disproportionate reactions keep
happening.

## A named, specific case: rejection

When the shared theme is specifically rejection — actual or anticipated —
and comes with a recognizable physical symptom signature and a
characteristic avoidance/procrastination consequence, it is worth
recognizing directly as [rejection sensitive
dysphoria](rejection-sensitive-dysphoria.md) rather than only as a generic
raw spot.
