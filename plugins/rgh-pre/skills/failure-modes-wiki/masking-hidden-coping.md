  back room — until crisis makes secrecy unsustainable.

## Recognizing it

- **Structure**: mask and addiction/coping are like two rooms — one clean for
  guests, one where mess from maintaining the first gets stashed. Hiding a
  coping mechanism parallels wearing a law-abiding professional mask while
  concealing criminal behavior — family may never suspect.
- **Trigger**: [masking](masking.md)-induced anxiety and energy depletion;
  [autistic intellectualized rumination](autistic-intellectualized-rumination.md)
  that coping blots out.
- **Marker**: secret, shameful, high-dopamine behaviors (substance use,
  binge eating, gambling, self-harm) done in private; the coping mechanism
  itself becomes a reason to keep autism/neurodivergence secret — easier to
  believe anxiety stems from "some shameful practice" than from nervous
  system difference.
- **Progression if unaddressed**: secrecy becomes unsustainable — DUI,
  overdose, suicide watch, purging discovered at a wedding, losing savings
  on bets. None believed they had a problem until an immovable obstacle.
  Stopping the coping can surface stimming, anxiety, shame, and friendship
  deficits — "everything under the mask came out."

## Statistics context (recognition, not prescriptive)

Higher rates of substance abuse, disordered eating, and self-harm among
autistics — patterns that develop suddenly and subtly, sometimes hidden by
another part of the self "in your best interests."

## What it is commonly confused with

Primary addiction without masking context — the distinguishing tell is the
paired concealment architecture and unmasking surfacing what was underneath.

## Relationship to other patterns

Feeds [escalating escapism](escalating-escapism.md) and [cure becomes
poison](cure-becomes-poison.md); schismogenesis (defining self in opposition
to drinkers while binge eating "snuck in the back door") is a specific
variant of identity-bound avoidance.
