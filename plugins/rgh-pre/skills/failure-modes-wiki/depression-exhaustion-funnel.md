## Recognizing it

- **Trigger**: mood decline plus beliefs that leisure is "unimportant" while
  all demands of family, friends, colleagues, and boss must still be met —
  regardless of reasonableness.
- **Marker**: the **exhaustion funnel** — circles of life grow smaller as
  optional-but-nourishing activities (seeing friends, fun outings) are
  dropped, leaving only work and stressors. The narrower the funnel, the
  more likely burnout and exhaustion. Those most likely to continue downward
  are often the most conscientious workers whose self-confidence is closely
  tied to work performance — not the workers commonly labeled "lazy."
- **Rest paradox**: considering going out, the thought arises "What's the
  point? Nothing will make a difference, so I'll rest" — but resting on the
  couch drifts into well-worn self-critical grooves, so rest makes the person
  feel worse — "the perfect setup for the persistence and deepening of
  depression."

## Progression if unaddressed

Feeds the [depression vicious cycle](depression-vicious-cycle.md) withdrawal
and neglect loops — depriving the person of one of the simplest ways to
reverse decline, while [rumination spiral](rumination-spiral.md) fills idle
time.

## What it is commonly confused with

Healthy prioritization during a busy period — the distinguishing tell is
dropping activities that actually gave pleasure while expanding the
"essential" obligation list, plus rest that worsens rather than restores
mood.
