## Recognizing it

- **Trigger**: mandatory social settings with no alternative — youth groups,
  church programs, structured bonding activities — where inclusion is
  enforced rather than optional, colliding with actual social capacity.
- **Marker**: elaborate escape routines to achieve physical absence from
  others: hiding in bathroom stalls, lying in dark empty rooms to slow
  breathing, or physically concealing oneself (e.g. above ceiling tiles)
  to let "the absolute lack of others radiate comfort." From hiding places,
  people below may sound distant — echoes, fading ghosts.
- **Surface social attempt**: periphery standing, scripted conversational
  lines, counted eye contact, letting conversations die — followed by
  internal state of broken, lonely, anxious when escape is not available.
- **What it is commonly confused with**: antisocial preference, rudeness, or
  refusal to participate — when the actual driver is overload and fear
  from repeated unexplained social failure, not disinterest in connection.

## Distinguishing marker from chosen withdrawal

When peer groups organically exclude and the person experiences solitude as
"a safe, non-threatening alternative," withdrawal can be relief rather than
this pattern. Forced socialization hide response appears when an environment
actively works to include and bond without offering escape — the stated
intent (belonging) collides with capacity, and hiding is the only available
[shutdown](shutdown.md)-direction coping.

## Progression if unaddressed

Repeated unexplained social failures erode self-confidence, producing quiet
withdrawal and intense fear of other people — even when the person still
wants friendship and keeps seeking it. Social struggle becomes its own
breakdown pattern layered on top of communication differences.

## Origin context

Often develops from childhood: lacking standard communication skills and
body-language reading, wanting friendship but failing without understanding
why — each failure compounding fear until avoidance or hiding is the only
predictable relief.
