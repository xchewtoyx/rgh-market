  undermining CBT-style cognitive questioning that adds more thinking.

## Recognizing it

- **Trigger**: painful memories or current distress — framed as spending
  energy recalling and reprocessing while trying to find a solution.
- **Mechanism**: autistics "chew on" feelings — moving a feeling from the
  animal part of the brain to the thinking part, but overthinking until
  anxiety re-alerts the animal part, so the solving attempt recycles the
  feeling through second, third, fourth perspectives. Combines with
  [alexithymia](alexithymia.md) difficulty labeling feelings and
  [rumination spiral](rumination-spiral.md) repetition without resolution.
- **Progression if unaddressed**: drives development of unhealthy coping to
  blot out the thinking/feeling cycle — shameful, thrilling, high-stakes,
  secret behaviors that trigger dopamine and veil a hostile inner life
  ([masking-hidden coping](masking-hidden-coping.md), [escalating
  escapism](escalating-escapism.md)).

## What it is commonly confused with

Productive CBT-style cognitive restructuring — autistics already tend to
intellectualize; adding more questioning can worsen rather than interrupt
the loop. Mindfulness practice difficulty is often this rumination getting
in the way.

## Distinguishing marker from meltdown overflow

[Meltdown](meltdown.md) overflow is emotional capacity exceeded into
bodily panic; intellectualized rumination is sustained neocortex routing
that keeps re-triggering emotion without discharge — both can coexist but
the rumination pattern is cognitive cycling, not acute eruption.
