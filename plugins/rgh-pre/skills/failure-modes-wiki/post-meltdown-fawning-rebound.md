## Recognizing it

- **Trigger**: aftermath of [meltdown](meltdown.md) — a wash of relief and
  often a clear head, but also possible absolute exhaustion and need for
  prolonged cocooning.
- **Marker**: sudden post-meltdown clarity that can look alarming to
  witnesses and tip into a fawning rebound — suppressing negative emotions
  and over-committing to please others again. The person may appear
  recovered and cooperative when they are still depleted.
- **Progression if unaddressed**: returns the person to [masking](masking.md)
  and demand accumulation, feeding the next burnout or meltdown cycle.
  Public meltdowns that draw shame can compound this — confirmation of worst
  fears about one's place in the world feeds back into
  [autistic burnout](autistic-burnout.md).

## What it is commonly confused with

Genuine recovery or insight — the distinguishing tell is the *rebound into
people-pleasing and suppression* rather than sustainable rest and load
reduction. Not a good moment to make major decisions even when the person
feels lucid.
