## Recognizing the misdiagnosis pattern

Mental health treatment that addresses symptoms ("sticking a plaster")
without investigating whether undiagnosed neurodivergence is the underlying
cause can fail repeatedly — CBT, mindfulness, DBT variants, EMDR, and
hypnotherapy may produce no lasting benefit when the actual driver is
overload or dysregulated attention rather than primary anxiety disorder.

Two common specific misreadings:

- **Tearful overwhelm read as panic attack** → may actually be autistic
  [meltdown](meltdown.md) or [shutdown](shutdown.md) from sensory or
  cognitive overload (too many things at once, too much sensory input) —
  not autonomic panic from perceived threat in the anxiety-disorder sense.
- **Constant circling thoughts read as anxious catastrophizing** → may
  actually be ADHD-driven internal hyperactivity — high-volume, not
  necessarily negative, never-ending thoughts as a classic ADHD trait, not
  worry per se.

## Contributing factors to the miss

Sociable presentation, conventional appearance, and gender can make
neurodivergence "not even a consideration" during mental health assessment —
so anxiety and depression diagnoses accumulate while meltdown, shutdown, and
ADHD hyperactivity patterns go unrecognized.

## Distinguishing marker

When standard anxiety treatment fails repeatedly, or when overwhelm
episodes track overload (sensory, cognitive, demand) rather than feared
future outcomes, consider whether the presentation is neurodivergent
breakdown mislabeled as mood disorder — not primary GAD or panic disorder
requiring more of the same intervention.

## Relationship to other misdiagnosis patterns

This pattern overlaps with [autistic presentation versus borderline
personality disorder](autistic-presentation-vs-borderline.md) and with
[alexithymia](alexithymia.md) obscuring distress signals during assessment.
