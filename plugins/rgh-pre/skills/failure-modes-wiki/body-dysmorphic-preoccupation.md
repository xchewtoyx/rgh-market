## Recognizing it

- **Preoccupation**: extreme focus on one or more physical features believed
  abnormal, defective, or ugly — perception counts even when others
  disagree.
- **Compulsions**: masking areas (clothing, make-up), continual mirror-
  checking to ensure the defect is concealed or hasn't worsened.
- **Mirror habits**: overuse to criticize (hom in on dissatisfaction) or
  avoidance to prevent confronting imperfection — both reinforce negative
  body image.
- **Progression if unaddressed**: prevents socializing, working, intimate
  relationships; cosmetic surgery often disappoints or shifts preoccupation
  to another feature. Muscle dysmorphia subpattern: belief body is too small
  or insufficiently muscular, driving excessive lifting, dieting, supplements,
  or steroids.

## Maintenance mechanism

Same shape as [obsessive-compulsive loop](obsessive-compulsive-loop.md) and
[problem-maintaining solution](problem-maintaining-solution.md) — mirror
rituals and concealment give short-term relief while maintaining the
preoccupation. ERP-style facing fears while stopping rituals is the
recognition frame.

## What it is commonly confused with

Healthy appearance concern or vanity — clinical threshold includes distress
despite reassurance, one hour or more per day on worries/checking, and
significant life interference.

## Related patterns

Unhealthy body image more broadly can feed [depression vicious
cycle](depression-vicious-cycle.md) social withdrawal; eating disorders
share ritual and control themes but center on weight/fear of fatness rather
than a single perceived defect.
