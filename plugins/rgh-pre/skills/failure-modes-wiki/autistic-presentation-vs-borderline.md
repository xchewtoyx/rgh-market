## Recognizing the misdiagnosis pattern

The same surface behavior can satisfy BPD criteria when interpreted through
a mood-disorder lens, but maps to autistic/neurodivergent mechanisms when
the interpretive frame shifts. Common reframings:

- **Emotional instability** (BPD: rage, sorrow, shame, panic, terror,
  emptiness) → rage as [meltdown](meltdown.md); sorrow as delayed
  emotional processing; shame from repeated social "getting it wrong";
  panic from unmet sensory/control/structure needs; terror from fear of past
  misunderstandings repeating; emptiness from chronic exclusion.
- **Disturbed thinking** (BPD: feeling like a terrible person / not
  existing) → lifetime of being called naughty, a bitch, or manipulative for
  traits that were natural communication or sensory differences.
- **Brief strange experiences** (BPD: hearing voices) → literal thinkers
  answering "yes" about their constant internal monologue, not auditory
  hallucination.
- **Prolonged abnormal experiences** (BPD: hallucinations) → heightened
  sensory processing (hearing adjacent conversations, electricity when
  lights switch) misread as hallucination rather than acute perception.
- **Unstable relationships** (BPD: frantic contact fearing abandonment) →
  hyperfixation on a person, or not intuiting that non-reply may mean
  someone doesn't want to talk, or mimicking prior love-bombing behavior.

## Why the confusion compounds

BPD and c-PTSD are themselves often trauma-caused conditions — and a
lifetime spent undiagnosed, gaslit, and misunderstood is its own form of
ongoing trauma. The surface presentation can therefore reflect both
neurodivergent wiring and trauma layered on top, making single-label
diagnosis especially unreliable.

## Distinguishing marker

Ask whether the "instability" tracks sensory overload, social confusion,
exclusion history, and literal/sensory-perception differences — versus
primarily interpersonal fear-of-abandonment dynamics without those
autistic-specific drivers. Many people are convinced they have BPD before
discovering autism or ADHD explains the same surface traits.
