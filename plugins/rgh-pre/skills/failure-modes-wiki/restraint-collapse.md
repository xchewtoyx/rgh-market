## Recognizing it

- **Trigger**: sustained [masking](masking.md) and self-editing across a
  workday or longer stretch — it takes more cognitive energy to restrain
  thoughts and feelings than to express them, and constantly editing for
  surroundings erodes the habit of connecting with oneself at all. One
  framing: "a tide receding before a tsunami."
- **Marker**: typically at the end of the workday, sometimes extending for
  days — no energy for anything beyond phone-scrolling (sometimes sitting in
  the driveway before going inside), passive TV, minimal dopamine-seeking,
  little interaction with others; heating up food or delivery instead of
  cooking; staying up too late in a kind of "revenge" for a stolen day
  without doing anything meaningful with the reclaimed time. The person may
  be incredulous that coworkers sustain hobbies, coach kids' teams, or
  socialize after work.
- **Progression if unaddressed**: often precedes full
  [autistic burnout](autistic-burnout.md) — sensory issues intensify next
  (light, noise, touch, crowds, smells become overwhelming), irritability
  increases with a short fuse before losing temper or crying.

## What it is commonly confused with

Ordinary tiredness after a long day, or simple introversion — the
distinguishing tell is the *restraint* frame: the collapse follows sustained
inhibition of authentic expression, not just physical exertion, and the
passive disengagement can persist across multiple days while masking
demands continue.
