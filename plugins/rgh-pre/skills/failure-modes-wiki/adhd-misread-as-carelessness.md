## Recognizing the misread

- **Surface presentation**: spelling errors, wrong appointment times,
  missed details, forgotten instructions — read as carelessness or low
  attention to detail.
- **Actual mechanism**: slower and more variable reaction times and errors
  of omission indicating poor attentional ability on unstimulating tasks —
  not lack of care. ADHDers typically care greatly; mistakes often come
  from a mind processing at high speed, outpacing the ability to catch
  errors, or from tasks that under-stimulate an interest-led brain.
- **Progression if unaddressed**: damaged self-esteem; others read the
  person as untrustworthy or incompetent; some adopt a "scatty or ditzy"
  persona pre-emptively as self-protection.

## Distinguishing marker from genuine carelessness

Interest and stimulation profile predict performance: mundane tasks
(admin, housework, long boring manuals) produce wandering focus and
omission errors; tasks with interest, novelty, challenge, or urgency can
produce [hyperfocus](hyperfocus.md) exceeding neurotypical levels. The
pattern is dysregulated attention, not variable conscientiousness.

## What it is commonly confused with

Lazy, disorganized, or unreliable — when the actual driver is executive
function delay (~30% developmental lag, adults reaching only ~75–80% of
peer executive-function capacity) combined with an interest-led motivational
system that cannot voluntarily attach focus to unstimulating work.
